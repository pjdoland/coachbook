1. **Add a client-side filter/search to the models index.** With 94+ entries on `src/pages/models/index.astro` rendered as a flat alphabetical card grid, a returning user who knows "Willis" or "9927" still has to eyeball or Cmd-F a 94-card list, and a first-time visitor cannot narrow by era. Add a small input above `<ul class="card-grid">` that filters cards by name, style number, and era token, plus a row of era pills (E1–E5) that toggle visibility. The data is already in each card's `meta` string, so this is a ~30-line vanilla JS addition. Rationale: `Task-flow`.

2. **Surface the wizard CTA above the fold on the home page.** The primary user task is "is this real and from when," but `src/pages/index.astro` opens with a paragraph lede about "primary-source reference" and only then shows the "Authenticate a bag" card row at equal weight to "Browse." Promote the wizard to a single, visually distinct primary CTA (use `.cta.primary` styling already defined in `authentication/index.astro`) immediately under the lede, before the card grid. Demote the decision tree and "All topics" cards to a smaller secondary row. Rationale: `Task-flow`.

3. **Persist the global nav with the wizard as the primary action.** The header in `src/layouts/BaseLayout.astro` lists "Models / Authenticate / History" — three peers — but "Authenticate" is the high-intent task while the others are browsing. Either rename to "Authenticate a bag" (keeping it a button-styled link to `/wizard/`) or split into a primary action button plus the two browse links. This makes the primary task one click from any page, including deep model entries. Rationale: `Navigation`.

4. **Add visible breadcrumbs on every detail page.** `src/pages/models/[slug].astro`, `authentication/[slug].astro`, and `catalogs/[slug].astro` jump directly into `<h1>` with no upward navigation; users entering from search engines have only the small site-header brand link to escape. Insert a `<nav class="breadcrumbs">` above the `<h1>` rendering "coachbook / Models / Willis" with each segment linked. Rationale: `Navigation`.

5. **Group the models index by era, not just alphabetically.** The mental model collectors carry is era-driven (Cashin / NYC / late-NYC / Krakoff), not alphabetical. In `src/pages/models/index.astro`, render the `card-grid` inside era-keyed sections (`<section><h2>E2 Cashin (1962–1974)</h2>...`) using the `entry.data.era` field already present in frontmatter. Keep an A–Z toggle for users who arrive knowing the model name. Rationale: `IA`.

6. **Move the wizard's "your answers so far" trail above the question, not below.** In `src/pages/wizard/index.astro` the `.wiz-trail` renders after `.wiz-controls`, so on mobile the user has to scroll past the question and answer buttons to see what they have already chosen. Render the trail (collapsed by default after step 2) between the progress bar and the step body. Rationale: `Cognitive-load`.

7. **Add an "Unknown / not sure" answer to every wizard step.** Several steps in `STEPS` (e.g., `wordmark`, `country`, `serial`) currently force a definite pick; a real user holding a worn 1970s creed often cannot read the wording. Add an "I cannot tell" option that records a neutral era hint (no flag, no era assertion) and routes to the next step. Without it, the wizard pushes users toward false-precision answers that distort the verdict. Rationale: `Affordance`.

8. **Show the wizard verdict's flag and era reasoning, not just the label.** In the `renderVerdict` function the user sees a tag and bullets, but not which of their answers contributed flags or era hints. Render a compact "Why this verdict" panel that lists each step's recorded `era` and `flags` (e.g., "Step 5 hardware: +2 flags — magnet sticks"). This converts a black-box result into a teaching artifact, which matches the archive's editorial stance. Rationale: `Affordance`.

9. **Add a "Jump to era" sticky strip to the history page.** `src/pages/history.astro` builds a beautiful era-card grid at the top, but once a user scrolls into the 99-event timeline they lose access to it; the only escape is the `↑ Back to top` link. Add a `position: sticky; top: 0` thin strip with five era links (E1–E5) that appears once the user scrolls past the era-card grid. Rationale: `Navigation`.

10. **Add per-era anchors and counts inside the timeline body, not just in the TOC.** The era cards link to `era.events[0]?.slug`, which jumps to the first event of that era — but there is no visual `<h2>` boundary in the rendered timeline marking the era transition. Insert era-section headings into `history/timeline.md` (or inject them in `history.astro` based on year inference) so the body itself is scannable as five chapters with their own H2s, matching the TOC's mental model. Rationale: `IA`.

11. **Replace the bare source-ID list on detail pages with linked, human-readable citations.** On `models/[slug].astro` and `authentication/[slug].astro`, the Sources section currently renders `<li><code>{id}</code></li>` — opaque tokens like `coach-10k-fiscal-2003` with no link or expansion. Resolve each ID against `bibliography.md` / `sources/registry.md` at build time and render `<li><a href="...">Coach 10-K, fiscal 2003 (SEC EDGAR)</a></li>`. The current treatment hides the archive's strongest editorial asset. Rationale: `Affordance`.

12. **Add a "Related models" block to model entries.** `models/willis.md` already declares a `related: [winnie, willis-city-bag, court, station, murphy]` array in frontmatter, but `src/pages/models/[slug].astro` never renders it. After the article body, render a card row of related entries with their labels and eras. This converts dead-end pages into a browse network and matches a documented secondary task ("research a specific model"). Rationale: `Discoverability`.

13. **Add a model-page cross-link from authentication topics, and vice versa.** A user reading `authentication/serial-numbers/` cannot jump to a sample bag; a user on `models/willis/` does not see the wizard. Add a small "Authenticate a Willis" CTA in the model-page DL near `style_numbers` linking to `/wizard/?style=9927` (or just `/wizard/`), and on each authentication topic page list the 3–5 most relevant model entries. Rationale: `Task-flow`.

14. **Make the confidence chip legend a reusable, dismissable footer/sidebar — not a top-level home section.** The "Confidence" section in `src/pages/index.astro` consumes prime real estate explaining a vocabulary that only matters once a user has clicked into a detail page. Move it to a small expandable element in the site footer (or a floating glossary on detail pages) and reclaim that home-page space for the second wizard CTA or recently updated entries. Rationale: `Hierarchy`.

15. **Demote the "Reference" card grid (PLAN/STYLE/Bibliography/Errata) from the home page.** These six links in `src/pages/index.astro` point at GitHub repo Markdown files — they are contributor docs, not visitor docs, and they pull a casual collector away from the site entirely. Move them under a single "About this archive" link in the footer or a `/about/` page, and use the freed home-page space to feature 4–6 representative model entries with thumbnails. Rationale: `Hierarchy`.

16. **Show images on model and catalog cards.** Every card grid in the site is currently text-only on a paper-toned background, which reads as a directory rather than a visual archive — a problem for a domain whose identification task is heavily visual. Add an optional `hero_image` field to the models / catalogs content schemas in `src/content/config.ts`, render a small thumbnail on each card, and fall back gracefully when absent. Even a single representative silhouette per era would dramatically improve scannability. Rationale: `Affordance`.

17. **Disambiguate the two authentication entry points on the landing page.** `src/pages/authentication/index.astro` shows "Start the wizard" and "Printable decision tree" as a CTA row, but the home page exposes a third path ("All topics") at equal weight in the same card row. New visitors face three near-synonymous entry points. On the authentication landing page, rewrite the lede to say "If you have a bag in hand, start the wizard. If you want a printable reference, use the decision tree. To browse by topic, see below," and on the home page collapse to two cards (Wizard, Decision tree) with topics behind the wizard's "More" link. Rationale: `Cognitive-load`.

18. **Add a "What is this site?" 1-line explainer next to the brand mark in the header.** First-time visitors landing on a deep page (`/models/willis/`) see only "coachbook" in the header — a coined word with no context. Add a tagline span, e.g., `coachbook — vintage Coach reference, 1941–2002`, in the `header.site-header` brand area, hidden under 540px. Rationale: `Discoverability`.

19. **Order catalogs newest-first or grouped by decade with a per-decade heading.** `src/pages/catalogs/index.astro` currently sorts ascending by year, so the index opens with 1985-fall and the user must scroll to find the more numerous late-1990s catalogs. Either reverse the sort or group under decade headings (`<h2>1990s</h2>`, `<h2>1980s</h2>`) with newest decade first; this matches how collectors search ("I have a mid-90s bag"). Rationale: `IA`.

20. **Add a permanent "Report a correction" link in the footer.** The archive's editorial stance is citation-disciplined and the repo includes `errata.md`, but a non-technical visitor who spots a wrong attribution has no obvious channel — the GitHub repo links sit on the home page only. Add a footer line in `BaseLayout.astro` linking to a GitHub issue template (or a simple mailto), so the correction loop is one click from every page including model entries where errors are most likely to be spotted. Rationale: `Affordance`.
