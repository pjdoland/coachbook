The 1998 Fall catalog window is the first full Krakoff-era catalog year
with the Ergo line in production. Reed Krakoff joined Coach in December
1996 as Senior Vice President and Executive Creative Director per the
fiscal-2003 10-K primary [coach-10k-fiscal-2003]; the Ergo Hobo (style
9020/9033) is dated 1998 in collector references, with most Ergo bags
launching in 1997. Fall 1998 is therefore the catalog window that
should document the Ergo silhouette break from the classic-NYC canon.

## What to look for when a scan is retrieved

- Cover and inside-cover models — likely an Ergo hero shot; Krakoff's
  visual repositioning was deliberate, so the cover styling itself
  is a data point.
- Ergo collection — every named Ergo model and style number,
  dimensions, leather/material spec (Ergo introduced sewn-on straps
  and rounded silhouettes that visually break from the classic
  glove-tanned NYC bags), and color. The 9020 and 9033 Ergo Hobo
  variants are the documented anchors.
- Fall 1998 color palette — record every named color; Krakoff-era
  palette diverges from the 1990s bright-color period.
- Creed format examples on shown bags — era anchor for authentication
  entries; 1998 sits inside the post-1994 serial-format era and is
  subject to the single-digit-year ambiguity (`B8D-XXXX` resolves to
  Feb 1998 *or* Feb 2008).
- "Made in" stamping — Costa Rica and other offshoring is in progress
  through this window; per-bag country stamps in catalog photography
  are timeline data points.
- Hardware and construction details — Ergo introduced contrast
  fittings and softer shapes; document every visible variant.

## Models likely documented

- Ergo collection (Hobo 9020/9033 and other Ergo variants pending
  scan).
- Continuing Soho, Sonoma, Manhattan, Sheridan, Camden collections.
- Continuing classic-NYC canon: Court, Willis, Madison, Station,
  Mailbox, Penny, Bucket — likely a smaller share of pages than in
  pre-Krakoff catalogs.
- Licensed product lines: Movado watches (1997 license per
  FundingUniverse [fundinguniverse-coach]) and Motorola leather phone
  cases (fiscal 1998 per FundingUniverse) may appear in catalog copy
  even if not pictured.
