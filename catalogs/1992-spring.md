The 1992 Spring catalog window is the introduction point for the Sheridan
and Camden collections per FundingUniverse [fundinguniverse-coach]. By
early 1992 Coach reported a worldwide store count of 53, and Sheridan and
Camden are the two named collections introduced in this season's
merchandising push.

## What to look for when a scan is retrieved

- Cover and inside-cover models — likely a Sheridan or Camden hero shot.
- Spring 1992 color palette — record every named color and its hex
  approximation; this is the last season before the Manhattan-collection
  brights of Spring 1993 [fundinguniverse-coach].
- Sheridan collection introductions — confirm whether the Sheridan
  Stewart Bag appears here or first appears in 1993, which would resolve
  the disputed Sheridan launch date (1992 per FundingUniverse vs 1993
  per the Coach.com brand page) [coach-vintage-sheridan-stewart].
- Camden collection — every named Camden model and its style number.
- Creed format examples on shown bags — era anchor for authentication
  entries; the 1992 creed wording is the late-NYC/Carlstadt
  `COACH LEATHERWARE / EST. 1941 / NEW YORK CITY` standard, but
  catalog photography may show the patch clearly enough to confirm.
- Style-number formats visible on hangtags or inside flaps.

## Where to look

- PurseForum vintage Coach catalogs thread — member-uploaded scans
  [purseforum-vintage-catalogs]. Browser-only access.
- ilexberry Pinterest board — ~300 scanned catalog pages, search by
  year [pinterest-ilexberry-catalog-pages].
- Coach official "From the Archives" Pinterest — 88 brand-curated pins,
  search captions for 1992 [coach-pinterest-archives].

## Models likely documented

- Sheridan collection (Stewart Bag candidate; full collection list
  pending scan) [fundinguniverse-coach, coach-vintage-sheridan-stewart].
- Camden collection [fundinguniverse-coach].
- Continuing classic-NYC canon: Court, Willis, Stewardess, Madison
  variants, Station, Mailbox, Penny, Bucket — likely the bulk of pages
  with Sheridan and Camden as the new-season feature.
