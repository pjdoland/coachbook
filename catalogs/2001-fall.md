The 2001 Fall catalog window is the **vintage-era cutoff** for this
archive. The Signature C collection (Double C logo monogram) launches
in 2001 per FundingUniverse [fundinguniverse-coach] and marks the
visual brand pivot away from solid-leather identity toward logo
monogram fabric. **April 2001** Sara Lee ended its ownership of Coach
with a distribution of its remaining shares via an exchange offer
per the fiscal-2003 10-K primary [coach-10k-fiscal-2003] — Coach Inc.
becomes fully independent. **November 2001** Coach launches its first
jewelry collection (joint venture with Carolee Designs, Inc.) per
FundingUniverse [fundinguniverse-coach]. From Signature C onward is
post-vintage scope; this is the last catalog the archive aims to
document in vintage detail.

## What to look for when a scan is retrieved

- Cover and inside-cover models — likely a Signature C hero shot;
  the launch of logo-monogram fabric is the brand-identity event of
  this catalog and the cover is the most visible signal of the
  pivot [fundinguniverse-coach].
- Signature C collection — every named Signature C model, style
  number, dimensions, fabric/material spec (the launch silhouette
  uses logo-monogram fabric paired with leather trim), and color;
  this is the launch catalog [fundinguniverse-coach]. Mark these
  as the **end-of-vintage marker** — the archive's scope ends with
  the 2001 Fall catalog, and Signature C variants belong in any
  post-vintage successor archive rather than this one.
- Continuing Bleecker, Ergo collections — pre-Signature C Krakoff-era
  silhouettes still in production.
- Fall 2001 color palette — record every named color, distinguishing
  Signature C palette from continuing solid-leather palette.
- First jewelry collection — November 2001 per FundingUniverse
  [fundinguniverse-coach]; if Fall 2001 was distributed early enough,
  jewelry references may not appear, but holiday-window catalog
  copy may.
- April 2001 corporate independence — any "Coach is now an
  independent public company" or similar copy is a corporate-context
  data point reflecting the April 2001 Sara Lee final divestiture
  [coach-10k-fiscal-2003].
- Creed format examples on shown bags — era anchor for authentication
  entries; 2001 sits inside the post-1994 serial-format era and is
  subject to single-digit-year ambiguity (`B1D-XXXX` resolves to
  Feb 2001 *or* Feb 2011).
- "Made in" stamping — country-of-origin labels in catalog
  photography are data points for the offshoring timeline. Lares,
  Puerto Rico manufacturing was still operating (closed April 2002
  per [coach-10k-fiscal-2003]).
- Post-IPO marketing voice — Coach IPO'd October 2000
  [coach-10k-fiscal-2003]; Fall 2001 is the second post-IPO catalog
  and the first after Sara Lee's full exit. Any change in voice
  toward fully independent-public-company positioning is a data
  point.
