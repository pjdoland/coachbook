The 1993 Spring catalog window introduces the Manhattan collection,
described as Coach's first major use of bright colors per FundingUniverse
[fundinguniverse-coach]. This season is also the brand-published
introduction date for the Sheridan Stewart Bag per the Coach.com vintage
re-release page [coach-vintage-sheridan-stewart], placing Spring 1993 as
a key disambiguation point for the Sheridan launch dispute.

## What to look for when a scan is retrieved

- Cover and inside-cover models — likely a Manhattan-collection hero
  shot showcasing the new bright palette.
- Spring 1993 color palette — record every named color; this is the
  first catalog where bright colors are expected to dominate
  [fundinguniverse-coach]. Compare against the Spring 1992 palette to
  document the shift.
- Manhattan collection — every named Manhattan model and its style
  number; this is the central new-season feature.
- Sheridan Stewart Bag presence — if the Stewart Bag appears for the
  first time in this catalog, that corroborates the Coach.com 1993
  introduction date and resolves the 1992-vs-1993 Sheridan dispute
  in favor of 1993 [coach-vintage-sheridan-stewart].
- Creed format examples on shown bags — era anchor for authentication
  entries.
