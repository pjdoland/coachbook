The 1999 Fall catalog window sits inside a major operational year for
Coach. **July 1999** Coach consolidated worldwide warehousing,
distribution, and repair into a 560,000 sq ft facility in Jacksonville,
Florida per the fiscal-2003 10-K primary [coach-10k-fiscal-2003].
**September 1999** Reed Krakoff was promoted to President, Executive
Creative Director — his first catalog season under full creative
direction per the same primary [coach-10k-fiscal-2003]. **October
1999** coach.com launches per FundingUniverse [fundinguniverse-coach],
the brand's first online presence. Fall 1999 is therefore the catalog
that should document the visual transition into Krakoff's full
creative authority and the first catalog likely to reference an
e-commerce URL.

## What to look for when a scan is retrieved

- Cover and inside-cover models — Krakoff's first season as President
  and Executive Creative Director; the cover styling itself is a data
  point on the brand's repositioning [coach-10k-fiscal-2003].
- Fall 1999 color palette — record every named color; this palette
  is the first under Krakoff's full creative direction.
- Continuing Ergo collection — Ergo's silhouette break from the
  classic-NYC canon should now be a larger share of pages than it
  was in Fall 1998.
- coach.com references — any catalog copy mentioning the new
  e-commerce site (launched October 1999 per FundingUniverse
  [fundinguniverse-coach]) is a corporate-context data point. Fall
  1999 is the first catalog season that can credibly print the URL.
- Jacksonville return-address or repair-instruction copy — the
  fiscal-2003 10-K's July 1999 consolidation primary
  [coach-10k-fiscal-2003] is the date Coach Repair functions moved
  to Jacksonville; any change in printed return address or repair
  instructions in this catalog vs prior seasons is the on-paper
  evidence of that consolidation.
- Creed format examples on shown bags — era anchor for authentication
  entries; 1999 sits inside the post-1994 serial-format era and is
  subject to single-digit-year ambiguity (`B9D-XXXX` resolves to
  Feb 1999 *or* Feb 2009).
- "Made in" stamping — country-of-origin labels in catalog
  photography are data points for the offshoring timeline. Lares,
  Puerto Rico manufacturing was still operating (closed April 2002
  per [coach-10k-fiscal-2003]).

## Models likely documented

- Continuing Ergo collection (1997–1998 Krakoff-era introductions);
  likely the Fall 1999 cover and feature focus.
- Continuing Soho, Sonoma, Manhattan, Sheridan, Camden collections.
- Continuing classic-NYC canon: Court, Willis, Madison, Station,
  Mailbox, Penny, Bucket — likely a smaller share of pages than in
  pre-Krakoff catalogs.
- Licensed product lines: Movado watches (1997 license per
  FundingUniverse [fundinguniverse-coach]), Motorola leather phone
  cases (fiscal 1998 per FundingUniverse), and Baker Furniture (1999
  per FundingUniverse) may appear in catalog copy or sidebar pages.
