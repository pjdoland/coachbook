The 2000 Spring catalog window is the pre-IPO catalog. Coach Inc. was
incorporated in Maryland on June 1, 2000 per the fiscal-2003 10-K
primary [coach-10k-fiscal-2003]; the S-1 prospectus was filed June 20,
2000 per WWD's coverage of the filing event; the IPO followed in
October 2000 with 17.0 million shares sold on the NYSE under ticker COH
[coach-10k-fiscal-2003]. Spring 2000 also follows the October 1999
launch of coach.com [fundinguniverse-coach] and is the introduction
window for the Bleecker Satchel per the Coach.com vintage Bleecker
brand page [coach-vintage-bleecker]. This is the last vintage-era
catalog before the Signature C collection launches in 2001 and marks
the visual brand pivot away from solid-leather identity.

## What to look for when a scan is retrieved

- Cover and inside-cover models — likely a Bleecker hero shot; the
  Bleecker Satchel is the named 2000 introduction
  [coach-vintage-bleecker].
- Bleecker collection — every named Bleecker model, style number,
  dimensions, leather/material spec, and color; this is the launch
  catalog [coach-vintage-bleecker].
- coach.com references — any catalog copy referencing the new
  e-commerce site (launched October 1999 per FundingUniverse
  [fundinguniverse-coach]) is a corporate-context data point.
- Spring 2000 color palette — record every named color; this is the
  last palette before Signature C, which introduces logo-monogram
  fabric and shifts the brand identity.
- Creed format examples on shown bags — era anchor for authentication
  entries; 2000 sits inside the post-1994 serial-format era and is
  subject to single-digit-year ambiguity (`B0D-XXXX` resolves to
  Feb 2000 *or* Feb 2010).
- "Made in" stamping — country-of-origin labels in catalog photography
  are data points for the offshoring timeline. Lares, Puerto Rico
  manufacturing was still operating (closed April 2002 per
  [coach-10k-fiscal-2003]).
- Pre-IPO marketing voice — the 2000 catalog copy was written knowing
  an IPO was imminent; any forward-looking language or expanded
  product-category copy (shoes, eyewear, home and pet accessories per
  WWD's reading of the S-1) is a data point on the brand's pre-IPO
  positioning.
