The 1996 Spring catalog window is the last full pre-Krakoff catalog
season. Reed Krakoff joined Coach in **December 1996** as Senior Vice
President and Executive Creative Director per the fiscal-2003 10-K
primary [coach-10k-fiscal-2003]; Spring 1996 ships several months
before that hire and should still reflect the late-NYC design direction
that ran from the Sara Lee acquisition (July 1985) through 1996.
Fiscal 1996 is also the year of the Pacific Rim Waikiki flagship
opening per FundingUniverse [fundinguniverse-coach].

## What to look for when a scan is retrieved

- Cover and inside-cover models — likely a continuing classic-NYC
  hero shot rather than a new-collection feature; the Spring 1996
  catalog precedes Krakoff's December 1996 arrival
  [coach-10k-fiscal-2003].
- Spring 1996 color palette — record every named color; this palette
  is the late-NYC vocabulary, not yet the Krakoff-era shift visible
  from Fall 1997 / 1998 forward.
- Continuing Sonoma collection — Sonoma launched Fall 1995 per
  FundingUniverse [fundinguniverse-coach], so Spring 1996 should be
  Sonoma's second catalog appearance; record models, style numbers,
  and any new variants.
- Continuing Soho, Sheridan, Camden, Manhattan collections —
  pre-Krakoff catalog likely still gives full pages to each.
- Pacific Rim Waikiki flagship — any catalog copy referencing the
  fiscal 1996 Waikiki flagship opening is a corporate-context data
  point [fundinguniverse-coach].
- Creed format examples on shown bags — era anchor for authentication
  entries; 1996 sits inside the post-1994 serial-format era and is
  subject to the single-digit-year ambiguity (`B6D-XXXX` resolves to
  Feb 1996 *or* Feb 2006).
- "Made in" stamping — Costa Rica and other offshoring is in progress
  through this window; per-bag country stamps in catalog photography
  are timeline data points.
