The 1988 Spring catalog window covers Coach's first international
expansion year. Per FundingUniverse, 1988 was the year of the first
international boutiques in England and Japan, the introduction of silk
scarves (later discontinued), and the opening of a new manufacturing
facility near Miami [fundinguniverse-coach]. Spring 1988 is the earlier
of the two seasonal catalogs in this window and would carry the first
visible signals of the international push.

## What to look for when a scan is retrieved

- Cover and inside-cover models — likely classic-NYC canon; the
  pre-collection-launch pattern was still in place (Sheridan and
  Camden do not appear until 1992).
- Silk-scarf debut — every named scarf, color, and price; this is the
  first non-leather product line in the modern era and was later
  discontinued, so the catalog appearance is the primary documented
  evidence of the line [fundinguniverse-coach].
- International references in catalog copy — England and Japan
  boutique addresses or store-locator text would corroborate the
  FundingUniverse 1988 opening date [fundinguniverse-coach].
- Manufacturing references — any "Made in U.S.A." subtext; the new
  Miami-area facility opened in this same window
  [fundinguniverse-coach], so the catalog may carry contemporaneous
  factory-expansion copy.
- Spring 1988 color palette — record every named color; this predates
  the 1993 Manhattan-collection bright-color expansion, so palette
  comparison across the late-1980s catalogs is itself a data point.
- Creed format examples on shown bags — era anchor for authentication
  entries; 1988 sits well inside the pre-1994 serial-format era.

## Models likely documented

- Classic-NYC canon: Court, Willis, Stewardess, Madison variants,
  Station, Mailbox, Penny, Bucket.
- Cashin-vocabulary carryovers (turnlock-closure bags). Cashin's
  contract ended 1974; any bag introduced post-1974 is not a Cashin
  design regardless of hardware vocabulary.
- Silk scarves — first appearance [fundinguniverse-coach].
