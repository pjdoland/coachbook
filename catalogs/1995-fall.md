The 1995 Fall catalog window is the introduction point for the Sonoma
collection per FundingUniverse [fundinguniverse-coach]. 1995 is also
the year Lew Frankfort was named Chairman and CEO (under continuing
Sara Lee ownership) and the year of Coach's first freestanding Japan
store [fundinguniverse-coach].

## What to look for when a scan is retrieved

- Cover and inside-cover models — likely a Sonoma hero shot.
- Sonoma collection — every named Sonoma model, style number,
  dimensions, leather/material spec, and color; this is the launch
  catalog [fundinguniverse-coach].
- Fall 1995 color palette — record every named color.
- Creed format examples on shown bags — era anchor for authentication
  entries; 1995 sits at the early-transition era (E5) boundary as
  defined in `PLAN.md`.
- "Made in" stamps on shown bags — Costa Rica offshoring is in progress
  in this window; any bag with a Costa Rica or other non-U.S. stamp in
  catalog photography is an authentication-timeline data point.
- Any flagship- or Japan-store reference — corporate context for the
  same-year freestanding Japan store opening [fundinguniverse-coach].
