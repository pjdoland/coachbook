The 1994 Fall catalog window covers two major events: the Soho
collection launch — confirmed 1994 per the Coach.com vintage Soho
brand page [coach-vintage-soho] — and the opening of Coach's two-level
New York flagship in late 1994 [fundinguniverse-coach]. This is the
single most important catalog year between the Manhattan-collection
debut (1993) and the Sonoma launch (1995).

## What to look for when a scan is retrieved

- Cover and inside-cover models — likely a Soho hero shot or a flagship-
  store dedication spread.
- Soho collection — every named Soho model, style number, dimensions,
  and color; this is the launch catalog [coach-vintage-soho].
- Fall 1994 color palette — record every named color.
- Two-level NYC flagship reference — any catalog copy or photography
  marking the flagship reopening [fundinguniverse-coach].
- Creed format examples on shown bags — Fall 1994 is at or near the
  serial-format change documented in `research/E-authentication.md`,
  so creed and tag photography is especially valuable as an era anchor
  for authentication entries.
- "Made in U.S.A." stamping on shown bags — useful for the offshoring
  timeline, since Costa Rica production begins in this general window.
