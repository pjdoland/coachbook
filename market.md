# Market and pricing

Vintage Coach has an active resale market across multiple platforms, with sold-listing data the most useful indicator of actual transaction prices. This page indexes where to look and how to read what's there. Specific prices change too quickly for static documentation, so the page emphasizes methodology over snapshot pricing.

## Marketplaces

Listing platforms are Tier 4 sources — useful for dated photo evidence and market data, never authority for facts.

- **eBay** [ebay-vintage-coach] — largest vintage Coach inventory. Sold-listing search is the most useful data source for actual transaction prices, not asking prices. Filter to "Sold Items" and "Completed Items."
- **Etsy** [etsy-vintage-coach] — curated vintage stores; prices generally higher than eBay.
- **Poshmark** — peer-to-peer; pricing variable.
- **Mercari** — peer-to-peer; lower-end pricing.
- **The RealReal** — authenticated luxury resale; vintage Coach selectively carried.
- **Worthpoint** — completed-listing archive; partial preview free, full archive paywalled.
- **Vintage shops** — physical stores in major cities; per-store inventory.

## Pricing methodology

The archive does not publish snapshot prices. To estimate fair market value for any vintage Coach bag at the moment you need a number:

1. Search eBay sold listings (filter "Sold" and "Completed") for the specific style number and era diagnostics.
2. Sample size: at least 10 recent sales for a meaningful range.
3. Adjust for condition (excellent vs good vs fair).
4. Note variant scarcity — rare colorways command a premium.
5. Compare against Etsy and The RealReal asking prices for high/low bookends.

## Premium drivers

General patterns observed in research. All `tentative` — community-sourced, not primary.

- Cashin-era bags (1962–1974) with primary-source attribution, pre-creed.
- Rare colors (e.g., Aqua, persimmon orange, watermelon).
- Original packaging present (dust bag, hangtag, registration card).
- Excellent condition: no edge wear, no leather cracking, hardware patina intact.
- Smaller or harder-to-find variants (e.g., Winnie 9023 mini Willis, baby Courier).

## Discount drivers

- Replacement components installed by Coach Repair / Factory Restoration (turnlocks, dogleash clips, creed patches).
- Color fading or sun damage on leather.
- Lining replacement (affects collector value but not function).
- Edge wear or stitch loosening.

## Notable resale prices

Specific examples from research, all `tentative` snapshots — verify against current sold listings before relying on any number.

- **Courier** (1970s NYC, very rare) — "typically sells for over $200" per the Yourgreatfinds collector guide [yourgreatfinds-collectors-guide]. `tentative`.
- **Cashin-attributed, primary-sourced bags** — premium over post-1974 reissues using the same vocabulary. `tentative`.
- More specific data deferred to per-model authentication blocks.

## What this page is not

This is not a price guide. Prices change. Use the methodology above to estimate at the moment you need a price. The archive's role is to help you authenticate, not to estimate value.
