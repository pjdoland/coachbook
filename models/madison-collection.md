# Madison Collection

## Lead — primary-confirmed Holiday 1993 launch

**The Madison Collection launches in the Holiday 1993 catalog**
[coach-catalogs-1960-1989] per research stream P2 — a wholly new
sub-collection at the back of the Holiday catalog (pages 68-72)
with **limited distribution** (asterisk-symbol denoting "items
available through mail order and select Coach stores"). Madison is
NOT present in Fall Preview 1993 or Holiday Preview 1993; it
appears for the first time in Holiday 1993 specifically.

The Madison Collection should NOT be confused with:
- The **Madison Satchel (Style 9725)** — a Classic-line single
  model from Spring 1986, ~7 years pre-Madison-Collection (per O1)
- The **Madison Avenue flagship store** (a retail location)
- The **Madison Royalton** (a separate later 1990s bag)

The "Madison" name was reused across multiple distinct Coach
products. The Madison Collection (4400-series, textured leather,
exclusive palette) and the Madison Satchel (9725, glove-tanned
cowhide, Classic palette) are entirely separate.

## Brand voice (Holiday 1993 launch — verbatim)

> "THE MADISON COLLECTION COMBINES SLEEK SOPHISTICATION WITH
> CLASSIC STYLE AND GRACE TO PRODUCE HANDBAGS THAT CARRY
> BEAUTIFULLY FROM DAYTIME INTO EVENING. EACH DESIGN IS ELEGANTLY
> CRAFTED FROM A SPECIALLY DEVELOPED TEXTURED LEATHER, AND
> ACCENTED BY SIGNATURE BRASS CLOSURES AND TAILORED JACQUARD LINING.
> EACH STYLE COMES IN A RANGE OF COLORS THAT ARE EXCLUSIVE TO THIS
> COLLECTION."

[coach-catalogs-1960-1989]

The "exclusive to this collection" framing is critical: Madison
launches with a **dedicated palette** distinct from the Classic
10-color palette: **Beechnut, Black, Cherry, Fern, Ivory, Maple,
Navy** (7 colors).

## Material: "specially developed textured leather"

Madison's defining material is "specially developed textured
leather" — distinct from the Classic Collection's glove-tanned
cowhide, the Lightweight/Soho Series' thinned glove-tanned cowhide,
and the Sheridan Collection's pebble-textured leather. Madison
Collection's textured leather is its own material specification,
specifically developed for the line.

Per M3 (1995-1996 deep-read), the Madison Collection continues with
"sleek, pebble-textured leather, signature brass fittings and
tailored jacquard linings" framing in the 1996-1997 Source Book —
the texture descriptor shifts toward "pebble" by 1996 but remains
distinct from glove-tanned cowhide.

## Style numbers

Madison uses the dedicated **4400-series** numbering block
(4400-4409 at launch) [coach-catalogs-1960-1989] — distinct from:
- 9XXX Classic Collection
- 4000-4200 Lightweight/Soho Series
- 5XXX briefcases / Sheridan Sumter Briefcase 5268
- 6XXX Spectator
- 4200s Sheridan Collection

The 4400-series prefix becomes the durable Madison Collection
identifier through 1995-1996 (per M3).

## Era and designer

**Era E4-E5 boundary (Classic NYC late, 1985–1994 / Krakoff
transition starting 1996).** Designer is **in-house Coach team**.
NOT Cashin (her contract ended 1974, 19 years before the Madison
Collection launched). NOT Krakoff (he joined December 1996, 3 years
after Madison launched).

The Holiday 1993 launch sits at the peak of the late-NYC era's
sub-collection-diversification push (Sheridan Fall 1992, Manhattan
Lightweights Spring 1993, Soho Series rename Fall Preview 1993,
Madison Holiday 1993 — four named sub-collections established in
~15 months).

## Limited distribution at launch

"Available through mail order and select Coach stores" — Madison
launches as a **restricted-distribution sub-collection**, not in
all Coach retail stores [coach-catalogs-1960-1989]. This pattern
parallels the Manhattan Series Spring 1993 launch ("AVAILABLE
EXCLUSIVELY THROUGH MAIL ORDER AND SELECT COACH STORES" per M's
main file). Both Manhattan and Madison were positioned as
premium/exclusive sub-collections at launch, with broader retail
distribution following later.

## Authentication

For a vintage Madison Collection bag (Holiday 1993 onward):
- **Style 4400-series** in creed [coach-catalogs-1960-1989]
- **Pre-1994 era**: LEATHERWARE creed wordmark expected (Holiday
  1993 launch is just before the 1994 creed-format transition)
- **"Specially developed textured leather"** — softer/different
  texture than glove-tanned cowhide; not pebble-grain Sheridan
  texture
- **Solid brass closures** with **jacquard lining** — both
  brand-published spec characteristics
- **Madison-exclusive palette**: Beechnut/Black/Cherry/Fern/Ivory/
  Maple/Navy at launch (Holiday 1993). Later years add additional
  colors per M3 (Mandarin Spring 1995, Citron/Granite Autumn 1995).
