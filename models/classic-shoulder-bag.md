# Classic Shoulder Bag (9170)

## Lead

The **Classic Shoulder Bag (Style 9170)** is documented in primary
Coach catalog copy as **"the original Coach bag"** — Coach's own
brand-published claim, primary-confirmed in the Spring 1989 catalog
[coach-catalogs-1960-1989]. Of all silhouettes in the archive, the
9170 carries the strongest brand-published claim to status as
Coach's foundational handbag silhouette.

## The "original Coach bag" framing (Spring 1989 primary)

Spring 1989 catalog page 29:

> "After more than two decades, women still regard this bag—the
> original Coach bag—as an essential part of their Coach
> collection." [coach-catalogs-1960-1989]

This single sentence anchors three load-bearing facts:

1. **"Original"** — Coach's own claim that 9170 is the foundational
   silhouette of the Coach handbag line. No other 1989-era catalog
   model receives this framing.
2. **"More than two decades"** — 1989 minus 20+ years = pre-1969.
   The 9170 was already a bag in continuous production by 1969.
3. **"Still regard"** — the bag was in active production as of
   Spring 1989, ~26 years into Cashin's 1962 collection arc and
   15 years after her contract end (1974).

## Designer attribution — open

**The 9170 may or may not be a Bonnie Cashin design.** Two readings:

1. **Cashin design**: Cashin's Coach contract ran 1962-1974. If the
   9170 was introduced in the 1962-1968 window, it would fall within
   her tenure. The "more than two decades" Spring 1989 framing
   permits any introduction year from 1969 backward. A 1962-1968
   debut places it in the Cashin window.

2. **Pre-Cashin in-house**: Coach's pre-1962 line was wallets,
   billfolds, and small leather goods — no documented handbag line.
   But the company itself dates to 1941 (as Gail Leather Products),
   and a transitional handbag predating Cashin's 1962 collection is
   possible if undocumented.

The "more than two decades" framing leaves both readings open. The
Cashin designation requires direct verification against the Cashin
papers at UCLA Library Special Collections (gift of estate, 2003) —
which document her Coach output sketch-by-sketch but have not been
deep-read for this archive yet.

Designer attribution `tentative` until either Cashin papers or a
1960s catalog primary settles it.

## Continuity — long production run

The 9170 is in production:
- **Pre-1969**: per Spring 1989 "more than two decades" framing
  [coach-catalogs-1960-1989]
- **Spring 1989**: $140, 6-color palette
  [coach-catalogs-1960-1989]
- **Continued**: silhouette appears in subsequent late-NYC catalogs
  (1990-1994 specific verifications pending)

A 25+ year production run as of Spring 1989, making the 9170 one of
the longest-running silhouettes in the archive — peer to the Duffle
Sac (in continuous production since 1973 per the 1996-1997 Source
Book).

## Authentication

For a vintage Classic Shoulder Bag (9170):

- **Style 9170** in creed [coach-catalogs-1960-1989]
- **Pre-1994 era**: LEATHERWARE creed wordmark expected
- **Pre-1985 examples**: "Made in New York City, U.S.A." or earlier
  factory framings possible (Carlstadt, NJ from 1979 onward — early
  examples may show NYC-only stamping)
- **Solid brass hardware** throughout
- **Glove-tanned cowhide** with raw or burnished edges
- **Color in 6-color Spring 1989 palette** for that specific year:
  Black, British Tan, Mahogany, Navy, Putty, Tabac. Earlier/later
  examples may have different palettes.

A 9170 is a strong candidate for any "I have a vintage Coach but
don't know what it is" inquiry — it is the most-sold and
longest-running Coach silhouette in the archive's primary record.

## Open verification items

1. **Cashin attribution** — needs UCLA Cashin papers cross-reference
   to confirm or deny her authorship.
2. **First-catalog appearance** — earliest dated catalog showing
   the 9170 would settle the introduction year. The 1960 catalog
   (pre-Cashin, wallets only) does not include it. The Spring 1989
   "more than two decades" framing places it pre-1969.
3. **Pre-1989 dimensional and palette consistency** — earlier
   catalogs (1981-1984) need deep-read for cross-year continuity
   of the 9170 specs.
