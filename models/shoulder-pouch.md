A 1970s NYC-made shoulder pouch, with a black leather example
documented in [yourgreatfinds-collectors-guide]. The bag is a
pouch-form silhouette with a shoulder strap. Dimensions, full color

## Authentication

A 1970s NYC-era Shoulder Pouch should carry the diagnostics common
to the Classic NYC era (cross-reference `authentication/` for the
full era-keyed matrix):

- **Creed wording (E3 diagnostic).** Should read "COACH LEATHERWARE"
  with "LEATHERWARE" in small letters beneath "COACH," with "Made in
  New York City, U.S.A." on NYC-factory examples
  [coachbagrehab-dating, vintagecoachie].
- **Serial format.** 1970s examples carry a digit-only unique serial
  in the `xxx-xxxx` format per [coachbagrehab-dating] — `tentative`.
  Some early examples may predate creed serialization; an absent
  serial is not by itself a fake tell.
- **Hardware.** Solid brass, non-magnetic. A magnet that sticks
  indicates plated steel and is a strong reproduction signal
  [coachbagrehab-dating].
- **Construction.** Glove-tanned cowhide with raw or burnished edges,
  not painted [coachbagrehab-dating]. Unlined construction is correct
  for many NYC-era leather bags and is not a fake tell.
