# Drawstring Backpack

The Drawstring Backpack is a Classic NYC-era backpack-form Coach bag,
style **9994**, distinguished by a drawstring closure at the top of
the body rather than a flap or zipper [yourgreatfinds-collectors-guide].
It belongs to the broader 9990-series of small NYC-era styles and is
documented in the dealer literature as an uncommon find, with at least
one rare colorway noted.

## Description

The bag is a backpack silhouette in glove-tanned cowhide, gathered at
the top by a leather drawstring that cinches the opening closed.
Hardware is solid brass throughout — drawstring grommets/anchors and
strap fittings — consistent with NYC-era construction. Style 9994
places the bag in the post-1974 in-house design era; the four-digit
9000-series style numbers are a hallmark of Coach's Classic NYC
production and are not a Cashin-era convention.

## Colors

[yourgreatfinds-collectors-guide] documents an **Aqua** colorway as
notably rare for this style. Most NYC-era 9000-series bags appear in
the standard British Tan / black / burgundy / mahogany palette;
saturated colors like aqua are uncommon and are flagged accordingly
in the dealer record. Confidence on the aqua attribution is
`tentative` — single Tier 3 source.

## Designer attribution

The Drawstring Backpack is **not** a Bonnie Cashin design. Cashin's
Coach contract ended in **1974** [wwd-coach-early-years,
vintagecoachie]; the 9994 style number sits in the 9000-series numbering
scheme that Coach used for its post-Cashin in-house catalog. Any
listing or article attributing this bag to Cashin is wrong on the era
alone.

## Authentication

Standard NYC-era diagnostics apply:

- **Numbered creed.** Pre-1994 examples carry a digit-only serial
  (e.g., `xxxx-xxx`) with no style number in the creed; post-1994
  examples carry an `[A-M][0-9][A-Z]-9994` code where the trailing
  9994 is the style number.
- **Solid brass hardware.** Non-magnetic; develops a warm honey
  patina. A magnet that sticks indicates plated steel and a fake or
  much later piece.
- **Glove-tanned cowhide**, drum-dyed through; cut edges show body
  color. Painted glossy edges are a fake or reproduction signal for
  this era.
- **Drawstring construction.** The leather drawstring should pass
  through brass grommets or leather-reinforced channels with no
  plastic or polyester reinforcement on a vintage example.

Confidence on this entry is `tentative` overall — the style number
and rare-aqua attribution rest on a single Tier 3 dealer compilation
[yourgreatfinds-collectors-guide]. Promotion to `likely` requires a
dated catalog page or a second independent source documenting the
9994 style number.
