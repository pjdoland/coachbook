## Description

A Classic NYC-era leather bag whose name derives from its pocket-shaped
form. Colors documented in the collector literature are **black, bone,
and khaki** [yourgreatfinds-collectors-guide]. Style number, dimensions,
introduction year, and closure type have not yet been confirmed against
a primary catalog and are recorded as open verification items. Hardware
is presumed solid brass per Classic NYC-era construction norms; this
should be verified against a dated example before promotion above
`tentative`.

## Authentication

Era diagnostics for a Classic NYC-era Pocket Purse follow the standard
E3–E4 matrix; cross-reference `authentication/` for full feature lists.

- **Designer.** The Pocket Purse is **not** a Bonnie Cashin design.
  Cashin's tenure ended in 1974, and any Coach silhouette introduced
  after that is a Classic NYC-era staff/in-house design unless dated
  otherwise from a primary source. Community attributions of post-1974
  bags to Cashin are folklore.
- **Creed wording.** A pre-1994 Pocket Purse should read "COACH
  LEATHERWARE" with "LEATHERWARE" stamped in small letters beneath
  "COACH," typically with "Made in United States" or "Made in New York
  City, U.S.A." on earlier examples — the single most cited textual
  anchor for pre-1994 dating.
- **Serial format.** Pre-1994 examples carry a digit-only unique serial
  (formats like `xxx-xxxx` in the 1970s and `xxxx-xxx` in the 1980s,
  per Tier 3 sources — `tentative`). Any post-1994 production would use
  the `[A-M][year-digit][factory-letter]-[style-number]` pattern, with
  the documented single-digit-year ambiguity (e.g., a "6" resolves to
  either 1996 or 2006 without contextual cross-checks).
- **Hardware.** Solid brass, non-magnetic. A magnet that sticks
  indicates plated steel and is a strong fake or post-vintage signal.
- **Construction.** Glove-tanned cowhide with raw or burnished edges.
  Painted/glossy edges on a bag claiming 1980s manufacture is a strong
  reproduction signal.
- **Lining.** Many NYC-era bags are unlined; an unlined Pocket Purse is
  not by itself a fake tell.
