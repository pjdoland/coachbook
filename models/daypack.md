## Lead

The Daypack is Coach's NYC-era backpack form, identified by style
number 9791, with a smaller Mini Daypack variant at style 9960
[yourgreatfinds-collectors-guide]. The separate **Drawstring Backpack
(9994)** is a related but distinct Coach backpack-form model — not a
Daypack variant — and is documented elsewhere in research stream D
with a noted rare Aqua coloring [yourgreatfinds-collectors-guide].

## Description

A glove-tanned cowhide backpack in the Classic NYC-era hardware
vocabulary: solid brass fittings throughout. Two sizes are documented
in the collector literature — the standard Daypack (9791) and the
smaller Mini Daypack (9960) [yourgreatfinds-collectors-guide]. Precise
dimensions, closure details, and color ranges are not yet anchored to
a primary catalog and are recorded here as `tentative`.

## Variants

- **Daypack (9791)** — standard size [yourgreatfinds-collectors-guide].
- **Mini Daypack (9960)** — smaller variant
  [yourgreatfinds-collectors-guide].

The **Drawstring Backpack (9994)** shares the backpack form but is a
separate model in the Classic NYC catalog, not a Daypack size variant
[yourgreatfinds-collectors-guide]. It deserves its own entry; the
rare Aqua coloring noted in research stream D should anchor that
future entry.

## Authentication

Tells specific to a vintage Daypack — cross-reference the era-keyed
matrix in `authentication/` for full feature lists:

- **Style number 9791 (standard) or 9960 (mini)** on the leather creed
  inside the bag for numbered-creed examples
  [yourgreatfinds-collectors-guide]. A style number alone does not
  date a specific bag — Coach reused style numbers across production
  years.
- **Creed wording (E3–E4 diagnostic).** A pre-1994 Daypack should
  read "COACH LEATHERWARE" with "LEATHERWARE" stamped in small letters
  beneath "COACH," typically with "Made in United States" on Classic
  NYC examples [coachbagrehab-dating, vintagecoachie]. This is the
  single most cited textual anchor for dating.
- **Serial format.** Pre-1994 Daypack bags carry a digit-only unique
  serial (formats like `xxx-xxxx` in the 1970s and `xxxx-xxx` in the
  1980s, per E3 — `tentative`) [coachbagrehab-dating]. Post-1994
  production (if the style number carried forward) would use the
  `[A-M][year-digit][factory-letter]-9791` (or `-9960`) pattern, with
  the documented single-digit-year ambiguity (e.g., a "6" resolves to
  either 1996 or 2006 without contextual cross-checks).
- **Hardware.** Solid brass, non-magnetic. A magnet that sticks to
  the hardware indicates plated steel and is a strong fake or
  post-vintage signal.
- **Construction.** Glove-tanned cowhide with raw or burnished edges.
  Painted/glossy edges on a bag claiming 1980s manufacture is a
  reproduction signal.
