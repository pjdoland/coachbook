# Classic Hobo (9855)

## Lead — primary-confirmed Spring 1989 launch

**The Classic Hobo (Style 9855) debuts in the Spring 1989 catalog
as a NEW Classics-line addition at $172** [coach-catalogs-1960-1989]
per research stream M2. The silhouette receives an immediate
Spectator-trim variant Fall 1989 with the corresponding **Style 6855
Classic Hobo Spectator** [coach-catalogs-1960-1989] — part of the
8-style Spectator launch with Tabac trim per M2.

NOT to be confused with the **Ergo Hobo (Style 9026)** — a
Krakoff-era Ergo line silhouette from 1997 Early Fall (per M4),
distinct silhouette family.

## Era and designer

**Era E4 (Classic NYC late, 1985–1994).** Designer is **in-house
Coach team**. NOT Cashin (her contract ended 1974, 15 years before
the Classic Hobo launched).

The Classic Hobo's 9855 number sits within the late-1980s
Classics-line numbering range that produced multiple new
silhouettes Spring 1989 (Compartment Bag 9850, Compartment Bag
Small 9845, Classic Hobo 9855, plus later Plaza/Court/Regency Brief
in Fall 1989 Continental Series). The 1989 Classics expansion was
substantial.

## Spectator variant 6855 (Fall 1989)

Per Fall 1989 Spectator Collection launch (M2), the **Classic Hobo
Spectator (Style 6855)** at $186 launched as one of 8 Spectator
styles, all originally with **Tabac trim** (transitioned to British
Tan trim by Holiday 1991). The 6855 carries the "6XXX = Spectator
variant of corresponding 9XXX Classic" naming convention codified
by the Fall 1989 Spectator Collection launch.

## Authentication

- **Style 9855** (Classic) or **6855** (Spectator) in creed
  [coach-catalogs-1960-1989]
- **Pre-1994 era**: LEATHERWARE creed wordmark expected
- **Solid brass hardware**, non-magnetic
- **Glove-tanned cowhide** with raw or burnished edges
- **6855 Spectator variant**: contrast trim color is a dating signal
  — Tabac trim ~Fall 1989 to Holiday 1990 (Cocoa-trim sub-line
  6018/6021/6022 is a Holiday 1990 sub-line, not 6855); British
  Tan trim Holiday 1991+
