# Regency Brief (5255)

## Lead — Continental Series Fall 1989 launch

**The Regency Brief (Style 5255) launched Fall 1989** as part of
the **Coach Continental Series** debut [coach-catalogs-1960-1989]
per research stream M2. The Continental Series launched with four
styles:
- **Plaza Bag (9860)**
- **Plaza Bag Large (9865)**
- **Court Bag (9870)**
- **Regency Brief (5255)** — this entry

The 5XXX prefix reflects the **briefcase/portfolio numbering
convention** Coach used through the 1980s — distinct from the
9XXX Classics handbag prefix and the later 4XXX Lightweight/sub-
collection prefix. The Regency Brief is the briefcase-line
member of the Continental Series; its three handbag-line siblings
(Plaza, Plaza Large, Court) all use 9XXX numbers.

## Era and designer

**Era E4 (Classic NYC late, 1985–1994).** Designer is **in-house
Coach team**. NOT Cashin (her contract ended 1974, 15 years before
the Regency Brief launched).

By Holiday 1991 (per M's main file) the Continental Series styles
had been re-absorbed into Classics-line branding. The Regency Brief
likely continued production through the early 1990s as a standard
briefcase-line model rather than under the Continental Series
sub-series banner.

## Authentication

- **Style 5255** in creed [coach-catalogs-1960-1989]
- **Pre-1994 era**: LEATHERWARE creed wordmark expected
- **Solid brass hardware**, non-magnetic (turnlock or briefcase-
  appropriate closure)
- **Glove-tanned cowhide** with raw or burnished edges
- **5XXX = briefcase/portfolio** numbering convention
