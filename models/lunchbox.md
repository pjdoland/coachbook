## Lead

The Lunchbox (style 9991) is a Coach Classic NYC-era model documented
in a single Tier 3 dealer compilation [yourgreatfinds-collectors-guide]
with minimal additional detail. The name refers to the bag's
lunchbox-shaped silhouette. Confidence is `tentative` until a dated
catalog scan or a second independent source corroborates the style
number and silhouette.

## Description

The model name "Lunchbox" describes the silhouette: a structured,
boxy carrier reminiscent of a metal lunch pail
[yourgreatfinds-collectors-guide]. Beyond the name and style number
9991, the surviving public record is sparse. Dimensions, available
colors, closure type, and introduction year are all unconfirmed. The
bag appears only as an entry in a long Tier 3 collector list of
NYC-era styles; no images, catalog appearances, or dimensional data
have been located in free public sources to date.

## Authentication

A pre-1994 Lunchbox should carry the diagnostics common to the
Classic NYC era (cross-reference `authentication/` for the full
era-keyed matrix):

- **Style number 9991.** Visible on the leather creed inside the bag
  on numbered-creed examples [yourgreatfinds-collectors-guide]. A
  style number alone does not date a specific bag — Coach reused
  style numbers across multiple production years.
- **Creed wording (E3 diagnostic).** A pre-1994 Lunchbox should read
  "COACH LEATHERWARE" with "LEATHERWARE" stamped in small letters
  beneath "COACH," typically with "Made in United States" or
  "Made in New York City, U.S.A." on earlier examples
  [coachbagrehab-dating, vintagecoachie].
- **Serial format.** Pre-1994 examples carry a digit-only unique
  serial (`xxx-xxxx` 1970s, `xxxx-xxx` 1980s) per
  [coachbagrehab-dating] — `tentative`. Any post-1994 production
  carrying 9991 forward (E4) would use the
  `[A-M][year-digit][factory-letter]-9991` pattern with the
  documented single-digit-year ambiguity.
- **Hardware.** Solid brass, non-magnetic. A magnet that sticks
  indicates plated steel and is a strong reproduction signal
  [coachbagrehab-dating].
