# Ergo Hobo

**Primary catalog evidence (2026-05-02 update).** The 1997 Early Fall
catalog launches the Ergo line with a dedicated 2-page spread titled
"Ergo / The shapely ones" and six styles. The **Ergo Hobo is style
9026** — a correction from earlier archive framing that conflated 9020
(Mini Zip Bag) and 9033 (Pocket Zip Bag) with the Hobo
[coach-catalogs-1960-1989].

Launch chronology:
- **1997 Summer catalog** — first appearance, framed as preview: "the
  new Coach Ergo Collection, **previewed here**"
- **1997 Early Fall catalog** — full launch: "Ergo / The shapely ones,"
  six styles
- **Holiday 1997** — Holly Solomon ("SoHo Arts Pioneer") modeling for
  Ergo, Coach's first named cultural-figure brand association.

Where the Classic NYC canon (Willis, Court, Sheridan) is structured,
flap-fronted, and turnlock-closed, the Ergo Hobo is rounded, slouchy,
and uses sewn-on straps — a deliberate stylistic break from the
silhouette vocabulary this archive otherwise documents. It is included
here as **era contrast only**.

## Description

The Ergo Hobo is a rounded, soft-sided shoulder bag with **sewn-on
straps** — that is, the strap is permanently attached at each end
rather than clipped via a brass dogleash to a D-ring as on Willis and
Court. The silhouette reads as a hobo: curved top, no rigid flap, no
front turnlock plate. Per D1 it was offered in **small** and **large**
scales [yourgreatfinds-collectors-guide].

Hardware is presumed solid brass consistent with late-1990s Coach
production, but the spec has not been catalog-confirmed for this
model — `tentative`.

## History

- **Ergo line previewed**: 1997 Summer catalog [coach-catalogs-1960-1989].
  Confidence: `confirmed` (primary catalog).
- **Ergo line launched**: 1997 Early Fall catalog with six styles
  including the Ergo Hobo (9026) [coach-catalogs-1960-1989].
  Confidence: `confirmed`.
- **Ergo Hobo introduced**: 1997 Early Fall, style 9026
  [coach-catalogs-1960-1989]. Confidence: `confirmed`. Earlier archive
  framing as "1998" was based on a single Tier 3 source (D1) and is
  superseded by the 1997 catalog evidence.
- **Position in the timeline**: Reed Krakoff was hired as creative
  director in 1996 [fundinguniverse-coach]; the Signature C
  collection (the end-of-vintage marker for this archive) launched in
  2001 [fundinguniverse-coach]. The Ergo Hobo sits in the middle of
  that window — after the silhouette break that Krakoff initiated but
  before the visual pivot to logo monogram.

The Ergo line is the first widely-documented Krakoff-era silhouette
that visibly diverges from the NYC structured-flap vocabulary. That
divergence is the diagnostic point for an archive of NYC-era bags;
the Ergo Hobo's value here is as an era boundary marker, not as a
canon entry.

- **Black** (9033)

  Costa Rica, where Coach was producing bags by the late 1990s
  [purseforum-costa-rica].

Both are `tentative`.

## Era context — why this entry exists

This archive's scope (per `PLAN.md`) is the NYC era through the
Krakoff transition, with Krakoff-era bags **included as contrast
only**. The Ergo Hobo earns an entry because it is the cleanest
illustration of the silhouette break that defines the transition:

- **NYC era (E3–E4)**: structured flap, front turnlock, removable
  strap with brass dogleash clip and D-rings, glove-tanned cowhide
  with raw or burnished edges.
- **Krakoff transition (E5)**: rounded hobo silhouette, no flap,
  **sewn-on** straps (no removable-clip hardware at the strap-to-bag
  interface), softer construction.

Anyone using this archive to authenticate a bag should recognize
that an "Ergo"-style silhouette is **not** an NYC-era silhouette;
attempts to date such a bag against E3/E4 authentication checks
(turnlock construction, dogleash stamping) will fail because the
relevant hardware is absent by design.

## Authentication

Light coverage — the Ergo Hobo is out of full scope; what follows is
the minimum needed to place a bag in the right era.

- **Post-1994 serial format expected.** By 1998, all Coach creeds
  carry the post-1994 letter-digit-letter pattern: `[A-M][0-9][A-Z]-
  ####` [coachbagrehab-dating]. Pre-1994 digit-only serials should
  not appear on a real Ergo Hobo.
- **Worked example: `A8P-9020`.** Decoded by position:
  - `A` — month letter A = January
  - `8` — year digit 8
  - `P` — factory letter P = Costa Rica
    [purseforum-costa-rica]
  - `9020` — Ergo Hobo style number

  Under a 1998 reading, `A8P-9020` describes a January 1998
  Costa Rica Ergo Hobo — internally consistent with D1's 1998
  introduction date.

  **Single-digit year ambiguity**: the year digit `8` resolves
  equally to **1998 or 2008** under the post-1994 scheme (see
  `research/E-authentication.md` §2 and the worked Willis example
  `B6D-9927` for the same caveat). Cross-check creed wording,
  country-of-origin tag, and lining material before committing to a
  year reading. Do not publish a year from the code alone.
- **Sewn-on straps are correct.** Absence of a brass dogleash clip
  at the strap-to-bag interface is not a fake tell on this model;
  it is the design.
- **Designer attribution.** The Ergo Hobo is a Krakoff-era in-house
  design. It is **not** a Bonnie Cashin design — Cashin's contract
  ended in **1974**, twenty-four years before the Ergo Hobo's
  introduction. Any listing crediting Cashin is wrong on its face
  (this is the most common community misattribution; see `STYLE.md`
  §4 high-folklore zones).

For full era diagnostics see `authentication/` (forthcoming) and
`research/E-authentication.md`.
