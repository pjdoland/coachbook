# Soho Collection

## Lead — corrected timeline (rename was Fall Preview 1993, not 1994)

**Update 2026-05-02 (research stream P2).** Coach.com's "Soho
introduced 1994" claim is off by ~9 months. Primary catalog evidence
shows the **Lightweight → Soho rename happened in Fall Preview 1993**
[coach-catalogs-1960-1989] — at least one full season before the
brand-published 1994 date. The Coach.com retroactive date may
collapse the rename and the NYC SoHo flagship store opening (1994)
into a single "Soho 1994" narrative.

Primary catalog evidence (corrected timeline):
- **Early Summer 1987**: Coach Lightweight Collection LAUNCHES (10
  styles + 11-color palette) [coach-catalogs-1960-1989] per O2
- **Pre-Spring 1989**: 14+ Lightweight styles in catalog
- **Holiday 1991**: Lightweight Collection at 10 styles including
  Soho Bag 4082, Whitney 4115, Hudson 4113, Cafe 4111, Studio 4081
- **Spring 1993**: Lightweight Collection still named "Lightweight"
  with 5-style expansion + 3 new colors (per M)
- **Fall Preview 1993**: **RENAME MOMENT — "Soho Series" brand name
  confirmed in print** [coach-catalogs-1960-1989] per P2. Discrete
  sub-collection page header "SOHO SERIES" in same all-caps display
  setting as MANHATTAN SERIES, SHERIDAN COLLECTION, BUSINESS
  COLLECTION, CLASSIC COLLECTION
- **Holiday Preview 1993, Holiday 1993**: "SOHO SERIES" header reused
  with identical brand voice
- **Early Autumn 1994**: "Lightweight leather" still used as material
  descriptor in body copy on individual bags (per P3 partial), but
  the collection-level "Soho Series" / "Soho Collection" branding
  is in use
- **1994 (Coach.com claim)**: Coach.com's brand-published "Soho
  introduced 1994" is approximately when the NYC SoHo flagship store
  opens — possibly the source of the conflated date
- **Spring 1995 Preview**: Cover banner "**Five New Soho Styles**" —
  fully named Soho expansion
- **1996-1997 Source Book**: "Soho — A lightweight version of our
  glove-tanned leather… youthful, modern appeal… clean, casual
  shapes"

**Critically**: many "Soho" styles documented in 1995-1996 catalogs
were already in the catalog 4-8 years earlier as Lightweight
Collection styles — Whitney 4115, Soho Bag 4082, Waverly 4133,
Bleeker 4154, etc. The 4XXX prefix carries forward unchanged
across the Fall Preview 1993 rename.

See also: [models/lightweight-collection.md](lightweight-collection.md)
which documents the predecessor line; [models/soho-bag.md](soho-bag.md)
which documents the Soho Bag (4082) specifically as a Holiday 1991
primary-confirmed Lightweight Collection style.

## Original framing for context

Coach.com publishes 1994 as the Soho introduction date on the current
Vintage Soho Bag re-release product page [coach-vintage-soho]. Under
the renaming reading above, this is best interpreted as the
collection-level name change, not a per-silhouette debut. The launch
moment sits at the very end of the Classic NYC era and just before
the Reed Krakoff transition: Cashin had been gone twenty years,
Krakoff would not arrive for two more, and the Soho-renamed line is
the last sub-collection rebranded under the company's NYC-era design
language.

## History

- **Introduced**: 1994. Coach.com states the Vintage Soho Bag was
  "first introduced 1994" [coach-vintage-soho]. Confidence:
  `confirmed`.
- **Launch context**: 1994 was the year Coach opened its two-level
  New York flagship store [fundinguniverse-coach]. The Soho launch
  and the flagship opening were the two most visible 1994 marks on
  the brand, and they reinforce each other as a single moment of
  late-NYC-era expansion before the Sara Lee–era pivot toward Krakoff.
- **Cashin attribution**: none. Bonnie Cashin's contract with Coach
  ended in 1974 [research/C-cashin.md]. Soho is a 1994 in-house
  design and is not a Cashin silhouette regardless of community
  claims to the contrary.
- **Modern re-release**: Coach has reissued the model as the
  "Vintage Soho Bag" (SKU CU909) in its current Coach Originals line
  [coach-vintage-soho, coach-originals]. A re-released Soho is in
  current production and is not a vintage Soho — see "Modern
  re-release" below.

## Description

The Soho is a 1994 NYC-era Coach silhouette. Beyond the introduction
date itself, descriptive specifics — exact dimensions, the canonical
style number, the original closure type, and the launch color list —

Hardware is `tentative` solid brass, consistent with the NYC-era
construction standard [research/E-authentication.md] but not yet

This entry will be filled in as catalog scans and dated reference

## Authentication

What to check on a vintage Soho specifically:

- **Creed wording, transitional period.** 1994 sits exactly on the
  pre-1994 / post-1994 creed boundary
  [research/E-authentication.md]. A Soho creed reading "COACH
  LEATHERWARE / Made in United States" with the small-caps
  "LEATHERWARE" beneath "COACH" indicates pre-1994 production
  conventions still in use; a creed reading "COACH" alone (no
  "LEATHERWARE") indicates the post-1994 wording. Either is plausible
  for an early Soho — wording overlap during the 1994–~1996
  transition is documented [research/E-authentication.md §1].
- **Serial format.** The post-1994 letter-digit-letter-dash-digits
  serial format begins in 1994 [research/E-authentication.md §2]. A
  Soho carrying a digit-only `xxxx-xxx` serial is highly suspect for
  this model; an early Soho should carry the post-1994 format with
  year digit `4` (1994), `5` (1995), or later. Single-digit-year
  ambiguity applies — `4` resolves to 1994 *or* 2004 under the same
  scheme [research/E-authentication.md §2] — so the serial cannot
  date the bag without cross-checks.
- **Country of origin.** Costa Rican production began ~1994
  [research/E-authentication.md §3, E12]. A "Made in Costa Rica"
  Soho is era-appropriate; the factory letter `P` is community-
  reported as the Costa Rica code [research/E-authentication.md §3]
  (`tentative`).
- **Solid brass hardware.** Non-magnetic. Magnetic hardware on a
  bag claiming 1994–1996 manufacture is a strong fake or
  post-vintage-plated-steel signal [research/E-authentication.md §4].
- **Edge treatment.** Glove-tanned cowhide with raw or burnished
  edges. Painted glossy edges are a strong fake or reproduction
  signal — Coach NYC-era construction does not paint edges
  [research/E-authentication.md, PLAN §6].
- **Designer attribution.** A vintage Soho is not a Cashin design.
  Any listing or article attributing Soho to Cashin is wrong on its
  face — Cashin's tenure ended in 1974, and Soho launched in 1994
  [research/C-cashin.md].

## Modern re-release

Coach currently sells a "Vintage Soho Bag" (SKU **CU909**) in the
Coach Originals re-release line [coach-vintage-soho, coach-originals].
This is the source of the brand-published 1994 introduction date that
anchors the rest of this entry.

A re-released Soho is in current production and is not a vintage Soho.
Modern re-releases will carry post-2014 sewn-in country-of-origin tags
rather than creed serials [research/E-authentication.md §6, E8] and
will not match pre-1994 or transitional creed wording. Treat
CU909 as its own catalog entry — useful as a brand-published date
citation, not as evidence about the original 1994 production.
