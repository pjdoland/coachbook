# Pocket Bag (9875)

## Lead — primary-confirmed Fall 1989 launch

**The Pocket Bag (Style 9875) debuts in the Fall 1989 catalog as a
NEW Classics-line addition** [coach-catalogs-1960-1989] per
research stream M2. Part of the Fall 1989 Classics expansion (9875
Pocket Bag, 9880 Classic Satchel, 9885 Club Satchel) that ran
alongside the Continental Series and Spectator Collection launches.

NOT to be confused with **Pocket Purse (Style 9755)** — earlier
Classic-line model in continuous production from at least Spring
1989 onward at $118 per M2.

## Era and designer

**Era E4 (Classic NYC late, 1985–1994).** Designer is **in-house
Coach team**. NOT Cashin (her contract ended 1974, 15 years before
the Pocket Bag launched).
