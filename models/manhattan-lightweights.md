# Manhattan Lightweights Collection

## Lead

The Manhattan Lightweights Collection launched **Spring 1993** —
the Spring Preview 1993 catalog cover announces "Introducing
Manhattan Lightweights Pages 2-5" [coach-catalogs-1960-1989]. A
five-style sub-collection in Coach's Lightweight (thinned
glove-tanned cowhide) family with a distinct color palette of
"dusty neutrals."

## Brand voice (Spring 1993)

> "In Manhattan Lightweights, ornamentation is spared to accentuate
> the elegance that lies beyond simple shape. While encompassing all
> the softness of Coach's Lightweight leather, this new collection
> exemplifies sleek design. Its understatement is realized in subtle
> shades of dusty neutrals like Celadon, Terracotta and Buff, as well
> as Blue and Black."

## Manhattan Lightweights palette (Spring 1993)

5 colors, distinct from Classic Collection palette:
- **Black**
- **Blue** (Manhattan-specific shade, distinct from Navy)
- **Buff** (light beige)
- **Celadon** (pale green/grey, "dusty neutral")
- **Terracotta** (red-orange-brown)

## Distribution at launch

Per Spring Preview 1993 catalog:
> "AVAILABLE EXCLUSIVELY THROUGH MAIL ORDER AND SELECT COACH STORES"

So Manhattan Lightweights at launch was a limited-distribution
sub-collection — not in all Coach retail stores. By Fall 1993 the
"Manhattan Series" appears in the main catalog at pages 54-55
(reduced from 4 pages to 2 — possibly slimmer offering or merged
with broader Classic).

## Distinctive features

1. **Style number prefix 4XXX** (shared with Sheridan Collection
   and other 1992-1993 sub-collections)
2. **Lightweight leather** — thinned glove-tanned cowhide (process
   "reduces the thickness and weight of our glove-tanned cowhide"
   per Holiday 1991 Lightweight Collection brand voice)
3. **"Hidden magnetic snap closure"** on Museum Bag and Bistro Bag —
   no visible turnlock
4. **Top zipper closure** on the three larger styles
5. **"Dusty neutrals"** palette — distinct from Classic Collection
   browns and Sheridan's two-tones

## Authentication

For a Manhattan Lightweights bag:
- **Style 4125, 4126, 4127, 4128, or 4129** in creed
  [coach-catalogs-1960-1989]
- **Lightweight leather** — softer drape than Classic glove-tanned;
  thinner profile
- **Manhattan palette colors** — Black / Blue / Buff / Celadon /
  Terracotta (or later additions)
- **Hidden magnetic snap or top zipper** — not turnlock

## Designer

In-house Coach team. NOT Cashin (Cashin's tenure 1962-1974 ended
nearly 20 years before Manhattan Lightweights launched).
Designer attribution `confirmed` to Coach's pre-Krakoff (Krakoff
joined December 1996) in-house team.
