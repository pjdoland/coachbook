# Classic Satchel (9880)

## Lead — primary-confirmed Fall 1989 launch

**The Classic Satchel (Style 9880) debuts in the Fall 1989 catalog
as a NEW Classics-line addition** [coach-catalogs-1960-1989] per
research stream M2. The Classic Satchel is part of the Fall 1989
expansion that also included Pocket Bag (9875), Club Satchel (9885),
and the Continental Series (Plaza/Court/Regency Brief). Distinct
from the **Madison Satchel (9725)** — Spring 1986 debut Classic-line
single model — and the **Madison Satchel Large (9765)** — Madison
satchel-family large size.

## Era and designer

**Era E4 (Classic NYC late, 1985–1994).** Designer is **in-house
Coach team**. NOT Cashin (her contract ended 1974, 15 years before
the Classic Satchel launched).

The 9880 number sits in the late-1980s Classics-line numbering
range that produced the Fall 1989 expansion (9870 Court, 9875
Pocket Bag, 9880 Classic Satchel, 9885 Club Satchel) — a
multi-style late-NYC-era roster addition.
