## Lead

The Wristlet Clutch is a 1970s NYC-produced small leather good
documented in dealer-compiled references as a clutch with an optional
wrist strap, configured for convertible shoulder or clutch carry
[yourgreatfinds-collectors-guide]. Bone and putty are the documented
colorways [yourgreatfinds-collectors-guide]. The 1970s production
window straddles the end of Bonnie Cashin's Coach contract (1974) and
the early classic NYC era; designer attribution is `tentative` and no
primary catalog citation has been matched to this model in the
archive's working references.

## History

The model is named in a single Tier 3 dealer compilation
[yourgreatfinds-collectors-guide] as a 1970s NYC piece. No catalog
page, museum holding, or higher-tier source has yet been matched to
the Wristlet Clutch. Treat the introduction year, exact configuration,
and run length as `tentative` until corroborated.

## Description

The bag is described as a small clutch with a wrist strap, additionally
configurable for shoulder carry — i.e., a convertible carry between
clutch, wristlet, and shoulder forms [yourgreatfinds-collectors-guide].
Bone and putty are the two recorded colorways
[yourgreatfinds-collectors-guide]. Dimensions, lining, and hardware
specifics beyond "solid brass" period-typical fittings are not
documented in the working references and remain open verification
items.

## Authentication

Authenticate against the 1970s NYC profile rather than a model-specific
fingerprint, since no catalog match exists yet:

- **Numbered creed**, "COACH LEATHERWARE / EST. 1941 / NEW YORK CITY"
  era wording, U.S.A. country line. The numbered creed is an NYC-era
  feature and should be present on a 1970s Wristlet Clutch.
- **Solid brass hardware**, including the turnlock if present.
  Non-magnetic, heavy for size; a magnet that sticks indicates plated
  steel and a much later piece.
- **Glove-tanned cowhide**, drum-dyed through; cut edges show the
  same color as the surface.
- **Made in U.S.A.** for 1970s production. Offshoring was gradual and
  model-specific; a 1970s NYC bag should not carry a foreign country
  stamp.

A bag advertised as a 1970s Wristlet Clutch with a non-numbered creed,
a magnetic-steel turnlock, painted edges, or a foreign country line is
inconsistent with the dealer-compiled description and should be
treated with skepticism.

## Designer attribution

The 1970s production window spans the last years of Bonnie Cashin's
Coach contract (which ended in 1974) and the early classic NYC era
under in-house design. Without a dated catalog page or a Cashin-papers
illustration matching this model, attribution cannot be assigned to
Cashin or to the in-house team with confidence. Default attribution
in this archive is `in-house` with `designer_confidence: tentative`;
promotion requires either a 1962–1974 catalog page (which would
support a Cashin attribution) or a post-1974 NYC catalog page (which
would confirm in-house). See `research/C-cashin.md` §5 on the
over-attribution problem.
