# Whitney Bag

## Lead

The Whitney Bag (Style 4115) is a Coach **Lightweight Collection**
crossbody, primary-confirmed in the Holiday 1991 catalog at $220
[coach-catalogs-1960-1989]. Earlier archive framing as a Classic
NYC-era model was incorrect — Whitney is in the Lightweight
Collection (thinned glove-tanned cowhide), not Classic.

## Description (Holiday 1991 primary)

Per Holiday 1991 catalog Lightweight Collection pages:
> "B. WHITNEY BAG  No. 4115  $220  Roomy main compartment with top
> zipper closure and inside zipper pocket. Full-length front pocket
> under flap. 42" Adjustable shoulder strap. 11¾" × 11¾" × 4¼"
> Berry, Black, Blue, Chamois, Cocoa, Ivy, Taupe. Pictured in Berry."

## Authentication

For a Whitney Bag claiming pre-1994 production:
- **Style 4115** in creed [coach-catalogs-1960-1989]
- **Lightweight Collection palette colors** — Berry, Cocoa, Ivy,
  Chamois are Lightweight-specific identifiers
- **Pre-1994 era**: LEATHERWARE creed wordmark
- **Post-1994 era**: letter-digit-letter style code (e.g.,
  `B6P-4115` ambiguous between 1996 and 2006)
- **Top zipper closure** — not turnlock, not magnetic snap
- **Thinner leather** than Classic Collection bags of similar
  dimensions (Lightweight processing reduces leather thickness)

## Designer

In-house Coach team, post-Cashin (Cashin's tenure 1962-1974 ended
nearly two decades before Whitney's launch). Designer attribution
`confirmed` as in-house.
