## Lead

The Nicholas Bucket Drawstring (style 9946) is a Coach Classic
NYC-era bucket-style crossbody documented in a single Tier 3 dealer
compilation [yourgreatfinds-collectors-guide]. Confidence is
`tentative` until a dated catalog scan or a second independent
source corroborates the style number and silhouette.

## Description

Per [yourgreatfinds-collectors-guide], the Nicholas is a bucket-
shape bag with a drawstring closure and crossbody strap. It belongs
to the Coach bucket family, which includes other documented Classic
NYC-era entries such as the Equestrian Compartment Bucket (9809),
the Bradley Bucket Crossbody (9936), and the
Drawstring Backpack (9994) [yourgreatfinds-collectors-guide]. The
combination of drawstring closure plus crossbody strap is the
canonical bucket-style configuration; specific dimensions, color
list, and introduction year for the Nicholas are unconfirmed.

## Authentication

The era-keyed authentication matrix in `authentication/` covers the
full set of diagnostics. Bucket-specific notes for the Nicholas:

- **Style number 9946.** Visible on the leather creed on numbered-
  creed examples [yourgreatfinds-collectors-guide]. A style number
  alone does not date a specific bag — Coach reused style numbers
  across multiple production years.
- **Closure.** Drawstring through grommets at the top opening, with
  the closing strap typically tipped in solid brass or knotted
  leather. The drawstring detail is the silhouette's key visual
  identifier [yourgreatfinds-collectors-guide].
- **Creed wording (E3 diagnostic).** A pre-1994 Nicholas should
  read "COACH LEATHERWARE" with "LEATHERWARE" stamped in small
  letters beneath "COACH" [coachbagrehab-dating, vintagecoachie].
- **Serial format.** Pre-1994 examples carry a digit-only unique
  serial (`xxx-xxxx` 1970s, `xxxx-xxx` 1980s) per
  [coachbagrehab-dating] — `tentative`. Any post-1994 production
  carrying 9946 forward (E4) would use the
  `[A-M][year-digit][factory-letter]-9946` pattern with the
  documented single-digit-year ambiguity.
- **Hardware.** Solid brass, non-magnetic. A magnet that sticks
  indicates plated steel and is a strong reproduction signal
  [coachbagrehab-dating].
