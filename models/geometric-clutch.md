## Lead

The Geometric Convertible Clutch (style 9044) is a Coach Classic
NYC-era clutch with a detachable strap, documented in a single
Tier 3 dealer compilation [yourgreatfinds-collectors-guide] with
explicit dimensions and a recorded original-hangtag observation.
Confidence is `tentative` until a dated catalog scan or a second
independent source corroborates the style number and dimensions.

## Description

Per [yourgreatfinds-collectors-guide], the Geometric Convertible
Clutch is a black full-grain leather clutch with brass hardware,
measuring 10.25" × 5.25" × 2", with a detachable strap with a 20"
drop. The same source notes an example surviving with its original
hangtag attached — an unusual surviving feature that supports
unworn or near-unworn provenance for that specific example, but
does not by itself date the silhouette.

The "convertible" descriptor refers to the detachable strap: clutch-
in-hand when the strap is removed, shoulder/crossbody when attached.
This convertible-clutch silhouette also appears in the Cashin-era
list as a separate "Convertible Clutch" entry [yourgreatfinds-collectors-guide],
and the relationship between that earlier clutch and the
Geometric 9044 — successor, variant, or unrelated reuse — is
unconfirmed.

## Authentication

The era-keyed authentication matrix in `authentication/` covers the
full set of diagnostics. Geometric-specific notes:

- **Style number 9044.** Visible on the leather creed on numbered-
  creed examples [yourgreatfinds-collectors-guide]. A style number
  alone does not date a specific bag.
- **Documented dimensions.** 10.25" × 5.25" × 2" body, detachable
  strap with a 20" drop [yourgreatfinds-collectors-guide]. A
  candidate that diverges materially from these dimensions warrants
  scrutiny — though Coach did produce minor sizing variants across
  runs, so small deviations are not by themselves disqualifying.
- **Leather.** Black full-grain glove-tanned cowhide
  [yourgreatfinds-collectors-guide]; raw or burnished edges, not
  painted, on a pre-1994 example [coachbagrehab-dating].
- **Original hangtag.** [yourgreatfinds-collectors-guide] notes one
  documented example surviving with its original hangtag. Hangtag
  presence supports near-unworn provenance for that specific bag
  but is not a per-model authentication requirement — many
  authentic Coach clutches lost or had their hangtags removed in
  ordinary use.
- **Creed wording (E3 diagnostic).** A pre-1994 Geometric should
  read "COACH LEATHERWARE" with "LEATHERWARE" stamped in small
  letters beneath "COACH" [coachbagrehab-dating, vintagecoachie].
- **Serial format.** Pre-1994 examples carry a digit-only unique
  serial; post-1994 examples use the
  `[A-M][year-digit][factory-letter]-9044` pattern with the
  documented single-digit-year ambiguity [coachbagrehab-dating]
  (`tentative`).
- **Hardware.** Solid brass, non-magnetic
  [yourgreatfinds-collectors-guide, coachbagrehab-dating].
