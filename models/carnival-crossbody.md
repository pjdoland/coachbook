## Lead

The Carnival Crossbody (style 9925) is a Coach Classic NYC-era
model documented in a single Tier 3 dealer compilation
[yourgreatfinds-collectors-guide]. The model name indicates a
cross-body configuration. Confidence is `tentative` until a dated
catalog scan or a second independent source corroborates the style
number and silhouette.

## Description

The Carnival Crossbody is recorded as a cross-body configuration —
worn over the shoulder and across the body on a long strap
[yourgreatfinds-collectors-guide]. Beyond the name, style number
9925, and the cross-body wearing arrangement, the surviving public
record is sparse. Dimensions, available colors, closure type, and
introduction year are unconfirmed. No images, catalog appearances,
or dimensional data have been located in free public sources to
date.

## Authentication

A pre-1994 Carnival Crossbody should carry the diagnostics common
to the Classic NYC era (cross-reference `authentication/` for the
full era-keyed matrix):

- **Style number 9925.** Visible on the leather creed inside the bag
  on numbered-creed examples [yourgreatfinds-collectors-guide]. A
  style number alone does not date a specific bag — Coach reused
  style numbers across multiple production years.
- **Creed wording (E3 diagnostic).** A pre-1994 Carnival Crossbody
  should read "COACH LEATHERWARE" with "LEATHERWARE" stamped in
  small letters beneath "COACH," typically with "Made in United
  States" or "Made in New York City, U.S.A." on earlier examples
  [coachbagrehab-dating, vintagecoachie].
- **Serial format.** Pre-1994 examples carry a digit-only unique
  serial (`xxx-xxxx` 1970s, `xxxx-xxx` 1980s) per
  [coachbagrehab-dating] — `tentative`. Any post-1994 production
  carrying 9925 forward (E4) would use the
  `[A-M][year-digit][factory-letter]-9925` pattern with the
  documented single-digit-year ambiguity.
- **Hardware.** Solid brass, non-magnetic. A magnet that sticks
  indicates plated steel and is a strong reproduction signal
  [coachbagrehab-dating].
- **Strap.** A long cross-body strap is part of the model's
  identification; a 9925-creed bag without provision for cross-body
  wear is suspect.
