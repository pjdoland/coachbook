## Lead

The Nottingham is a Coach Classic NYC-era crossbody bag in
binocular-form silhouette, documented in black full-grain leather
with brass hardware in a single Tier 3 dealer compilation
[yourgreatfinds-collectors-guide]. A leather hangtag is noted on
documented examples but is not always present. Confidence is
`tentative` until a dated catalog scan or second independent source
corroborates the model.

## Description

Per [yourgreatfinds-collectors-guide], the Nottingham is a black
full-grain leather crossbody with brass hardware in a binocular-form
silhouette — a deeper, rounded body proportioned roughly like an
optical-binocular case rather than a flat envelope or rectangular
satchel. A leather hangtag was noted on the documented example, but
hangtags are easily lost in three to four decades of use; their
absence on an otherwise consistent bag is not by itself a fake tell.
Dimensions, strap drop, style number, and introduction year are all
unconfirmed.

## Authentication

A pre-1994 Nottingham should carry the diagnostics common to the
Classic NYC era (cross-reference `authentication/` for the full
era-keyed matrix):

- **Creed wording (E3 diagnostic).** A pre-1994 example should read
  "COACH LEATHERWARE" with "LEATHERWARE" stamped in small letters
  beneath "COACH," typically with "Made in United States" or
  "Made in New York City, U.S.A." on earlier examples
  [coachbagrehab-dating, vintagecoachie].
- **Serial format.** Pre-1994 examples carry a digit-only unique
  serial (`xxx-xxxx` 1970s, `xxxx-xxx` 1980s) per
  [coachbagrehab-dating] — `tentative`. Any post-1994 production
  would use the `[A-M][year-digit][factory-letter]-[style]` pattern
  with the documented single-digit-year ambiguity.
- **Leather.** Full-grain glove-tanned cowhide is the NYC-era
  standard; edges raw or burnished, not painted. Painted glossy
  edges on a bag claimed pre-1995 are a strong fake or reproduction
  signal (`research/E-authentication.md` §7).
- **Hardware.** Solid brass, non-magnetic. A magnet that sticks
  indicates plated steel and is a strong reproduction signal
  [coachbagrehab-dating].
- **Hangtag.** A leather hangtag was present on the documented
  example [yourgreatfinds-collectors-guide]. Authentic hangtags are
  thick glove-tanned leather, riveted or looped through a brass
  ring; absence does not by itself indicate inauthenticity, since
  hangtags are commonly lost.
