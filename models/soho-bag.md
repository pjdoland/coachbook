# Soho Bag (Style 4082)

## Lead

The Soho Bag (Style 4082) is the largest Coach bag of the early-1990s
Lightweight Collection — a 17¼"×14½"×8½" softly-structured tote
with hidden magnetic snap closure and adjustable double shoulder
strap. The Holiday 1991 catalog explicitly calls it "**Largest Coach
bag**" of that era [coach-catalogs-1960-1989]. Production ran from
1991 through 1997 per coacharchive.com [coacharchive-com].

## History

The Soho Bag launched as part of Coach's Lightweight Collection by
Holiday 1991 (primary-confirmed via the Holiday 1991 catalog page 22).
Coach's brand-published Vintage Soho Bag re-release page
[coach-vintage-soho] cites a 1994 introduction date — this likely
refers to a specific reissue model (CU909) within Coach's modern
Coach Originals line, not the original 1991 Soho Bag 4082.

By 1993, Soho was a substantial sub-line within the Classic
Collection — the Fall 1993 catalog Table of Contents lists "Soho
Series" at pages 50-53. The Spring 1994 catalog also lists "Soho
Series" at pages 60-65. By 1998 the line had expanded to include
"Soho Small" sub-styles: Soho Small Zip (4104), Soho Small Satchel
(4106), Soho Small Basket (4107), Soho Small Flap (4108).

## Description

Per Holiday 1991 catalog (primary):
- Style number: **4082**
- Price (1991): **$228**
- Dimensions: **17¼" × 14½" × 8½"**
- Closure: **Hidden magnetic snap closure**
- Strap: Adjustable double shoulder strap, each strap with single buckle
- Inside zipper pocket
- Material: thinned glove-tanned cowhide (Lightweight Collection process)

## Colors

Documented Holiday 1991 colors for Soho Bag:
- Berry, Black, Chamois, Cocoa, Ivy

The Lightweight Collection palette was distinct from the Classic
Collection palette. The Soho Bag did NOT come in British Tan,
Mahogany, Burgundy, or other Classic-Collection-standard colors at
launch — it was specifically positioned for the Lightweight palette
of warm earth tones plus Berry as a vibrant accent.

## Authentication

For a Soho Bag claiming 1991-1997 production:
- **Style 4082** in creed [coach-catalogs-1960-1989]
- **Hidden magnetic snap closure** — not turnlock, not zip, not springlock
- **17¼"×14½"×8½"** silhouette — substantially larger than most
  Classic Collection bags
- **Lightweight Collection palette colors** — Berry / Black /
  Chamois / Cocoa / Ivy (or later Lightweight palette additions)
- **Pre-1994 era**: should bear the LEATHERWARE creed wordmark
  (1991-1993 production)
- **Post-1994 era**: letter-digit-letter style code (e.g.,
  `B6P-4082` would be Feb of either 1996 or 2006 — single-digit
  year ambiguity; cross-check with creed wording per
  [authentication/serial-numbers.md](../authentication/serial-numbers.md))

A bag claiming to be a Soho 4082 with a creed reading just "COACH"
(no LEATHERWARE) is post-1994. A bag claiming Soho 4082 with a 1991
era LEATHERWARE creed is consistent with the launch year.

## Designer

In-house. NOT a Bonnie Cashin design — Cashin's contract ended 1974;
Soho launched 1991. Designer attribution is `confirmed` to in-house
team operating during the Sara Lee era (Frankfort president, Krakoff
not yet at Coach until December 1996).

## Variants and related models

The Soho Series expanded over its 1991-1997 run:
- **Soho Bag 4082** (the original, 1991)
- **Soho Small Zip 4104** (1998)
- **Soho Small Satchel 4106** (1998)
- **Soho Small Basket 4107** (1998)
- **Soho Small Flap 4108** (1998)

Plus other named pieces in the Soho Series across 1993-1997 (per
Fall 1993 catalog TOC) — full roster pending additional catalog
deep-readings.
