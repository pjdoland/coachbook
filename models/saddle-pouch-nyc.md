## Lead

**The Saddle Pouch (Style 9585) and its variant (Style 9590) are
Coach silhouettes in continuous catalog production from at least
Fall 1981 onward** [coach-catalogs-1960-1989] per research streams
N1 (1981-1982), N2 (1983), N3 (1984), M1 (1985), and M2 (1989).
A small-size companion (**Style 9595, "Saddle Pouch Small"**) was
in catalog through Spring/Summer 1982 but was **discontinued
between Spring/Summer 1982 and Fall 1982** — the first documented
silhouette discontinuation in the deep-read corpus.

Coach's own brand-voice copy describes "**authentic solid brass
saddlery hardware**" on the Saddle Pouch (per Spring 1983
brand-voice quote, research stream N2) — a deliberate reference
to the saddlery-leathergoods heritage that Coach explicitly claims
across the 1981-1989 catalog corpus.

## Description

A NYC-made pouch-style bag with a saddle silhouette, listed among
Classic NYC-era styles in [yourgreatfinds-collectors-guide]. The
"NYC" qualifier in the name reflects that examples documented to
date were produced in the New York City factory, placing the bag
within the pre-c.1980 NYC production window. Style number,
dimensions, color range, and exact introduction year are
unconfirmed in free public sources.

## Authentication

A NYC-made Saddle Pouch should carry the diagnostics common to the
Classic NYC era (cross-reference `authentication/` for the full
era-keyed matrix):

- **Creed wording (E3 diagnostic).** Should read "COACH LEATHERWARE"
  with "LEATHERWARE" in small letters beneath "COACH," typically
  with "Made in New York City, U.S.A." on NYC-factory examples
  [coachbagrehab-dating, vintagecoachie].
- **Serial format.** Pre-1994 examples carry a digit-only unique
  serial (`xxx-xxxx` 1970s, `xxxx-xxx` 1980s) per
  [coachbagrehab-dating] — `tentative`.
- **Hardware.** Solid brass, non-magnetic. A magnet that sticks
  indicates plated steel and is a strong reproduction signal
  [coachbagrehab-dating].
- **Construction.** Glove-tanned cowhide with raw or burnished edges,
  not painted [coachbagrehab-dating].
