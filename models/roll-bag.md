A 1970s NYC-made roll-style bag, with a black leather example

## Authentication

A 1970s NYC-era Roll Bag should carry the diagnostics common to the
Classic NYC era (cross-reference `authentication/` for the full
era-keyed matrix):

- **Creed wording (E3 diagnostic).** A 1970s Roll Bag should read
  "COACH LEATHERWARE" with "LEATHERWARE" in small letters beneath
  "COACH," with "Made in New York City, U.S.A." on earlier examples
  [coachbagrehab-dating, vintagecoachie].
- **Serial format.** 1970s examples carry a digit-only unique serial
  in the `xxx-xxxx` format per [coachbagrehab-dating] — `tentative`.
  Some early examples may predate creed serialization entirely; an
  absent serial is not by itself a fake tell.
- **Hardware.** Solid brass, non-magnetic. A magnet that sticks
  indicates plated steel and is a strong reproduction signal
  [coachbagrehab-dating].
- **Construction.** Glove-tanned cowhide with raw or burnished edges,
  not painted [coachbagrehab-dating].
