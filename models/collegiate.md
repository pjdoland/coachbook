# Collegiate

## Lead

The **Collegiate** (Style 9815) is a Coach Classic Collection
shoulder bag introduced in **Summer 1989** at $118
[coach-catalogs-1960-1989]. A compact crossbody with a full 3" depth,
brass turnlock, and three full-length pockets — positioned by Coach
as a work-and-weekend silhouette. Documented in the inaugural
ten-color Classics palette that included the newly-introduced
Sandstone color.

## Description (Summer 1989 catalog primary)

> "For work as well as for casual weekends, this compact bag has a
> full 3" width so there is plenty of room for bulky items. There
> are three full-length pockets: one on the outside back, another
> under the flap, and a third inside. Comes with a solid brass
> turnlock closure and adjustable 44" shoulder strap. Dimensions:
> 8¼"×6"×3". Black, Bone, British Tan, Burgundy, Mahogany, Navy,
> Putty, Red, Sandstone, Tabac shown on page two. Pictured in Bone."
> [coach-catalogs-1960-1989]

## History

- **Introduced**: Summer 1989. Style 9815, primary-confirmed in
  Summer Preview 1989 catalog [coach-catalogs-1960-1989]. Confidence:
  `confirmed`.
- **Continuity**: Collegiate carries forward into the 1991 catalogs
  as a recurring Classic Collection bag (per M2 research stream
  notes), suggesting an extended production run rather than a
  short-season introduction.

## Designer

In-house Coach team. NOT Cashin (her contract ended in 1974,
fifteen years before the Collegiate launched). Designer attribution
`confirmed` to Coach's pre-Krakoff (Krakoff joined December 1996)
in-house team.

## Authentication

- **Style 9815** in creed [coach-catalogs-1960-1989]
- **Pre-1994 era**: LEATHERWARE creed wordmark expected
- **Solid brass turnlock**, non-magnetic
- **44" shoulder strap** — fixed-length adjustable
- **Glove-tanned cowhide** with raw or burnished edges
- **Sandstone color** is a Summer 1989+ palette indicator; a Sandstone
