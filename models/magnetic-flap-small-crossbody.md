## Lead

The Magnetic Flap Small Crossbody (style 9145) is a Coach Classic
NYC-era small crossbody distinguished by a magnetic flap closure —
an unusual choice for vintage Coach, where the brass turnlock
dominates the era [yourgreatfinds-collectors-guide]. It is
documented in a single Tier 3 dealer compilation with no further
detail; confidence is `tentative` until a dated catalog scan or
second independent source corroborates the model.

## Description

Beyond the model description "Magnetic Flap Small Crossbody," the
style number 9145, and the small-crossbody form, the surviving
public record is sparse [yourgreatfinds-collectors-guide].
Dimensions, available colors, lining, and introduction year are all
unconfirmed.

The closure type is the most distinguishing detail. Vintage Coach
overwhelmingly used solid brass turnlocks (Cashin's 1964
introduction, per `vintagefashionguild-cashin`) and, secondarily,
zip and snap closures; magnetic flap closures appear rarely on
NYC-era bags. A magnetic-flap small crossbody therefore stands out
as an atypical specification for the era and is itself a useful
identification cue.

## Authentication

A pre-1994 Magnetic Flap Small Crossbody should carry the
diagnostics common to the Classic NYC era (cross-reference
`authentication/` for the full era-keyed matrix):

- **Style number 9145.** Visible on the leather creed inside the
  bag on numbered-creed examples [yourgreatfinds-collectors-guide].
  A style number alone does not date a specific bag — Coach reused
  style numbers across multiple production years.
- **Creed wording (E3 diagnostic).** A pre-1994 example should read
  "COACH LEATHERWARE" with "LEATHERWARE" stamped in small letters
  beneath "COACH," typically with "Made in United States" or
  "Made in New York City, U.S.A." on earlier examples
  [coachbagrehab-dating, vintagecoachie].
- **Serial format.** Pre-1994 examples carry a digit-only unique
  serial (`xxx-xxxx` 1970s, `xxxx-xxx` 1980s) per
  [coachbagrehab-dating] — `tentative`. Post-1994 production
  carrying 9145 forward (E4) would use the
  `[A-M][year-digit][factory-letter]-9145` pattern.
- **Hardware.** Solid brass on the strap fittings and any visible
  metal, non-magnetic [coachbagrehab-dating]. The magnetic
  closure is the exception, located inside the flap and not visible
  externally — a magnet that sticks to the flap interior is
  expected; a magnet that sticks to the strap rings is a
  reproduction signal.
- **Closure as identification cue.** A flap closure that holds
  shut by magnet rather than turnlock or snap is itself a useful
  identification cue, given how unusual it is for the era.
