## Authentication

A pre-1994 Bixby Sling should carry the diagnostics common to the
Classic NYC era (cross-reference `authentication/` for the full
era-keyed matrix):

- **Creed wording (E3 diagnostic).** A pre-1994 Bixby Sling should
  read "COACH LEATHERWARE" with "LEATHERWARE" stamped in small
  letters beneath "COACH," typically with "Made in United States"
  or "Made in New York City, U.S.A." on earlier examples
  [coachbagrehab-dating, vintagecoachie].
- **Serial format.** Pre-1994 examples carry a digit-only unique
  serial (`xxx-xxxx` 1970s, `xxxx-xxx` 1980s) per
  [coachbagrehab-dating] — `tentative`. Any post-1994 (E4)
  production would use the
  `[A-M][year-digit][factory-letter]-[style-number]` pattern with
  the documented single-digit-year ambiguity.
- **Hardware.** Solid brass, non-magnetic. Sling strap hardware
  (D-rings, swivel clips) on a Classic NYC-era example should
  also test non-magnetic; magnetic plated-steel hardware is a
  strong reproduction signal [coachbagrehab-dating].
