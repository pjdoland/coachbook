Distinguishing construction features:

- **Magnetic-snap-style closures** (not turnlock; not zipper-only)

## Era and designer

**Era E5 (Krakoff transition).** Designer is in-house Krakoff-era
team. The Holiday 1999 catalog is the first after Krakoff's
September 1999 promotion to President + Executive Creative
Director, but per the 21-year silent-creative-director pattern,
Krakoff is not credited in any 1999 catalog body copy.
