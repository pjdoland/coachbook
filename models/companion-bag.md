# Companion Bag (9300)

## Lead

**The Companion Bag (Style 9300) is a Coach Classic Collection
silhouette in continuous catalog production from at least Fall 1981
onward** [coach-catalogs-1960-1989] per research streams N1
(1981-1982), N2 (1983), N3 (1984), M1 (1985), and M2 (1989). The
9300 number predates the 1981 Fall corpus, with the silhouette
likely introduced earlier in the late-1970s or early-1980s.

## Disambiguation

The Companion Bag (9300) and the **Companion Flap (9076)** are
distinct silhouettes despite sharing the "Companion" naming root.
See [models/companion-flap.md](companion-flap.md) for the 9076 entry
(Tier 3 only, specifics unconfirmed).

## Authentication

For a vintage Companion Bag (9300):
- **Style 9300** in creed [coach-catalogs-1960-1989]
- **Pre-1994 era**: LEATHERWARE creed wordmark expected
- **Solid brass hardware**, non-magnetic
- **Glove-tanned cowhide** with raw or burnished edges
- **All 10 Classics colors** documented in Spring 1989 — among the
  most palette-flexible models of the era

## Designer

In-house Coach team. The 9300 is in catalog from Fall 1981 onward,
~7 years post-Cashin (her contract ended 1974). Cashin authorship
of the silhouette is plausible (introduction predates the corpus,
could be any year from 1962 onward) but unconfirmed. **Cashin is
NOT named in any 1981-1984 Coach catalog** (per N4) despite the
Companion appearing in all of them — the silhouette is in catalog
without designer attribution.
