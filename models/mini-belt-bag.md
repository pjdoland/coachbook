## Description

A small belt-mounted bag with capacity for a bundle of keys and
roughly one credit card, listed in [yourgreatfinds-collectors-guide]
as a recently trending style on the secondhand market. Dimensions,
color range, style number, and exact introduction year are
unconfirmed in free public sources. Small accessories of this type
often shipped without serial numbers throughout Coach's vintage
production [coachbagrehab-dating].

## Authentication

A Classic NYC-era Mini Belt Bag should carry the diagnostics common
to the Classic NYC era (cross-reference `authentication/` for the
full era-keyed matrix), with allowances for small-leather-good
conventions:

- **Creed wording (E3 diagnostic).** A pre-1994 example should read
  "COACH LEATHERWARE" with "LEATHERWARE" in small letters beneath
  "COACH" [coachbagrehab-dating, vintagecoachie]. Some small
  accessories carry only a leather stamp without a numbered creed.
- **Serial format.** Small accessories (key fobs, coin purses, mini
  bags) often shipped without serials throughout Coach production
  per [coachbagrehab-dating]; absence of a serial alone is not a
  fake tell on this category.
- **Hardware.** Solid brass, non-magnetic. A magnet that sticks
  indicates plated steel and is a strong reproduction signal
  [coachbagrehab-dating].
- **Construction.** Glove-tanned cowhide with raw or burnished edges,
  not painted [coachbagrehab-dating].
