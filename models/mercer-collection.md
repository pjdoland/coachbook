# Mercer Collection

## Lead — primary-confirmed Fall 1999 launch

**The Mercer Collection launched in the Fall 1999 catalog** as a
twill-material sub-collection [coach-catalogs-1960-1989] per
research stream Q1b. Cover bag: **Slim Shoulder Tote 7414 in jet
($198)**. Eleven SKUs documented at launch: 7400, 7402, 7404, 7406,
7407, 7412, 7413, 7414, plus J676 Mercer Boot. The unbroken
numbering 7400-7414 strongly suggests 7401/7403/7405/7408-7411 are
on missing pages of the partial scan.

By 2001 Fall (per Q2 partial), Mercer had shifted to a
briefcase-leaning sub-collection at TOC pp 20-23 with 5117 Mercer
Computer Tote and Mercer-branded jewelry W100/W098.

By 2001 Holiday Preview (per Q2): Mercer at TOC p 10 in twill
(Jet/Slate/Teak) with 7400 Satchel, 7402 Hobo, 7404 Backpack, 7406
Zip, 7407 Demi Zip, 7412 Pocket Zip, 7413 East/West Flap.

## Sunset: Autumn 2002

Per Q2 follow-up: **Mercer handbag line GONE by Autumn 2002.** The
"Mercer" name survives only as eyewear (Mercer Sunglasses S311).
Total handbag run: ~3 years Fall 1999 → 2001 Holiday Preview.

## Era and designer

**Era E5 (Krakoff transition).** Designer is in-house Krakoff-era
team. The 21-year silent-creative-director pattern (per N4 + Q1 +
Q2 cross-cut) means Krakoff is never explicitly credited in any
catalog body copy through Autumn 2002 despite his September 1999
promotion to President + Executive Creative Director.

NOT Cashin (1974 cutoff). NOT a heritage Coach silhouette — Mercer
was a Krakoff-era twill collection from launch.

## Authentication

For a vintage Mercer Collection bag (Fall 1999 — 2001):

- **7400-series style number** in creed [coach-catalogs-1960-1989]
- **Post-1994 creed format**: letter-digit-letter serial
- **Post-1994 country of origin**: offshore (Costa Rica / Hungary /
  Turkey / Dominican Republic / Mexico)
- **Twill material** — specifically Mercer's signature twill
  weave; not glove-tanned cowhide
