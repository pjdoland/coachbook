## Lead

The Companion Flap (style 9076) is a Coach Classic NYC-era model
documented in a single Tier 3 dealer compilation
[yourgreatfinds-collectors-guide]. The name suggests a flap-closure
silhouette, but no images, dimensions, or dated catalog appearances
have been located in free public sources. Confidence is `tentative`
until a second independent source corroborates the style number and
silhouette.

## Disambiguation: Companion Flap (9076) vs Companion Bag (9300)

These are **two distinct silhouettes** that share the "Companion"
naming root:

- **Companion Flap (9076)** — this entry. Tier 3 only; specifics
  unconfirmed.
- **Companion Bag (9300)** — primary-confirmed in 1981-1989 catalogs
  [coach-catalogs-1960-1989], in continuous catalog production from
  Fall 1981 onward at $128 in Spring 1989, dimensions 11"×7"×2¾",
  available in all 10 Classics colors. Per research streams N1-N4
  and M2.

A separate model entry for **Companion Bag (9300)** is pending. The
two should not be conflated — different style numbers, likely
different silhouettes.

## Authentication

A pre-1994 Companion Flap should carry the diagnostics common to the
Classic NYC era (cross-reference `authentication/` for the full
era-keyed matrix):

- **Style number 9076.** Visible on the leather creed inside the bag
  on numbered-creed examples [yourgreatfinds-collectors-guide]. A
  style number alone does not date a specific bag — Coach reused
  style numbers across multiple production years.
- **Creed wording (E3 diagnostic).** A pre-1994 example should read
  "COACH LEATHERWARE" with "LEATHERWARE" stamped in small letters
  beneath "COACH," typically with "Made in United States" or
  "Made in New York City, U.S.A." on earlier examples
  [coachbagrehab-dating, vintagecoachie].
- **Serial format.** Pre-1994 examples carry a digit-only unique
  serial (`xxx-xxxx` 1970s, `xxxx-xxx` 1980s) per
  [coachbagrehab-dating] — `tentative`. Any post-1994 production
  carrying 9076 forward (E4) would use the
  `[A-M][year-digit][factory-letter]-9076` pattern with the
  documented single-digit-year ambiguity.
- **Hardware.** Solid brass, non-magnetic. A magnet that sticks
  indicates plated steel and is a strong reproduction signal
  [coachbagrehab-dating].
