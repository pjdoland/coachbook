# Sheridan Stewart Bag

**Dispute resolved 2026-05-02**. Primary catalog evidence settles
both prior framings:

- The **Sheridan Collection LINE** launched **Fall 1992** (Fall
  Special Edition 1992 catalog cover: "New Sheridan Styles
  pages 2-9") [coach-catalogs-1960-1989]. FundingUniverse's
  "early 1992" was approximately right.
- The **Stewart Bag (Style 4231)** was NOT in the Fall 1992
  launch roster of 12 styles. It first appears in the Fall 1993
  An American Legacy catalog at $224 [coach-catalogs-1960-1989].
  Coach.com's "first introduced in 1993" framing for the
  Stewart variant is correct.
- Both prior sources were right — they were dating different
  things (collection vs. variant). Original disputed framing
  is no longer accurate.

## Original framing for context

The Sheridan Stewart Bag is a Coach silhouette in the Sheridan
collection, a late-NYC-era line introduced in the early 1990s. Coach.com's
current Vintage Sheridan Stewart Bag re-release page (SKU CDE04) states
the model was "first introduced in 1993" [coach-vintage-sheridan-stewart].
The Sheridan collection's introduction year was previously `disputed`: 1992 per
FundingUniverse [fundinguniverse-coach] versus 1993 per Coach.com
[coach-vintage-sheridan-stewart].

## The 1992 vs 1993 dispute

The Sheridan introduction date does not resolve to a single year on the
free public record. Both readings are documented and both are tagged
here so neither is silently dropped.

- **1992 (FundingUniverse)**: the trade-press corporate history
  republished by FundingUniverse — sourced from the *International
  Directory of Company Histories* — places the launch in **early 1992**:
  "Sheridan and Camden collections introduced" alongside the worldwide
  store count of 53 [fundinguniverse-coach]. Tier 2.
- **1993 (Coach.com)**: the brand's current Vintage Sheridan Stewart
  Bag product page (CDE04) states the bag was "first introduced in
  1993" [coach-vintage-sheridan-stewart]. Tier 1, brand-published.

A plausible reconciliation: the **Sheridan collection** launched in
1992 (FundingUniverse), and the **Stewart variant** within that
collection was introduced in 1993 (Coach.com's date applies to the
specific silhouette, not the collection). Under this reading both
sources are consistent and the discrepancy is one of granularity, not
fact. The reading remains `disputed` until either a 1992 catalog page
showing the Stewart variant or a 1993 catalog page showing it as a new
introduction is matched.

The Tier 1 brand-published date (1993) outranks the Tier 2 trade-press
date (1992) on tier alone, but the Tier 2 source covers the
*collection*, not the *variant*, so this is not a straightforward
override. Both citations stand.

## The Sheridan collection

Sheridan was one of two named collections introduced together in early
1992 per FundingUniverse — the **Sheridan** and **Camden** collections
were launched at the same moment in the corporate timeline, with a
worldwide store count of 53 [fundinguniverse-coach]. Both collections
sit in the late Classic NYC era (E4), one to two years before the
**Manhattan** collection (Spring 1993, "first major use of bright
colors" per FundingUniverse [fundinguniverse-coach]) and roughly two
years before the **Soho** collection (1994 per Coach.com [models/soho.md,
coach-vintage-soho]).

This places the Sheridan collection in the brief window between Coach's
expansion-phase NYC identity and the late-1994 flagship-opening pivot
that introduced Soho — the last two years where named collections
launched under fully NYC-era design language before the Krakoff
transition began.

## Authentication

What to check on a vintage Sheridan Stewart specifically:

- **Creed wording, late E4.** A 1992–1993 Sheridan should carry the
  pre-1994 "COACH LEATHERWARE" creed, with the small-caps
  **LEATHERWARE** wordmark beneath "COACH"
  [research/E-authentication.md]. Presence of LEATHERWARE is the
  single strongest textual anchor for pre-1994 manufacture; an early
  Sheridan with a creed reading "COACH" alone (no LEATHERWARE) would be
  anomalous and is suspect.
- **Serial format.** Pre-1994 creeds carry a digit-only `xxxx-xxx`
  unique serial; the post-1994 letter-digit-letter-dash-digits format
  begins in 1994 [research/E-authentication.md §2]. A 1992–1993 vintage
  Sheridan Stewart should carry the digit-only format.
- **Made in U.S.A.** A 1992–1993 Sheridan should be U.S.-made; Costa
  Rican production began ~1994 [research/E-authentication.md §3]. A
  "Made in Costa Rica" Sheridan claiming pre-1994 manufacture is a
  red flag.
- **Solid brass hardware.** Non-magnetic. Magnetic hardware on a bag
  claiming early-1990s manufacture is a strong fake or
  post-vintage-plated-steel signal [research/E-authentication.md §4].
- **Edge treatment.** Glove-tanned cowhide with raw or burnished edges.
  Painted glossy edges on a "1990s" Sheridan are a strong fake or
  reproduction signal — Coach NYC-era construction does not paint
  edges [research/E-authentication.md, PLAN §6].
- **Designer attribution.** A vintage Sheridan Stewart is not a Cashin
  design. Cashin's tenure ended in 1974 [research/C-cashin.md]; the
  Sheridan collection launched 1992 or 1993. Any listing or article
  attributing the model to Cashin is wrong on its face.

## Modern re-release

Coach currently sells a "Vintage Sheridan Stewart Bag" (SKU **CDE04**)
on coach.com [coach-vintage-sheridan-stewart]. This is the source of
the brand-published 1993 introduction date that anchors this entry.

A re-released Sheridan Stewart is in current production and is not a
vintage Sheridan Stewart. Modern re-releases will carry post-2014
sewn-in country-of-origin tags rather than creed serials
[research/E-authentication.md §6, E8] and will not match pre-1994 creed
wording. Treat CDE04 as its own catalog entry — useful as a
brand-published date citation, not as evidence about original 1992–1993
production.
