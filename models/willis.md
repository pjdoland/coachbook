# Willis

Willis is the small flap-front shoulder bag that came to define Coach's
Classic NYC silhouette — rectangular, structured, glove-tanned, made to
hold its shape for decades. Style **9927** is one of the most
consistently documented numbers in the vintage Coach canon; Willis
examples turn up across every NYC-era color and every post-1974
hardware generation.

## Description

Willis is a small structured flap bag in glove-tanned cowhide, roughly
9" wide × 7" tall × 3.5" deep (deep dimension `tentative` — needs
catalog or measured-bag confirmation). Closure is a solid brass turnlock
on the front flap. The strap is removable and length-adjustable via a
buckle, attached at each end with a brass trigger-snap dogleash clip
that hooks to D-rings at the bag's top corners. Hardware is solid
brass throughout: turnlock, D-rings, dogleash clips, and strap buckle.
The body is unlined or minimally lined depending on year — an unlined
Willis is correct for the NYC era and is not a fake tell (E1, E6).

The silhouette is what defines a Willis: rectangular, flat-bottomed, with
a single full-width front flap that wraps over the top and down to the
turnlock plate. A short rolled top handle sits above the flap on most
examples. Murphy (style 9930) is the close cousin — "looks like Willis
but no handle" per D1.

## History

- **Introduced**: **post-1984**, exact year TBD between 1985 and
  1988. **Earlier framing as "early 1980s" was based on Tier 3
  community sources and is contradicted by primary catalog evidence**:
  Willis 9927 is **absent from all 1981, 1982, 1983, and 1984 Coach
  catalogs** [coach-catalogs-1960-1989] per research streams N1, N2,
  N3. The 9927 number is not in active use in any of those years.
  D1 (`yourgreatfinds-collectors-guide`) places Willis in a "1980s
  NYC group" — directionally correct, but the "early 1980s" specific
  framing is wrong. Updated framing: `tentative` post-1984 launch,
  window narrowed to 1985-1988, exact year still open.
- **Discontinued**: previously framed in archive as 1996 per Tier 3
  community sources. **This is contradicted by primary catalog
  evidence**: Willis 9927 appears in the **1996-1997 Source Book at
  $258** [coach-catalogs-1960-1989], where the Source Book frames it
  as **"Coach's best-selling handbag"** with the brand-voice tag
  **"Willis Bag introduced the use of the dowel as a design motif."**
  Willis was therefore still in active production through 1996-1997.
  The actual discontinuation year is open — needs 1998+ catalog
  deep-read to settle. Confidence: `open`.
- **Status in 1996-1997 Source Book**: "Coach's best-selling handbag"
  (Coach's brand-published primary copy) [coach-catalogs-1960-1989].
  The Willis remained the company's flagship handbag at the start of
  the Krakoff transition; it was not displaced by Ergo (1997) or
  Coach Neo (1998) at the cataloged-flagship level until later in the
  late 1990s.
- **Notability**: Willis sits at the center of the most-cited
  authentication worked example in the public collector literature.
  Coachbagrehab (E1) decodes the post-1994 serial format using
  **`B6D-9927`** as its example — month B (February), year digit 6,
  factory letter D, style 9927 (Willis).

  **Critical ambiguity**: under the single-digit-year scheme, "6"
  resolves equally to **1996** or **2006**. Coachbagrehab reads the
  example as 2006; under a 1996 reading the same code would describe a
  late-production Willis. The decoder cannot stand alone — cross-check
  creed wording (LEATHERWARE present → pre-1994; Willis-era 1996
  examples should still read "Made in United States" or an early
  offshore country, not "Handcrafted in China"), lining material, and
  hardware stamping. See `research/E-authentication.md` §2 for the full
  caveat.

## Authentication

What to check on a Willis specifically:

- **Style number 9927 in the creed.** Pre-1994 creeds carry a unique
  digit-only serial (e.g., `xxxx-xxx`) and the style number is *not*
  in the creed. Post-1994 creeds carry a code of the form
  `[A-M][0-9][A-Z]-9927`; the trailing 9927 is the Willis style number
  and is fixed across years. Style number alone does not date the bag.
- **Pre-1994 vs post-1994 serial format.** A Willis with a digit-only
  serial is pre-1994; a Willis with the letter-digit-letter-dash-digits
  pattern is 1994 or later. Pre-1994 creeds also carry the small-caps
  word "LEATHERWARE" beneath "COACH" — its presence is the single
  strongest textual anchor for pre-1994 manufacture (E1).
- **Brass turnlock.** Solid brass, non-magnetic. A magnet that sticks
  is a strong fake or post-vintage-plated-steel signal. "COACH" stamping
  on the turnlock back appears on later examples; absence on an
  earlier Willis is not a fake tell.
- **Dogleash clip.** Spring-loaded oval clip; "COACH" stamped on the
  body in late E3 onward. Casting seams or uneven oval geometry are
  fake tells (E1).
- **Solid brass hardware overall.** Heavy for size, develops a warm
  honey-amber patina rather than wearing through to a silver
  underlayer.
- **Edge treatment.** Glove-tanned cowhide with **raw or burnished**
  edges. Painted glossy edges on a "1980s" Willis are a strong fake or
  reproduction signal — Coach NYC-era construction does not paint edges
  (E1, PLAN §6).
- **Lining.** Unlined or minimally lined is correct. Heat-cut polyester
  lining on a bag claiming pre-1995 manufacture is a fake tell.
- **Designer attribution.** Willis is **not** a Bonnie Cashin design,
  despite frequent community misattribution. Cashin's tenure ended in
  **1974**; Willis was introduced in the 1980s. Any listing or article
  attributing Willis to Cashin is wrong on its face.

See `authentication/` (forthcoming) for the era-keyed matrix used to
ground these checks.

## Modern re-issue

Yourgreatfinds (D1) notes that Willis has appeared in Coach's recent
"Coach Originals" / Archive re-release line. Coach.com's vintage
re-release pages confirm the company is actively reissuing NYC-era
silhouettes (Soho — D5; Sheridan Stewart — D6; Bleecker — D7), so a
Willis re-release is consistent with the program even where a specific
re-release product page has not yet been pulled into the registry.
Confidence: `tentative` until a Coach.com Willis SKU is captured.

A re-released Willis is not a vintage Willis. Modern re-releases will
carry post-2014 sewn-in country-of-origin tags rather than creed
serials (per E8) and will not match pre-1994 creed wording. Treat them
as their own catalog entry, not as evidence about the original.
