# Compartment Bag (9850 / 9845)

## Lead — primary-confirmed Spring 1989 launch (two sizes)

**The Compartment Bag debuts in the Spring 1989 catalog as TWO new
sizes**: standard **Style 9850** at $130 and small **Style 9845** at
$110 [coach-catalogs-1960-1989] per research stream M2. Both NEW
on the Classics roster. The silhouette receives a Spectator-trim
variant (Style 6850) Fall 1989 in the Spectator Collection launch.

## Description (Spring 1989 catalog primary)

| Spec | 9850 (standard) | 9845 (small) |
|------|-----------------|--------------|
| Spring 1989 price | $130 | $110 |
| Dimensions | 9½" × 7" × 2¼" | 8¼" × 5" × 2¼" |
| Material | Glove-tanned cowhide | Glove-tanned cowhide |
| Colors (Spring 1989) | 9 colors (Black, Bone, British Tan, Burgundy, Mahogany, Navy, Putty, Red, Tabac) | same |
| Spectator variant (Fall 1989) | 6850 with Tabac trim | (none) |

## Era and designer

**Era E4 (Classic NYC late, 1985–1994).** Designer is **in-house
Coach team**. NOT Cashin (her contract ended 1974, 15 years before
the Compartment Bag launched).

The Compartment Bag's 9850/9845 numbers sit in the same Spring
1989 Classics expansion that produced the Classic Hobo (9855).

## Spectator variant 6850 (Fall 1989)

Per Fall 1989 Spectator Collection launch (M2), the **Compartment
Bag Spectator (Style 6850)** at $144 launched as one of 8 Spectator
styles, all originally with **Tabac trim**. Note: only the
standard-size 9850 Compartment Bag received a Spectator variant —
the small 9845 did not get a 6845 Spectator counterpart.

## Authentication

- **Style 9850** or **9845** (Classic) or **6850** (Spectator) in
  creed [coach-catalogs-1960-1989]
- **Pre-1994 era**: LEATHERWARE creed wordmark expected
- **Solid brass hardware**, non-magnetic
- **Glove-tanned cowhide** with raw or burnished edges
- **6850 Spectator variant**: Tabac trim ~Fall 1989 to ~Holiday
  1990 (with Cocoa-trim sub-line as a separate Holiday 1990 sub-
  series, not 6850); British Tan trim Holiday 1991+
