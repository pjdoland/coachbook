# Sheridan Collection

## Lead

The Sheridan Collection is a Coach sub-collection that **launched
Fall 1992** (Fall Special Edition 1992 catalog cover: "New Sheridan
Styles pages 2-9") [coach-catalogs-1960-1989]. Distinguished from
the Classic Collection by its pebble-textured leather, contrasting
bridle leather trim, faille jacquard lining, and Style 4XXX prefix.

## Sunset: Spring 1995 final appearance (~3-year run)

**Update 2026-05-02 (research stream M3).** The Sheridan Collection
**disappears from Coach catalogs after Spring 1995**
[coach-catalogs-1960-1989]:

- **Last appearance**: Spring 1995 (cataloged in main catalog body)
- **Absent from**: 1995 Holiday Preview table of contents and all
  subsequent 1996 catalogs and the 1996-1997 Source Book
- **Total run**: Fall 1992 launch → Spring 1995 sunset (~3 years)

Critically, the Sheridan sunset **precedes Reed Krakoff's December
1996 hire** by ~18 months — so the discontinuation is a **late-NYC era
management decision**, not a Krakoff-era rationalization. Sheridan was
cut while Coach was still operating under fully NYC-era design
language and pre-IPO Sara Lee ownership. This reframes the collection's
arc: the move toward "modern classics" voice (first surfacing Holiday
1996) and the trimming of named sub-collections began before Krakoff.

The collection's legacy lives on in two ways: (1) Coach's modern
"Vintage Sheridan Stewart Bag" re-release (CDE04) preserves the
silhouette in current production, and (2) the 4XXX style-number
convention initiated by Sheridan was inherited by other 1990s
sub-collections (Manhattan Lightweights Spring 1993, etc.).

## Distinctive features (vs Classic Collection)

1. **Style number prefix 4XXX** (vs 9XXX for Classic)
2. **Pebble-textured leather** (vs glove-tanned cowhide)
3. **"Bridle leather trim"** in contrasting Tan
4. **Faille jacquard lining** (vs unlined or simple cotton in Classic)
5. **Domed leather tabs**
6. **Brass turnlock closure** (most styles; consistent with Classic)
7. **"Scratch and stain resistant"** — Coach's positioning for daily use
8. Distinct color palette

## Sheridan palette (primary, Fall 1992)

| Color | Notes |
|-------|-------|
| Black | Standard |
| Bone | Off-white |
| Green | Sheridan-specific, distinct from Forest Green |
| Navy | Standard |
| Tan | Sheridan signature solid color |
| Red | Accent |
| Taupe | NEW for archive |

Most Sheridan bags shipped in two-tone treatments: body color +
Tan trim. Solid Black is offered on most styles as an alternative
to two-tone.

## Authentication

For a Sheridan Collection bag (1992-onward):
- **Style 4XXX number** (or 5268 for Sumter Briefcase) in creed
- **Pebble-textured leather** — not the smooth glove-tanned cowhide
  of Classic Collection
- **Bridle leather trim** in contrasting Tan (most variants;
  exception: solid Black versions)
- **Faille jacquard lining** when bag is opened
- **Domed leather tabs** at hardware attachment points
- **Brass hardware** (magnet test still applies)
