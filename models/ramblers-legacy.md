## Lead

Rambler's Legacy is a Classic NYC-era Coach model identified by style
number 9061. Per `research/D-models.md`, it is one of several bags in
Coach's in-house Legacy series, alongside Patricia's Legacy (style
9951) and Lula's Legacy.

## Description

The bag is recorded in a single Tier 3 dealer compilation
[yourgreatfinds-collectors-guide] with style number 9061 and the
Legacy-series grouping. Silhouette type, dimensions, available colors,
and introduction year are not yet documented in free public sources.
No dated catalog appearance has been located.

## The Legacy series

"Legacy" in the context of Rambler's Legacy, Patricia's Legacy, and
Lula's Legacy is a Coach **in-house naming convention** for a group
of related Classic NYC-era bags — not a designer credit. This matters
because the word "Legacy" is sometimes attached online to Bonnie
Cashin in a way that conflates two different things:

- The Coach **Legacy series** documented here is a Classic NYC-era
  (mid-1970s–1994) in-house product line. Cashin's tenure ended in
  1974, before this era. Any attribution of these specific bags to
  Cashin is folklore (see STYLE.md §4 and `research/D-models.md`).
- Cashin's broader design legacy (lowercase) — the turnlock, side
  toggles, kiss-lock, the Cashin Carry silhouette — is a separate
  matter and is not what the named Legacy series refers to.

Treat any seller listing Rambler's Legacy as "designed by Bonnie
Cashin" as a warning that other claims in that listing should be
checked.

## Authentication

Classic NYC-era diagnostics apply (cross-reference `authentication/`
for the full era-keyed matrix):

- **Style number 9061.** Visible on the leather creed inside the bag
  on numbered-creed examples [yourgreatfinds-collectors-guide]. A
  style number alone does not date a specific bag — Coach reused
  style numbers across multiple production years.
- **Creed wording.** A pre-1994 example should read "COACH
  LEATHERWARE" with "LEATHERWARE" stamped in small letters beneath
  "COACH," typically with "Made in United States" [coachbagrehab-dating,
  vintagecoachie].
- **Serial format.** Pre-1994 examples carry a digit-only unique
  serial (`xxx-xxxx` 1970s, `xxxx-xxx` 1980s) per
  [coachbagrehab-dating] — `tentative`. Post-1994 production (if any
  carried 9061 forward) would use the
  `[A-M][year-digit][factory-letter]-9061` pattern, with the known
  single-digit-year ambiguity.
- **Hardware.** Solid brass, non-magnetic.
- **Construction.** Glove-tanned cowhide with raw or burnished edges.
- **Designer.** Any seller listing Rambler's Legacy as "designed by
  Bonnie Cashin" is wrong; the Legacy series is a Classic NYC-era
  in-house product line.
