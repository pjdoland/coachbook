## Lead

The Buckled Pouch is a long-running NYC-era Coach pouch, in production
from the 1970s through the 1990s in two documented sizes
[yourgreatfinds-collectors-guide].

## Description

Per D1, the Buckled Pouch was made in NYC factories from the 1970s
through the 1990s in full-grain leather with brass hardware, and is
documented in two sizes: a **large** at 10.5" × 8.5" × 2.5" and a
**small** at 9" × 6.5" × 2.75" [yourgreatfinds-collectors-guide].
Closure is a buckle. Hardware is solid brass throughout, consistent
with NYC-era construction.

## History

The Buckled Pouch's production span — 1970s through 1990s — crosses
two of this archive's eras: the late Cashin / post-Cashin transition
(E2 boundary into E3) and the Classic NYC era proper (E3) into the
early offshore-transition period (E4). Because the model persisted
across decades, an individual Buckled Pouch can carry any era-
appropriate creed and serial format depending on its date of
production: a leather stamp with no creed (late 1960s–early 1970s
holdover examples), a numbered "COACH LEATHERWARE / Made in United
States" creed with a digit-only serial (mid-1970s–1994), or a
post-1994 letter-number style code on later examples
[coachbagrehab-dating, vintagecoachie].

**Designer attribution.** The Buckled Pouch's 1970s production span
straddles Bonnie Cashin's tenure (which ended in 1974) and the early
post-Cashin in-house period. Without a dated catalog appearance
anchoring the introduction year, attribution to either Cashin or a
specific in-house designer is `tentative`. Community claims that
attribute any post-1974 silhouette to Cashin are folklore; pre-1974
attribution requires a primary citation (UCLA Cashin papers or a
1962–74 Coach catalog).

## Authentication

A Buckled Pouch's correct era-keyed diagnostics depend on its date of
production. Cross-reference the era-keyed matrix in `authentication/`
for full feature lists.

- **Creed wording (E3 anchor).** A pre-1994 Buckled Pouch should read
  "COACH LEATHERWARE" with "LEATHERWARE" stamped in small letters
  beneath "COACH," typically with "Made in United States" or "Made in
  New York City, U.S.A." on earlier examples [coachbagrehab-dating,
  vintagecoachie]. This is the single most cited textual anchor for
  pre-1994 dating.
- **Serial format.** Pre-1994 examples carry a digit-only unique
  serial (formats like `xxx-xxxx` in the 1970s and `xxxx-xxx` in the
  1980s, per E3 — `tentative`) [lovetoknow-coach-serials,
  essexfashionhouse-serials]. Post-1994 examples would use the
  `[A-M][year-digit][factory-letter]-[style-number]` pattern; note
  the documented single-digit-year ambiguity (a "6" resolves to either
  1996 or 2006 without contextual cross-checks)
  [coachbagrehab-dating].
- **Earliest examples.** Some 1970s holdover examples may show a
  leather stamp with no creed patch — not a fake tell on a bag
  plausibly dated to the late 1960s / early 1970s
  [coachbagrehab-dating].
- **Hardware.** Solid brass, non-magnetic. A magnet that sticks
  indicates plated steel and is a strong fake or post-vintage signal.
- **Lining.** Many NYC-era pouches are unlined — an unlined Buckled
  Pouch is **not** a fake tell. Heat-cut polyester lining on a bag
  claiming pre-1995 manufacture is a strong reproduction signal.
- **Construction.** Glove-tanned cowhide with raw or burnished edges.
  Painted glossy edges on a bag claiming 1980s manufacture is a
  strong reproduction signal.
- **Country of origin.** "Made in New York City, U.S.A." on the
  earliest numbered creeds; "Made in United States" mid-1970s through
  1994; offshore origins (Costa Rica, Hungary, Turkey, Dominican
  Republic, Mexico) appear on 1994–early-2000s examples
  [coachbagrehab-dating, purseforum-costa-rica].
