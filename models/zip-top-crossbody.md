## Lead

The Zip Top Crossbody (style 9997) is a Coach Classic NYC-era
crossbody bag with a zippered top closure, documented in black in a
single Tier 3 dealer compilation [yourgreatfinds-collectors-guide].
Confidence is `tentative` until a dated catalog scan or a second
independent source corroborates the style number and silhouette.

## Description

Beyond the model description "Zip Top Crossbody," the style number
9997, the crossbody form, and a documented black colorway, the
surviving public record is sparse [yourgreatfinds-collectors-guide].
Dimensions, strap drop, lining, and introduction year are all
unconfirmed.

Zip-top closures appear on a handful of NYC-era models alongside
the dominant turnlock and the less-common snap and magnetic
closures. The zipper itself is an authentication cue: see
`research/E-authentication.md` §5 — Talon zippers are common on
pre-mid-1970s bags, with YKK dominant from late 1970s onward
(both `tentative` per Tier 3 sources).

## Authentication

A pre-1994 Zip Top Crossbody should carry the diagnostics common
to the Classic NYC era (cross-reference `authentication/` for the
full era-keyed matrix):

- **Style number 9997.** Visible on the leather creed inside the
  bag on numbered-creed examples [yourgreatfinds-collectors-guide].
  A style number alone does not date a specific bag — Coach reused
  style numbers across multiple production years.
- **Creed wording (E3 diagnostic).** A pre-1994 example should read
  "COACH LEATHERWARE" with "LEATHERWARE" stamped in small letters
  beneath "COACH," typically with "Made in United States" or
  "Made in New York City, U.S.A." on earlier examples
  [coachbagrehab-dating, vintagecoachie].
- **Serial format.** Pre-1994 examples carry a digit-only unique
  serial (`xxx-xxxx` 1970s, `xxxx-xxx` 1980s) per
  [coachbagrehab-dating] — `tentative`. Post-1994 production
  carrying 9997 forward (E4) would use the
  `[A-M][year-digit][factory-letter]-9997` pattern with the
  documented single-digit-year ambiguity.
- **Hardware.** Solid brass on the strap fittings and zipper
  pulls, non-magnetic. A magnet that sticks indicates plated steel
  and is a strong reproduction signal [coachbagrehab-dating].
- **Zipper.** Talon (pre-mid-1970s, `tentative`) or YKK (late
  1970s onward) is era-consistent; off-brand zippers warrant
  scrutiny.
