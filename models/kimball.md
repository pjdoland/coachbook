## Lead

The Kimball is a Coach Classic NYC-era shoulder bag commonly known
in collector circles by the nickname "Taco Bag" for its
folded-silhouette profile [yourgreatfinds-collectors-guide]. It is
documented in a single Tier 3 dealer compilation with dimensions but
no recorded style number; confidence is `tentative` until a dated
catalog scan or second independent source corroborates the model.

## Description

Per [yourgreatfinds-collectors-guide], the Kimball measures
**8.5"L × 2.5"W × 6.5"H** with a 51.5" shoulder strap drop and an
interior pocket bearing the creed. The folded, narrow-gusset
silhouette gives the bag a taco-shape profile when viewed from the
side, which is the basis for the community nickname. The long strap
drop puts it in shoulder/messenger-length range rather than
short-handled handbag form. No style number, introduction year, or
catalog appearance has been located in free public sources.

## Authentication

A pre-1994 Kimball should carry the diagnostics common to the
Classic NYC era (cross-reference `authentication/` for the full
era-keyed matrix):

- **Creed wording (E3 diagnostic).** A pre-1994 Kimball should read
  "COACH LEATHERWARE" with "LEATHERWARE" stamped in small letters
  beneath "COACH," typically with "Made in United States" or
  "Made in New York City, U.S.A." on earlier examples
  [coachbagrehab-dating, vintagecoachie]. The creed sits inside the
  interior pocket per [yourgreatfinds-collectors-guide].
- **Serial format.** Pre-1994 examples carry a digit-only unique
  serial (`xxx-xxxx` 1970s, `xxxx-xxx` 1980s) per
  [coachbagrehab-dating] — `tentative`. Any post-1994 production
  would use the `[A-M][year-digit][factory-letter]-[style]` pattern
  with the documented single-digit-year ambiguity.
- **Hardware.** Solid brass, non-magnetic. A magnet that sticks
  indicates plated steel and is a strong reproduction signal
  [coachbagrehab-dating].
- **Strap drop.** 51.5" per [yourgreatfinds-collectors-guide].
  Substantially shorter or non-adjustable straps on a bag claimed as
  Kimball warrant scrutiny.
