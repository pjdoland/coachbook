## Lead

The Bradley Bucket (style 9936), also recorded as the "Bradley
Bucket Crossbody," is a Coach Classic NYC-era bucket-silhouette bag
with a cross-body strap, documented in a Tier 3 dealer compilation
[yourgreatfinds-collectors-guide]. It sits within the broader
NYC-era bucket family alongside the Equestrian Compartment Bucket
(9809), the Nicholas Bucket Drawstring (9946), and the later
Bleecker Bucket.

## Description

The Bradley is identified as a bucket-style bag carried via a
cross-body strap under style number 9936
[yourgreatfinds-collectors-guide]. Dimensions, drawstring or other
top-closure details, and lining are unconfirmed in free public
sources. The bucket family it belongs to spans multiple NYC-era
silhouettes — the Equestrian Compartment Bucket adds interior
compartments, the Nicholas uses a drawstring closure, and the
Bleecker Bucket extends the family into the later catalog — but
specific shared construction details for the Bradley have not been
documented from a primary source.

## Authentication

A pre-1994 Bradley Bucket should carry the diagnostics common to
the Classic NYC era (cross-reference `authentication/` for the
full era-keyed matrix):

- **Style number 9936.** Visible on the leather creed inside the
  bag on numbered-creed examples [yourgreatfinds-collectors-guide].
  A style number alone does not date a specific bag — Coach reused
  style numbers across multiple production years.
- **Creed wording (E3 diagnostic).** A pre-1994 Bradley should
  read "COACH LEATHERWARE" with "LEATHERWARE" stamped in small
  letters beneath "COACH," typically with "Made in United States"
  or "Made in New York City, U.S.A." on earlier examples
  [coachbagrehab-dating, vintagecoachie].
- **Serial format.** Pre-1994 examples carry a digit-only unique
  serial (`xxx-xxxx` 1970s, `xxxx-xxx` 1980s) per
  [coachbagrehab-dating] — `tentative`. Any post-1994 production
  carrying 9936 forward (E4) would use the
  `[A-M][year-digit][factory-letter]-9936` pattern with the
  documented single-digit-year ambiguity.
- **Hardware.** Solid brass cross-body strap fittings,
  non-magnetic. A magnet that sticks indicates plated steel and is
  a strong reproduction signal [coachbagrehab-dating].
