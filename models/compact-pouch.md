## Description

The model name "Compact Pouch" implies a small, soft-sided pouch —
the family of NYC-era small leather goods that includes wristlets,
cosmetic pouches, and zippered pochettes. Beyond the name and style
number 9620, dimensions, closure type (zip vs snap vs drawstring),
lining, available colors, and introduction year are all unconfirmed
[yourgreatfinds-collectors-guide]. Small accessories of this era
often shipped without serial numbers per [coachbagrehab-dating],
which complicates dating individual examples.

## Authentication

A pre-1994 Compact Pouch should carry the diagnostics common to the
Classic NYC era (cross-reference `authentication/` for the full
era-keyed matrix):

- **Style number 9620.** Visible on the leather creed where present
  [yourgreatfinds-collectors-guide]. Note that small accessories
  frequently shipped without numbered creeds at all
  [coachbagrehab-dating]; absence of serial alone is not a fake
  tell.
- **Creed wording (E3 diagnostic).** A pre-1994 example, if creed-
  bearing, should read "COACH LEATHERWARE" with "LEATHERWARE"
  stamped in small letters beneath "COACH"
  [coachbagrehab-dating, vintagecoachie].
- **Serial format.** Where a serial is present, pre-1994 examples
  carry a digit-only unique serial (`xxx-xxxx` 1970s, `xxxx-xxx`
  1980s) per [coachbagrehab-dating] — `tentative`. Any post-1994
  production carrying 9620 forward (E4) would use the
  `[A-M][year-digit][factory-letter]-9620` pattern with the
  documented single-digit-year ambiguity.
- **Hardware.** Solid brass on any zipper pull, snap, or stud.
  Non-magnetic; a magnet that sticks indicates plated steel and is
  a strong reproduction signal [coachbagrehab-dating].
