## Lead

Patricia's Legacy is a Classic NYC-era Coach crossbody identified by
style number 9951. Per research stream D, it is one of several bags
in Coach's in-house "Legacy" series, alongside Lula's Legacy and
Rambler's Legacy (style 9061).

## Description

A crossbody-form glove-tanned cowhide bag with solid brass hardware,
typical of Classic NYC-era construction. The collector literature
notes mahogany brown as a documented colorway (D1); other colors
likely existed across the Classic NYC palette but are not yet
catalog-confirmed for this style. Dimensions, closure detail, and
introduction year remain open verification items pending a dated
catalog appearance.

## The Legacy series

"Legacy" in the context of Patricia's Legacy, Lula's Legacy, and
Rambler's Legacy is a Coach **in-house naming convention** for a
group of related Classic NYC-era bags — not a designer credit. This
matters because the word "Legacy" is sometimes attached online to
Bonnie Cashin in a way that conflates two different things:

- The Coach **Legacy series** documented here is a Classic NYC-era
  (mid-1970s–1994) in-house product line. Cashin's tenure ended in
  1974, before this era. Any attribution of these specific bags to
  Cashin is folklore (see STYLE.md §4 and `research/D-models.md`
  folklore flag).
- Cashin's broader design legacy (lowercase) — the turnlock, side
  toggles, kiss-lock, the Cashin Carry silhouette — is a separate
  matter and is not what the named Legacy series refers to.

Treat any seller listing Patricia's Legacy, Lula's Legacy, or
Rambler's Legacy as "designed by Bonnie Cashin" as a warning that
other claims in that listing should be checked.

## Authentication

Tells specific to a vintage Patricia's Legacy (cross-reference the
era-keyed matrix in `authentication/` for full feature lists):

- **Style number 9951.** Visible on the leather creed inside the bag
  on numbered-creed examples. A style number alone does not date a
  specific bag — Coach reused style numbers across multiple
  production years.
- **Creed wording.** A pre-1994 example should read "COACH
  LEATHERWARE" with "LEATHERWARE" stamped in small letters beneath
  "COACH," typically with "Made in United States" (E1, E2). This is
  the single most cited textual anchor for dating.
- **Serial format.** Pre-1994 examples carry a digit-only unique
  serial (formats like `xxx-xxxx` in the 1970s and `xxxx-xxx` in the
  1980s, per E3 — `tentative`). Post-1994 production (if any
  carried the style number forward) would use the
  `[A-M][year-digit][factory-letter]-9951` pattern, with the known
  single-digit-year ambiguity (a "6" resolves to either 1996 or 2006
  without contextual cross-checks).
- **Hardware.** Solid brass, non-magnetic. A magnet that sticks
  indicates plated steel and is a strong fake or post-vintage
  signal.
- **Construction.** Glove-tanned cowhide with raw or burnished
  edges. Painted/glossy edges on a bag claiming 1980s manufacture is
  a strong reproduction signal.
- **Designer.** Any seller listing Patricia's Legacy as "designed by
  Bonnie Cashin" is wrong; the Legacy series is a Classic NYC-era
  in-house product line.
