# Hippie Flap (9134) and Small Hippie Flap (9135)

## Lead — primary-confirmed Fall 1999 launch (NOT 2001 revival)

**The Hippie Flap and Small Hippie Flap launched as NEW in the Fall
1999 catalog** — Hippie Flap (Style 9134) at $198 and Small Hippie
Flap (Style 9135) at $148 [coach-catalogs-1960-1989] per research
stream Q1b. Brand voice at launch: **"Vintage forms. Streamlined
for now."**

Earlier archive framing (per Q2 partial reading of 2001 Holiday
Preview) treated the Hippie Flap as a Krakoff-era 1970s-silhouette
**revival**. **This was wrong**: the 9134/9135 numbers were already
in catalog as NEW Fall 1999 launches. The 2001 Holiday Preview entry
with "vintage form streamlined for now" copy was a **continuation
at the same style numbers**, not a single-season revival.

Final timeline: **2-year continuous run Fall 1999 → 2001 Holiday
Preview, then retired by Autumn 2002** (per Q2 follow-up).

## Era and designer

**Era E5 (Krakoff transition, 1996-~2002).** Designer is in-house
Krakoff-era team. NOT Cashin (her contract ended 1974, 25 years
before the Hippie Flap launched). NOT Krakoff specifically per the
21-year silent-creative-director pattern documented across all
1981-2002 catalogs (see N4 + Q1 + Q2 cross-cut findings).

The "Hippie" naming is part of the late-1990s Krakoff-era 1970s
revival aesthetic — silhouette references draw from period Cashin
forms but the design itself is post-Cashin. A vintage-Coach
authenticity claim attributing this model to Cashin is wrong on
its face.

## Authentication

For a vintage Hippie Flap (Fall 1999 — 2002):

- **Style 9134** (full size) or **9135** (small) in creed
  [coach-catalogs-1960-1989]
- **Post-1994 creed format**: letter-digit-letter serial (e.g.,
  `B0X-9134` or `B1X-9134`) — should be 1999 or 2001 production
  by single-digit-year decoder
- **No LEATHERWARE wording** in creed — post-1994 creed format
- **Country of origin**: offshore (Costa Rica / Hungary / Turkey /
  Dominican Republic / Mexico) consistent with E5 era
- **Solid brass hardware** typical of Hippie Flap-era Coach
  (consistent with Q1a Spring 1999 dual-hardware-option finding —
  many 1999-era Classics shipped with brass OR nickel; verify
  per-bag)
