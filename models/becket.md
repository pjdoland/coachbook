## Lead

The Becket (style 9920) is an uncommon Coach Classic NYC-era model
documented in a single Tier 3 dealer compilation
[yourgreatfinds-collectors-guide]. Its most distinctive recorded
feature is a detachable strap that attaches via a button rather than
the typical Coach buckle closure for the strap — a useful
authentication detail. Confidence is `tentative` until a dated
catalog scan or a second independent source corroborates the style
number and silhouette.

## Description

The Becket is described in the source compilation as an uncommon
NYC-era style [yourgreatfinds-collectors-guide]. The notable feature
is the strap attachment mechanism: rather than the buckle-and-tongue
arrangement Coach used on most NYC-era detachable straps, the Becket
strap attaches with a button. Beyond this, dimensions, available
colors, closure type for the bag body, and introduction year are
unconfirmed. No images, catalog appearances, or dimensional data
have been located in free public sources to date.

## Authentication

A pre-1994 Becket should carry the diagnostics common to the
Classic NYC era (cross-reference `authentication/` for the full
era-keyed matrix), with one model-specific detail:

- **Strap attachment by button (model-specific).** The detachable
  strap on an authentic Becket attaches with a button rather than
  the more common buckle-and-tongue arrangement seen on most
  NYC-era detachable straps [yourgreatfinds-collectors-guide]. A
  9920-creed bag with a buckle-style detachable strap is either
  mismatched (replacement strap) or misidentified.
- **Style number 9920.** Visible on the leather creed inside the
  bag on numbered-creed examples [yourgreatfinds-collectors-guide].
  A style number alone does not date a specific bag — Coach reused
  style numbers across multiple production years.
- **Creed wording (E3 diagnostic).** A pre-1994 Becket should read
  "COACH LEATHERWARE" with "LEATHERWARE" stamped in small letters
  beneath "COACH," typically with "Made in United States" or
  "Made in New York City, U.S.A." on earlier examples
  [coachbagrehab-dating, vintagecoachie].
- **Serial format.** Pre-1994 examples carry a digit-only unique
  serial (`xxx-xxxx` 1970s, `xxxx-xxx` 1980s) per
  [coachbagrehab-dating] — `tentative`. Any post-1994 production
  carrying 9920 forward (E4) would use the
  `[A-M][year-digit][factory-letter]-9920` pattern with the
  documented single-digit-year ambiguity.
- **Hardware.** Solid brass, non-magnetic. A magnet that sticks
  indicates plated steel and is a strong reproduction signal
  [coachbagrehab-dating].
