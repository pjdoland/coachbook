## Lead

The Soft Satchel — also known as the "Doctor Bag" — is a Coach
Classic NYC-era model recorded in a single Tier 3 dealer
compilation [yourgreatfinds-collectors-guide] as a medical
professional-style satchel configuration, with no style number or
introduction year documented. Confidence is `tentative` until a
dated catalog scan or a second independent source corroborates the
model and silhouette.

## Description

The Soft Satchel takes the form of a medical professional-style
satchel — a soft-sided bag with a rounded body and a top opening,
the silhouette traditionally associated with physicians' carry
bags [yourgreatfinds-collectors-guide]. The "Doctor Bag" alternate
name is recorded in the same compilation. Beyond the silhouette
category and the alternate name, no style number, dimensions,
color list, or introduction year has been located in free public
sources. No images or dated catalog appearances are documented.

## Authentication

A pre-1994 Soft Satchel should carry the diagnostics common to the
Classic NYC era (cross-reference `authentication/` for the full
era-keyed matrix):

- **Creed wording (E3 diagnostic).** A pre-1994 Soft Satchel
  should read "COACH LEATHERWARE" with "LEATHERWARE" stamped in
  small letters beneath "COACH," typically with "Made in United
  States" or "Made in New York City, U.S.A." on earlier examples
  [coachbagrehab-dating, vintagecoachie].
- **Serial format.** Pre-1994 examples carry a digit-only unique
  serial (`xxx-xxxx` 1970s, `xxxx-xxx` 1980s) per
  [coachbagrehab-dating] — `tentative`. Any post-1994 (E4)
  production would use the
  `[A-M][year-digit][factory-letter]-[style-number]` pattern with
  the documented single-digit-year ambiguity.
- **Hardware.** Solid brass, non-magnetic. A magnet that sticks
  indicates plated steel and is a strong reproduction signal
  [coachbagrehab-dating]. A doctor-bag-style top frame, if
  present, should also test non-magnetic on a Classic NYC-era
  example.
- **Construction.** Glove-tanned cowhide with raw or burnished
  edges. Painted glossy edges on a bag claiming pre-1994
  manufacture is a strong reproduction signal
  [coachbagrehab-dating].
