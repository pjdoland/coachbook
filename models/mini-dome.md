## Lead

The Mini Dome Handbag (style 9050) is a Coach Classic NYC-era model
documented in a single Tier 3 dealer compilation
[yourgreatfinds-collectors-guide]. The name indicates a small
dome-shaped handbag silhouette, but no images, dimensions, or dated
catalog appearances have been located in free public sources.
Confidence is `tentative` until a second independent source
corroborates the style number and silhouette.

The model name "Mini Dome" implies a compact rounded-top handbag

style number 9050, dimensions, closure type, lining, available

## Authentication

A pre-1994 Mini Dome should carry the diagnostics common to the
Classic NYC era (cross-reference `authentication/` for the full
era-keyed matrix):

- **Style number 9050.** Visible on the leather creed inside the
  bag on numbered-creed examples [yourgreatfinds-collectors-guide].
  A style number alone does not date a specific bag — Coach reused
  style numbers across multiple production years.
- **Creed wording (E3 diagnostic).** A pre-1994 example should read
  "COACH LEATHERWARE" with "LEATHERWARE" stamped in small letters
  beneath "COACH," typically with "Made in United States" or
  "Made in New York City, U.S.A." on earlier examples
  [coachbagrehab-dating, vintagecoachie].
- **Serial format.** Pre-1994 examples carry a digit-only unique
  serial (`xxx-xxxx` 1970s, `xxxx-xxx` 1980s) per
  [coachbagrehab-dating] — `tentative`. Any post-1994 production
  carrying 9050 forward (E4) would use the
  `[A-M][year-digit][factory-letter]-9050` pattern with the
  documented single-digit-year ambiguity.
- **Hardware.** Solid brass, non-magnetic. A magnet that sticks
  indicates plated steel and is a strong reproduction signal
  [coachbagrehab-dating].
