## Description

The Scooter is named on a long Tier 3 list of NYC-era styles
[yourgreatfinds-collectors-guide], with **bone** documented as one
colorway. Bone is a recurring NYC-era Coach color, also documented
on the Court (9870), Pocket Purse, and 1970s wristlet clutches
[yourgreatfinds-collectors-guide], so its appearance on the Scooter
is consistent with the era's standard palette but does not by
itself date a specific bag. Silhouette type, style number,

free public sources to date.

## Authentication

A pre-1994 Scooter should carry the diagnostics common to the
Classic NYC era (cross-reference `authentication/` for the full
era-keyed matrix):

- **Creed wording (E3 diagnostic).** A pre-1994 Scooter should read
  "COACH LEATHERWARE" with "LEATHERWARE" stamped in small letters
  beneath "COACH," typically with "Made in United States" or "Made
  in New York City, U.S.A." on earlier examples
  [coachbagrehab-dating, vintagecoachie].
- **Serial format.** Pre-1994 examples carry a digit-only unique
  serial (`xxx-xxxx` 1970s, `xxxx-xxx` 1980s) per
  [coachbagrehab-dating] — `tentative`. Any post-1994 (E4)
  production would use the
  `[A-M][year-digit][factory-letter]-[style-number]` pattern with
  the documented single-digit-year ambiguity.
- **Hardware.** Solid brass, non-magnetic. A magnet that sticks
  indicates plated steel and is a strong reproduction signal
  [coachbagrehab-dating].
- **Color check.** Bone is documented for the Scooter
  [yourgreatfinds-collectors-guide]. A bone Scooter should show
  the era-typical glove-tanned finish; painted glossy edges on a
  claimed pre-1995 example are a strong reproduction signal
  (cross-reference `authentication/` construction notes).
