This bibliography is a human-readable companion to `sources/registry.md`. The registry is the canonical citation database with stable IDs, accessed dates, archive snapshots, and tier ratings; this page reads as a curated list grouped by type, with brief annotations on what each source contributes to the archive. Every entry below resolves to a registry ID — to add a new source, update the registry first.

## Books

- Stephanie Lake (2016), *Bonnie Cashin: Chic Is Where You Find It* (Rizzoli) — `lake-cashin-2016` — most authoritative Cashin biography; archivist-authored. Free access via library lending or Google Books snippet view.

## SEC filings (primary)

- Coach, Inc. Annual Report on Form 10-K for fiscal year ended June 28, 2003 — `coach-10k-fiscal-2003` — first Tier 1 primary source pulled into the archive. Primary citation for 1941 founding, July 1985 Sara Lee acquisition, October 2000 IPO, April 2001 Sara Lee final divestiture, December 1996 Krakoff hire, July 1999 Jacksonville consolidation, April 2002 Lares Puerto Rico shutdown, and 2003 manufacturing geography.
- Coach Inc. SEC EDGAR filings index — `sec-coach-cik` — master index for Coach Inc. SEC filings (CIK 1116132).
- Coach 2000 S-1 prospectus — open research item, not yet pulled. Highest-value primary source for pre-IPO company history.

## Trade press and reputable secondary

- WWD, "Coach: The Early Years" — `wwd-coach-early-years` — trade-press history of the pre-Sara Lee era.
- WWD coverage of Coach IPO filing (June 2000) — open research item, not yet pulled.
- FundingUniverse, "History of Coach, Inc." — `fundinguniverse-coach` — republishing the *International Directory of Company Histories* entry; most thorough free public corporate history available without a paywall.
- Encyclopedia.com, "Coach Inc." — `encyclopedia-com-coach-inc`.
- Encyclopedia.com, "Coach Leatherware" — `encyclopedia-com-coach-leatherware`.
- Encyclopedia.com, "Bonnie Cashin" — `encyclopedia-com-cashin`.
- Vintage Fashion Guild, "Cashin, Bonnie" label resource — `vintagefashionguild-cashin` — source for the 1964 turnlock introduction date and Cashin's non-exclusive use across Coach, Sills, Meyers, Crescendoe-Superb, HBA, and D. Klein.

## Archival institutions and finding aids

- UCLA Library Special Collections, Bonnie Cashin Collection of Fashion, Theater, and Film Costume Design, 1913–2000 — `ucla-cashin-papers` — primary archive of Cashin's papers (~7,003 items; gift of the estate, July 2003). Finding aid by Kelly Besser, 2021. **Not at LACMA** (common error).
- UCLA Cashin Collection box list (PDF) — `ucla-cashin-pdf`.
- Bonnie Cashin Archive — `bonnie-cashin-archive-org` — operator-curated by Stephanie Lake; closest to a primary online archive of Cashin material.
- The New School Archives, Bonnie Cashin / Philip Sills & Company publicity photographs — `newschool-cashin-sills`.
- New York Public Library, Guide to the Bonnie Cashin Designs — `nypl-cashin-designs`.
- ASU FIDM Museum, "Bonnie Cashin" — `asu-fidm-cashin`.
- The Henry Ford, "Bonnie Cashin: Innovative and Influential" — `henryford-cashin`.
- Long Island Community Foundation, Bonnie Cashin Fund — `licf-cashin-fund`.
- LACMA Balch Art Research Library — related to fashion history but the Cashin papers themselves are at UCLA.

## Government and regulatory primary sources

- USPTO Trademark Status & Document Retrieval — `uspto-tsdr` — registration history, drawings, ownership chain for COACH / COACH LEATHERWARE / CASHIN CARRY.
- USPTO Patent Search — `uspto-patents` — Coach-assigned hardware patents (turnlock, dogleash clip).
