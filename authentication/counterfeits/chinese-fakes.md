# Modern Chinese fakes

## Lead

Modern Chinese fakes (2000s onward) are the most common counterfeit
family in vintage and modern Coach resale today
[purseforum-authenticate-coach, vintagecoachbags-authenticity]. The
recurring single most diagnostic signal is an era-impossible
country-of-origin combination — most often "Made in China" stamped
on a creed that still carries the small-caps "LEATHERWARE" wordmark,
a pairing the production timeline forbids
[coach-10k-fiscal-2003, coachbagrehab-dating]. All claims on this
page are `tentative`: the family is widely cited across Tier 3
community authentication sources, with the era-impossibility
anchor itself confirmed by a Tier 1 primary source.

## Era of production

Counterfeits date from the 2000s onward and continue to circulate.
Sub-types target two different markets: (1) **modern and Krakoff-era
Coach** — Signature C canvas, modern leather lines, and
post-vintage logo bags — which is the dominant volume; and
(2) **vintage NYC-era reproductions** — recreations of the Willis,
Court, Station Bag, and other LEATHERWARE-era silhouettes
[purseforum-authenticate-coach]. China became a legitimate Coach
manufacturing country during the same period; the fiscal-2003
sourcing list [coach-10k-fiscal-2003] names China alongside Costa
Rica, Mexico, India, Italy, Spain, Hungary, and Turkey. "Handcrafted
in China" appears on authentic post-vintage bags
[coachbagrehab-dating]. The legitimate channel and the counterfeit
stream therefore overlap in time, the same way they do for Turkey.

## Diagnostic tells

A modern Chinese fake typically carries multiple of these tells.
Each one echoes a documented authentic-bag construction anchor from
`research/E-authentication.md` or the cross-family signal list in
`authentication/counterfeits/index.md`; no single tell is sufficient,
but the combination is diagnostic.

- **"Made in China" combined with LEATHERWARE creed wording** — the
  single strongest tell. China-made Coach is post-vintage and
  post-LEATHERWARE per `country-of-origin.md`; the small-caps
  "LEATHERWARE" wordmark beneath "COACH" indicates pre-1994
  manufacture [coachbagrehab-dating, vintagecoachie]. The
  combination is era-impossible
  [coach-10k-fiscal-2003, coachbagrehab-dating].
- **Heat-cut polyester lining on a "vintage" creed era** —
  authentic NYC-era and many offshore-era leather bags are unlined
  or use cotton twill; heat-cut polyester is a fake tell on a bag
  claiming pre-1995 manufacture [vintagecoachie,
  vintagecoachbags-authenticity].
- **Magnetic hardware** — the turnlock or dogleash sticks to a
  magnet. Authentic brass turnlocks of the LEATHERWARE era are
  non-magnetic; modern Chinese fakes commonly use plated steel for
  cost [vintagecoachbags-authenticity,
  purseforum-authenticate-coach].
- **Wrong-era hangtag attached to wrong-era creed** — a Krakoff-era
  oval hangtag on a bag with a NYC-era LEATHERWARE creed, or a
  vintage-style hangtag on a Signature C canvas bag. The hangtag-
  creed mismatch is a Frankenbag-adjacent signal that recurs across
  this family [purseforum-authenticate-coach,
  bagforum-creed-history].
- **Era-impossible serial-creed combinations** — a letter-number
  serial (e.g., `B6D-9927`, post-1994 format) on a creed that reads
  "Made in New York City, U.S.A." (NYC wording ends approximately
  1980) [coachbagrehab-dating, vintagecoachie]. Or a digit-only
  pre-1994 serial on a "Made in China" creed
  [coachbagrehab-dating].
- **Edge paint on a "vintage" creed era** — authentic NYC-era
  Coach edges are raw or burnished, not glossy painted
  [valentinasvault-creed, bagforum-creed-history]. Modern Chinese
  fakes targeting the NYC-era silhouettes commonly add a glossy
  painted edge.
- **Signature C canvas with off-register pattern alignment, or
  letters that read closer to "G" than "C"** — the most common
  modern-line target carries pattern-fidelity tells of its own
  [purseforum-authenticate-coach,
  vintagecoachbags-authenticity].

## What they did right

Listed so an authentic modern or Krakoff-era Coach is not dismissed
by accident on a feature that the fakes also got right:

- "Made in China" stamping is itself legitimate on post-vintage
  bags — China appears in the fiscal-2003 manufacturing-country
  list [coach-10k-fiscal-2003] and "Handcrafted in China" is
  authentic on 2000s-and-later bags [coachbagrehab-dating]. Origin
  marking alone does not flag a fake.
- Silhouette is generally accurate to the targeted modern or
  vintage model.
- Style number on the creed often matches the model
  [coachbagrehab-dating].
- Hangtag shape can be approximately correct in isolation; only
  the *combination* with a wrong-era creed flags it.

A "Made in China" creed, correct silhouette, plausible hangtag, and
matching style number are therefore not authentication signals on
their own; they are the baseline both authentic Chinese-made Coach
and the fakes clear.

## China-as-authentic vs China-as-fake

This distinction matters for resale-shopper triage and is the central
reading challenge for any "Made in China" or "Handcrafted in China"
Coach:

- **Both exist.** Coach contracted with Chinese manufacturers from
  the 2000s onward [coach-10k-fiscal-2003, coachbagrehab-dating].
  Modern Chinese counterfeits circulated in the same period and
  remain the dominant counterfeit volume today
  [purseforum-authenticate-coach]. The country marking alone does
  not determine authenticity in either direction.
- **The era-consistency cross-check determines fake**, not the
  country marking. A "Made in China" or "Handcrafted in China"
  creed must be paired with a creed *without* the small-caps
  "LEATHERWARE" wordmark, a post-1994 letter-number serial, and
  hardware, lining, and construction consistent with post-vintage
  authentic Coach. Any one of these in disagreement with the
  country line points to a counterfeit or a Frankenbag — see the
  era-inconsistency tells in `authentication/counterfeits/index.md`
  and the decision tree in `creed-formats.md`.
- **The folklore trap.** A community claim that "Made in China
  means fake" is wrong; it conflates the dominant counterfeit
  stream with the country marking itself. Per the
  high-folklore-zone rule in `STYLE.md` §4.3, blanket country
  claims fail; per-bag evidence only. The decisive flag is the
  *combination* — "Made in China" with LEATHERWARE wording, or
  "Handcrafted in China" with a small-caps LEATHERWARE wordmark
  present, both of which are era-impossible.

## Replacement-component caveat

A magnetic dogleash, replaced lining, replacement creed, or swapped
hangtag on an otherwise-authentic bag could indicate Coach Repair
restoration rather than a modern Chinese fake. Coach Repair
(consolidated to Jacksonville, Florida in July 1999
[coach-10k-fiscal-2003]) sometimes replaced creeds, turnlocks,
dogleash clips, and linings during legitimate restoration. A
genuinely authentic Chinese-made Coach can carry a Coach-Repair-
installed replacement that itself reads as era-inconsistent.
Equally, an authentic NYC-era bag may carry a replacement creed
or hangtag that survived a legitimate restoration cycle. Cross-check
the *non-replaced* components before classifying as fake — leather
grain, stitching, hardware integrity, and the consistency of the
unaltered features carry the weight in close calls. A single
era-mismatched component is a question, not a verdict (see
`authentication/counterfeits/index.md` "Replacement-component
caveat" and the high-folklore-zone rule in `STYLE.md` §4.5).

## Detection at resale

What to ask the seller for, before bidding or buying:

- Clear photos of the creed with a ruler in frame for scale.
- Turnlock front and back, with magnet test if the seller will
  perform it on camera.
- Lining (full and any pocket details), with close-ups of any
  cut edges to identify heat-cut polyester.
- Country-of-origin tag, whether stamped into the creed or sewn
  into a seam.
- Hangtag (both sides), photographed alongside the creed in the
  same frame to verify era consistency.
- Serial format — the letter-number combination must be readable
  and consistent with the country line and creed wording.

If any of these are refused or unclear, walk away. The submission
photo set in `authentication/counterfeits/index.md` "Submission
workflow" is the canonical request list for any suspected fake.
