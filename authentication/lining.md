## Lead

Lining material on a vintage Coach is era-specific and model-specific; there
is no single "correct" lining across the canon. Many NYC-era leather bags are
correctly **unlined**, with raw suede interiors — absence of lining is not a
fake tell [coachbagrehab-dating, vintagecoachbags-authenticity]. The canonical
lining-related fake tell on a bag claiming pre-1995 manufacture is a
heat-cut polyester lining or pocket [coachbagrehab-dating].

## Unlined NYC-era

Court, Willis, and many other classic NYC silhouettes are correctly unlined
[coachbagrehab-dating, yourgreatfinds-collectors-guide]. An unlined bag with
a raw suede interior is era-correct for the late E2c through E4 windows
(roughly late 1960s through 1994). A lined "Willis" or "Court" claiming NYC
manufacture should be scrutinized; a perfectly unlined one should not.

## Cashin-era striped fabric (1962–1974)

Striped fabric lining is a Bonnie Cashin signature that recurs across her
Sills outerwear and her Coach bags of the period [lake-cashin-2016,
vintagefashionguild-cashin, ucla-cashin-papers]. The motif persisted across
several model lines into the early 1970s before fading from the Coach
canon. Per research/D and research/C, this striped lining is one of the
clearer non-creed Cashin-era diagnostics on bags from E2 that **were** lined.

Confidence: tentative. The pattern is widely cited in dealer and reference
sources but has not been catalog-anchored model-by-model in this archive.

## Cotton twill, grosgrain, branded jacquard (later eras)

Lined models from the late NYC and transition eras (E4 into E5) use
cotton twill, grosgrain, or, eventually, Coach-branded jacquard
[coachbagrehab-dating, vintagecoachie]. Branded jacquard with a repeating
"COACH" or "CC" motif belongs to the later transition and post-vintage
windows, not to NYC-era pieces.

## Pochette and cosmetics-pouch lining

Older versions of the pochette / cosmetics pouch are noted with a
**blue-and-white ticking** lining per research/D §"Small Leather Goods"
[yourgreatfinds-collectors-guide]. Treat blue-and-white ticking inside a
small zippered pouch as era-consistent for older variants; later variants
moved to other linings.

## Pocket construction

Square-bottomed inset pockets with **bound edges** are authentic to the
NYC and Carlstadt eras [coachbagrehab-dating, vintagecoachbags-authenticity].
**Heat-cut polyester pockets** — pockets with a fused, melted-looking edge
and a synthetic feel — are a fake tell on any bag claiming pre-1995
manufacture.

## Replacement-lining caveat

Coach Repair / Factory Restoration historically replaced creeds, turnlocks,
and dogleash clips, but lining replacement during restoration is
relatively rare. It is possible, however, and an otherwise consistent bag
with an anomalous lining should not be condemned on lining alone.
Authenticity should never hinge on a single feature; cross-check creed
wording, hardware, leather, and construction (per `creed-formats.md` and
research/E §11).

## Open verification items

- A model-by-model lining map — which models are correctly unlined, which
  are correctly lined, which carry striped Cashin-era fabric — requires
  primary catalog inspection. This is part of the broader catalog-
  anchoring work in research/E §10 and research/D's high-priority
  verification list.
- Cashin striped-lining swatches and date ranges per silhouette: candidate
  for resolution via the UCLA Cashin papers [ucla-cashin-papers].
- Date the introduction of branded jacquard (E5) to a specific year via
  catalog scans.
