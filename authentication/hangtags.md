## Lead

The leather "beanbag" hangtag is among the most-replaced parts of a vintage
Coach bag. Coach Repair routinely shipped restored bags out with new
hangtags, and many bags lost their original tag over years of use. A
"wrong era" hangtag — or no hangtag at all — does not by itself indicate
a counterfeit.

## Tag silhouette by era

Community guidance describes a gradual shift from a squarer, more
rectangular tag in the earliest creed-era bags toward a rounder,
softer-cornered "beanbag" silhouette in later production
[coachbagrehab-dating, vintagecoachie]. Specific transition dates are
not anchored to dated catalog imagery and remain `tentative`.

## Attachment hardware

Three attachment systems are reported across the vintage period
[vintagecoachbags-authenticity, purseforum-authenticate-coach]:

- **Brass clip** — a small swiveling brass clip on early creed-era bags.
- **Leather strap** — a thin leather thong tying the tag to the bag's
  hardware or strap loop.
- **Plastic-coated wire** — appears on later bags, into the offshore-
  production era.

Era assignments for each system are `tentative`; primary catalog imagery
is needed to anchor the dates.

## Stamping

Reverse-side stamping varies. Documented patterns include
[coachbagrehab-dating, vintagecoachie, purseforum-authenticate-coach]:

- "COACH" only.
- "COACH" plus a style number.
- Blank reverse.

Era-specific patterns are repeated across community sources but have not
been corroborated against dated catalogs. `Tentative`.

## Replacement caveat

Coach Repair shipped restored bags with new beanbag hangtags as a matter
of routine. Coach consolidated its repair operations at a Jacksonville,
Florida facility in July 1999 [coach-10k-fiscal-2003], and restored bags
returning to customers from that facility (and its predecessors)
frequently carried newer hangtags than the bag itself. **A vintage bag
with a newer-style hangtag is not necessarily inauthentic.** Cross-check
the creed, hardware, and construction before drawing conclusions from
the hangtag alone.

## The bag-without-tag question

Many vintage bags reach the resale market without their original
hangtag — lost in years of daily use, snipped off by an owner, or
detached during repair [purseforum-authenticate-coach]. **Absence of a
hangtag is not a fake tell.** It is a missing accessory, not a
diagnostic signal.

## Open verification items

1. Primary catalog imagery to anchor tag-silhouette evolution dates
   (squarer-to-rounder transition, attachment-hardware transitions).
2. Coach Repair shipping documentation or customer-service materials
   to confirm replacement-tag practice and timing.
3. Dated bags with original hangtags intact, to corroborate
   stamping-pattern eras.
