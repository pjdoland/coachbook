# Country-of-origin progression

## Lead

Coach manufacturing geography evolved in three broad phases: a **NYC-only era** (1941 through approximately 1980), a **multi-plant US era** (approximately 1980 through 1994), and an **offshore era** (1994 onward). Reading the country-of-origin marking on the creed — or, on later bags, on a separate sewn-in tag — is one of the strongest era anchors available, particularly when combined with creed wording (specifically the presence or absence of the small-caps "LEATHERWARE" beneath "COACH"). No single tag in isolation dates a bag with certainty; cross-checking origin against creed wording, serial format, hardware, and lining is required.

**Update 2026-05-01**: Coach Inc.'s fiscal-2003 10-K (`coach-10k-fiscal-2003`, Tier 1 primary, now in `sources/registry.md`) explicitly lists the countries Coach sourced from in fiscal 2003: **China, Costa Rica, Mexico, India, Italy, Spain, Hungary, and Turkey**. This upgrades those eight countries to `confirmed` for the 2003 sourcing footprint specifically. Notably, **Dominican Republic is NOT in the 2003 list** — either it had been phased out by 2003 or was a small-volume omission (downgrade to `tentative` until corroborated). **India and Spain are new additions** not previously identified in Tier 3 research. Earlier-decade dates (1994 Costa Rica start, etc.) remain `tentative` pending the 2000 S-1 primary pull.

## Diagnostic combinations

Country marking is most useful in combination with creed wording. The combinations below are the high-signal cases.

| Tag wording | Creed wording | Approximate window | Notes |
|-------------|---------------|--------------------|-------|
| "Made in New York City, U.S.A." | LEATHERWARE present | 1970s – c. 1980 | Earliest numbered-creed window. |
| "Made in U.S.A." / "Made in United States" | LEATHERWARE present | 1980 – 1994 | Multi-plant US era. |
| "Made in Costa Rica" | LEATHERWARE present | 1994 transitional period (verify) | Plausible during the 1994 transition; flag for primary verification before treating as a confirmed combination. |
| "Made in Costa Rica" / "Hungary" / "Turkey" / "Dominican Republic" / "Mexico" | LEATHERWARE absent (just "COACH") | 1994 – early 2000s | Standard offshore-era pattern. |
| "Made in Italy" | either | exotic / Madison / Gramercy lines | Italy is line-specific, not era-specific; do not use to date alone. |
| "Handcrafted in China" | post-LEATHERWARE wording | 2000s+ | Out of vintage scope. |

## Era-inconsistency = fake

The following combinations are era-inconsistent and indicate a counterfeit, a Frankenbag (real parts plus replacement creed), or a misrepresented item:

- **"Made in China" with LEATHERWARE creed** — China production postdates the LEATHERWARE wordmark window. Strong fake tell.
- **"Made in Costa Rica" / "Hungary" / "Turkey" / "Dominican Republic" / "Mexico" combined with the "Made in New York City, U.S.A." creed wording** — pre-1980 NYC-only wording cannot coexist with offshore origin.
- **"Made in U.S.A." with a post-LEATHERWARE creed (just "COACH"), pre-2014 serial format, on a model with no documented late-US-production run** — suspect; needs corroboration from documented US production of that specific model.
- **"Handcrafted in China" with the small-caps LEATHERWARE wordmark present** — era-impossible.
- **An offshore origin marking on a bag with a stamped-leather-only interior and no creed at all (E2b–E2c, late 1960s–early 1970s style)** — origin tag is era-impossible for that interior; almost certainly a replacement creed or a fake.

A bag that fails any of the above is not necessarily fake on this signal alone — replacement creeds installed by Coach Repair are a known confounder — but era-inconsistent origin markings always require a higher bar of corroborating evidence.

## Open verification items

1. **Coach S-1 prospectus (CIK 1116132, SEC EDGAR)** — the IPO-era business narrative is the most likely primary source for a manufacturing-geography summary covering the late-1990s offshore expansion. In the verification queue (`sec-coach-cik`).
2. **Factory-letter-code map** — the post-1994 serial format includes a third-character factory letter (e.g., "P" = Costa Rica per E12). The full letter-to-country mapping circulates in Tier 3 community sources only and needs primary-source confirmation. No Coach-published map has been located.
3. **NYC-to-Carlstadt transition date** — the c. 1980 date for the move out of NYC manufacturing is consistent across Tier 3 sources but has not been pinned to a primary source.
4. **Italy line scope** — community sources agree Italy was used for exotic skins and Madison / Gramercy production but disagree on whether Italian production was ever used for mainline glove-tanned cowhide.
5. **First-offshore-bag date** — 1994 is the consensus year for offshoring beginning; the specific country (Costa Rica per most sources) and the first models produced offshore are not yet primary-sourced.
