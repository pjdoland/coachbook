# Reed Krakoff

## Lead

Reed Krakoff joined Coach in December 1996 as Senior Vice President and Executive Creative Director, and was promoted to President, Executive Creative Director in September 1999 [coach-10k-fiscal-2003]. His arrival marks the boundary between the vintage Coach NYC-design vocabulary and the modern, marketing-led brand. This entry exists in the archive primarily as a date anchor for the end of the era this archive covers; Krakoff-era bags appear here only as contrast.

## Coach tenure (1996–2014)

- **December 1996** — hired as Senior Vice President and Executive Creative Director [coach-10k-fiscal-2003].
- **September 1999** — promoted to President, Executive Creative Director [coach-10k-fiscal-2003].
- **2001** — Signature C collection launches, the visual brand pivot away from the solid-leather identity toward logo-monogram canvas. This is the end-of-vintage marker for this archive [fundinguniverse-coach].
- **2014** — Krakoff departed Coach. `tentative`: the 2003 10-K is the latest primary source pulled into this archive, so post-2003 dates are not yet primary-cited here.

## Pre-Coach background

Confirmed from the fiscal 2003 10-K [coach-10k-fiscal-2003]:

- **July 1988 – April 1992** — Senior Designer (Design & Merchandising), Polo/Ralph Lauren.
- **April 1992 – January 1993** — Head Designer, Sportswear, Tommy Hilfiger USA.
- **January 1993 – December 1996** — Senior Vice President, Marketing, Design & Communications, Tommy Hilfiger USA.

## Education

Per the fiscal 2003 10-K [coach-10k-fiscal-2003]:

- A.A.S., Fashion Design, Parsons School of Design.
- B.A., Economics and Art History, Tufts University.

## Krakoff-era Coach bags in this archive

- **Bleecker Satchel** — introduced 2000 [coach-vintage-bleecker].
- **Signature C collection** — launched 2001 [fundinguniverse-coach].

## Contrast with Cashin

Cashin and Krakoff sit at opposite ends of the era this archive covers and the contrast is structural, not stylistic:

| Dimension | Bonnie Cashin | Reed Krakoff |
|-----------|---------------|--------------|
| Engagement | Contracted designer | Employee (SVP, then President) |
| Tenure | 1962–1974 (12 years) | 1996–2014 (~18 years) |
| Role scope | Design only | Design plus marketing, communications, brand direction |
| Material vocabulary | Glove-tanned leather, brass turnlock, side pocket | Mixed-material, logo-canvas-led from 2001 |
| Brand direction | Industrial-utility NYC-design vocabulary | Modern marketing-led brand, monogram era |

The archive treats Krakoff's bags as contrast because they fall outside the vintage NYC-design-language scope (`PLAN.md` decision 4: "Krakoff era (E5): included as contrast only"). Cashin is treated as primary because her tenure defined the original women's-handbag design vocabulary that the archive documents.
