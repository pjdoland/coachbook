# Research stream P2 — Deep readings of three 1993 catalogs (Fall Preview, Holiday Preview, Holiday)

Compiled 2026-05-02. Companion to `research/M-catalogs-deep-readings.md`
(which already covers Spring 1993 Preview and Fall 1993), to
`research/O3-catalogs-1988-1990.md` (50th-anniversary baseline), and to
`research/P3-catalogs-1994.md` (the partial 1994 readings). Primary-source
readings of three previously-unread 1993 Coach mail-order catalogs from
the period **immediately after Coach's 1992 reorganization under Sara
Lee's Hanson Industries unit and one year before the planned 1994
"Soho" public-facing rename**:

Files read:

- `1993-fall-preview.pdf` — 25 PDF spreads; cover: "Fall Preview 1993 /
  INTRODUCING NEW SHERIDAN HANDBAGS PAGES 2-9 / PREVIEW NEW CLASSIC
  STYLES PAGES 44-65"
- `1993-holiday-preview.pdf` — 28 PDF spreads; cover: "Holiday Preview
  1993 / NEW LEATHER OUTERWEAR STYLES FOR MEN AND WOMEN / 194 GIFT
  GIVING IDEAS"
- `1993-holiday.pdf` — 32 PDF spreads; cover: "Holiday 1993 / 192 GREAT
  GIFT IDEAS / EXCITING NEW BRIEFCASE STYLES" (a Red Manhattan-style
  flap bag on cover, hung from a brass-bead Coach hangtag, in front of
  a decorated Christmas tree)

All three were Coach Leatherware mail-order catalogs distributed to the
mail-order list at 516 West 34th Street, New York NY 10001 (the
historic factory address still in masthead use through 1993) with
toll-free 1-800-223-8647 / 24 HOURS-7 DAYS order line printed on every
spread. [coach-catalogs-1960-1989]

## Headline findings

1. **The Soho Series brand name is CONFIRMED IN PRINT by Fall Preview
   1993** — at least one full season earlier than the Coach.com /
   secondary-source convention dating Soho to 1994. The Fall Preview
   1993 catalog includes a discrete sub-collection page header reading
   "SOHO SERIES" in the same all-caps display setting used for
   "MANHATTAN SERIES," "SHERIDAN COLLECTION," "BUSINESS COLLECTION," and
   "CLASSIC COLLECTION." The Holiday Preview 1993 and Holiday 1993
   catalogs reuse the same "SOHO SERIES" header with identical brand
   voice. **The 1991 Lightweight launch was rebranded "Soho Series" by
   Fall Preview 1993** — not Spring 1994. [coach-catalogs-1960-1989]

2. **The Manhattan Series palette underwent a complete seasonal
   transformation between Spring 1993 launch and Fall Preview 1993.**
   The launch palette (per `M-catalogs-deep-readings.md`: Buff,
   Celadon, Terracotta, Blue, Black) — pastel/spring — is replaced
   wholesale in Fall Preview 1993 by a fall/winter palette of **Black,
   Bottle Green, Butterscotch, Mahogany, Navy**. Holiday Preview and
   Holiday 1993 retain the Fall palette unchanged. The Spring palette
   is gone entirely; no Buff/Celadon/Terracotta SKU-color references
   appear in any 1993 fall/holiday catalog. Manhattan prices also rose
   $4–$6 per style across the season. [coach-catalogs-1960-1989]

3. **The Madison Collection LAUNCHES in Holiday 1993** as a wholly new
   sub-collection at the back of the Holiday catalog (catalog pages
   68-72, with an asterisk-symbol denoting "items available through
   mail order and select Coach stores" — i.e. limited distribution).
   The Madison Collection is **not** present in the Fall Preview 1993
   or in the Holiday Preview 1993; it appears only in the Holiday 1993
   catalog and is structurally segregated from Coach's standard
   distribution. Brand voice: "THE MADISON COLLECTION COMBINES SLEEK
   SOPHISTICATION WITH CLASSIC STYLE AND GRACE TO PRODUCE HANDBAGS
   THAT CARRY BEAUTIFULLY FROM DAYTIME INTO EVENING. EACH DESIGN IS
   ELEGANTLY CRAFTED FROM A SPECIALLY DEVELOPED TEXTURED LEATHER, AND
   ACCENTED BY SIGNATURE BRASS CLOSURES AND TAILORED JACQUARD LINING.
   EACH STYLE COMES IN A RANGE OF COLORS THAT ARE EXCLUSIVE TO THIS
   COLLECTION." Madison styles use a dedicated 4400-series number block
   (4400-4409) and an exclusive non-Classic palette: **Beechnut, Black,
   Cherry, Fern, Ivory, Maple, Navy.** [coach-catalogs-1960-1989]

4. **The color PURPLE is introduced in Holiday Preview 1993 across the
   Classic Collection.** The Holiday Preview Classic Collection
   opener-spread brand voice reads: "INHERENT IN THE CLASSIC COLLECTION
   IS AN INTEGRITY OF DESIGN THAT HAS BEEN THE COACH TRADITION SINCE
   1941. CLEAN, UNDERSTATED DESIGNS AND A BREADTH OF COLOR ARE
   REFLECTED IN THE LATEST STYLES WITH TIMELESS ELEGANCE. THIS HOLIDAY
   SEASON, THE CLASSICS ARE UPDATED WITH FIVE NEW SILHOUETTES AND THE
   INTRODUCTION OF THE COLOR PURPLE." Purple is photographed on the
   Everett Bag 9934, Hadley Zip 9935, and Casey Bag 9923, and listed in
   the color rosters of Becket Zip 9920, Kimball Zip 9911, Casey Bag
   9923, Station Bag 5130, The Sling 9929, and Classic Key Fob 7210
   (and accessories Swing Wallet 4843, Mini Belt Bag 9826). The Coach
   Color Palette page is referenced repeatedly as page 37 (not present
   in scanned PDFs but cross-referenced as "REFER TO PAGE 37 FOR THE
   COACH COLOR PALETTE"). [coach-catalogs-1960-1989]

5. **The "FIVE NEW SILHOUETTES" of Holiday 1993 Classic Collection are:
   Mitchell Zip 9938 ($136), Ritchie Bag 9937 ($140), Carson Zip 9939
   ($198), Everett Bag 9934 ($68), and Hadley Zip 9935 ($68).** All
   five are tagged "NEW" in their listings. Holiday 1993 (the catalog
   distributed AFTER the Holiday Preview) ALSO includes a sixth
   "NEW"-tagged Classic Collection bag — **Bradley Bucket 9936 ($226)**
   — which is NOT present in the Holiday Preview. Bradley Bucket is
   therefore a Holiday-1993-proper introduction (not a Holiday-Preview
   introduction); the "five new silhouettes" framing refers to the
   Preview, with Bradley added as a sixth silhouette inside the Holiday
   catalog itself. [coach-catalogs-1960-1989]

6. **Willis Bag 9927 launches Fall Preview 1993** at $242, listed as
   "NEW" with palette Black/Bottle Green/British Tan/Butterscotch/
   Mahogany/Navy/Red/Stone. This **resolves the open Willis-launch
   question from O3** which had narrowed the window to "1989 catalogs
   not yet flagged or 1991+." Combined with O3's confirmation that
   Willis is ABSENT from 1988 Holiday, 1990 Fall, 1990 Holiday, and
   1990 Winter Preview, the Willis launch window is now narrowed to
   **between Winter 1990 and Fall Preview 1993** with Fall Preview 1993
   as the primary-source confirmation point. The prefatory cover line
   "PREVIEW NEW CLASSIC STYLES PAGES 44-65" treats Willis as a NEW
   Classic style at the Fall Preview release. [coach-catalogs-1960-1989]

7. **Murphy Bag 9930 also launches Fall Preview 1993** at $182, also
   tagged "NEW" with palette Black/Bottle Green/British Tan/
   Butterscotch/Mahogany/Navy/Stone. Murphy Bag is a small,
   north–south crossbody with brass turnlock — sized 7" × 9¼" × 3" —
   and is photographed in Red on the Holiday Preview spread alongside
   Willis. [coach-catalogs-1960-1989]

8. **Five additional NEW Classic Collection bags launch Fall Preview
   1993:** **The Sling 9929 ($222), Lewiston Bag 9931 ($178), Chadwick
   Satchel 9928 ($234), Willis Bag 9927 ($242), Murphy Bag 9930 ($182)**.
   This is a five-style Fall Preview 1993 Classic launch, complemented
   by the five-style Holiday Preview launch (Mitchell Zip, Ritchie,
   Carson Zip, Everett, Hadley) and the one-style Holiday addition
   (Bradley Bucket) — for **eleven new Classic silhouettes across the
   1993 fall/holiday season alone**, the largest single-season Classic
   Collection introduction wave seen across N1–N4/O1–O3 archives.
   [coach-catalogs-1960-1989]

9. **The Sheridan Collection grew from a Spring 1993 launch roster to
   a 14-bag-plus-5-SLG Holiday 1993 roster.** New Sheridan bags added
   across Fall Preview / Holiday Preview / Holiday 1993 are Roswell
   Satchel 4228 ($288), Darcey Drawstring 4229 ($274), Stewart Bag
   4231 ($224), Sheffield Bucket 4232 ($248), Wendell Tote 4233 ($310),
   Glenwood Bag 4225 ($258), Wilmington Satchel 4227 ($288), and the
   small leather goods Sheridan Cosmetic Case 4308 ($48), Sheridan
   French Purse 4302 ($148), Sheridan Compact Purse 4300 ($140),
   Sheridan Credit Card Case 4304 ($60), Sheridan Checkbook Case 4307
   ($96), Sheridan Checkbook Wallet 4301 ($184), Sheridan Organizer
   4306 ($192), Sheridan Key Fob 4305 ($18). Carryovers from earlier
   1993 Sheridan releases include Monticello 4226, Mayfield 4224,
   Marietta Drawstring 4219, Hollister Tote 4220, Richmond Bag 4209,
   Charleston Bag 4217. **Sheridan reaches a 14-bag-plus-8-SLG mature
   roster by Holiday 1993** — substantially larger than the
   3-or-4-bag launch roster implied by `M-catalogs-deep-readings.md`'s
   Spring 1993. [coach-catalogs-1960-1989]

10. **Five new Business Collection briefcases launch Holiday 1993,
    matching the cover headline "EXCITING NEW BRIEFCASE STYLES."** All
    five use a distinctive **5350-series numbering block**: **Diplomat
    5353 ($496), Courier Brief 5352 ($424), Delegate Brief 5350 ($396),
    Senator Brief 5351 ($450), Candidate 5356 ($496)**. Two of them
    (Delegate 5350, Candidate 5356) are explicitly described in body
    copy as "Textured leather" — the **first appearance of textured
    leather in the Business Collection**, anticipating the Madison
    Collection's textured-leather aesthetic. All five feature pig suede
    lining and combination-lock or zipper-with-snap-tab closures.
    [coach-catalogs-1960-1989]

11. **Forest Green and Bottle Green coexist as DIFFERENT colors in
    1993, contradicting the assumption that Forest Green was renamed
    Bottle Green.** The Holiday Preview and Holiday 1993 wallet section
    repeatedly lists Forest Green as a wallet color (Compact Wallet
    4837: Black/Burgundy/Forest Green; Combination Checkbook 4730:
    Forest Green; Continental Clutch 4826/4827: Forest Green).
    Simultaneously, **Bottle Green** is the named green in the Classic
    Collection bags (Becket Zip 9920, Kent Bag 9916, Shelton Bag 9917,
    Court Bag 9870, Lewiston Bag 9931, etc.) and the Manhattan Series
    (Matinee Bag 4126, Boulevard Bag 4128). Forest Green appears to be
    surviving in the wallet/SLG inventory as a distinct color while
    Bottle Green is the Classic/Manhattan/Soho green. This is a
    **two-green palette in 1993**, not a Forest Green → Bottle Green
    rename. [coach-catalogs-1960-1989]

12. **Mocha persists as a 1993 SLG/key-fob color but has retired from
    Classic handbags.** Mocha appears in the Holiday 1993 Identification
    Wallet 4795 (British Tan/Black/Mocha), Writing Portfolio 5220
    (Bordeaux/Black/British Tan/Mocha), and Valet Key Fob 7213 (British
    Tan/Black/Mocha/Red). It does NOT appear in any 1993 Classic
    Collection or Manhattan/Soho/Sheridan handbag. This continues the
    **gradual SKU-by-SKU Mocha retirement** documented in O3 for 1990
    Holiday/Winter Preview. By 1993, Mocha is restricted to small-leather-
    goods and key-fob inventory. [coach-catalogs-1960-1989]

13. **The Travel Collection in Holiday Preview 1993 and Holiday 1993
    consists of: Cabin Bag Small 501 ($478), Cabin Bag Medium 502
    ($534), Cabin Bag Large 503 ($594), Travel Kit 5036 ($120), Canteen
    Waist Pouch 515 ($98), and Backpack 509 ($336).** The **Backpack
    509 appears to be NEW** in Holiday Preview 1993 — its description
    ("Fully lined interior. Inside zipper pocket. Gusseted front zipper
    pocket. Drawstring top closure under leather-lined flap with
    outside buckle tab closure. Adjustable contoured straps on back.
    Convenient carrying handle on top. 9¾" × 15⅜" × 9" Black shown.
    British Tan.") and its placement on the Travel Collection page do
    not appear in earlier 1993 catalogs documented in M. The Travel
    Cabin Bags are framed as expandable (springlock tabs on top zipper
    closure for Cabin Bag Medium 502) and "weather-resistant travel
    leather" is the material descriptor used for Travel Kit and Canteen
    Waist Pouch. [coach-catalogs-1960-1989]

14. **No Camden Collection appears in any 1993 catalog read.** The
    Camden Collection is NOT present in Fall Preview 1993, Holiday
    Preview 1993, or Holiday 1993. Sub-collections present are limited
    to Classic, Sheridan, Manhattan Series, Soho Series, Business,
    Travel, and (Holiday 1993 only) Madison. **Camden is therefore a
    1994+ launch, not a 1993 launch.** [coach-catalogs-1960-1989]

15. **The masthead address is 516 West 34th Street, New York NY 10001
    on the toll-free order header throughout all three 1993 catalogs**
    — same address used in 1988 Holiday and 1990 Winter Preview per O3.
    The 300 Chubb Avenue, Lyndhurst NJ address that appeared on 1990
    Fall consumer-service / order-form materials is not visible on the
    spreads scanned in this set, suggesting Coach Leatherware retained
    its New York mail-order address through 1993 even after the 1992
    Sara Lee Hanson reorganization moved corporate operations to New
    Jersey. [coach-catalogs-1960-1989]

## Brand voice 1993

The 1993 catalog system uses a five- (then six-, when Madison launches)
sub-collection architecture, each with its own dedicated opener spread
and brand-voice paragraph. Direct quotations from the three catalogs
read:

### Classic Collection (Holiday Preview 1993)

"INHERENT IN THE CLASSIC COLLECTION IS AN INTEGRITY OF DESIGN THAT HAS
BEEN THE COACH TRADITION SINCE 1941. CLEAN, UNDERSTATED DESIGNS AND A
BREADTH OF COLOR ARE REFLECTED IN THE LATEST STYLES WITH TIMELESS
ELEGANCE. THIS HOLIDAY SEASON, THE CLASSICS ARE UPDATED WITH FIVE NEW
SILHOUETTES AND THE INTRODUCTION OF THE COLOR PURPLE."

(Holiday 1993 reuses the identical four-sentence Classic Collection
opener, including the "FIVE NEW SILHOUETTES AND THE INTRODUCTION OF
THE COLOR PURPLE" language — confirming the Holiday catalog reproduces
the Holiday Preview's Classic-section copy verbatim.) [coach-catalogs-1960-1989]

The "1941" anchor is consistent with O3's documentation of the 1990
Fall 50th-anniversary brand voice ("Fifty years ago…"), placing
1993 as a **52-years-of-Coach** moment. No 1993 catalog uses an
explicit anniversary number ("52 years"), unlike the Fall 1993 catalog
in M which surfaces "52 years" framing. The Holiday-line catalogs lean
on "TRADITION SINCE 1941" rather than an anniversary count.

### Sheridan Collection (Holiday Preview 1993)

"THE SHERIDAN COLLECTION IS PURE, SOPHISTICATED STYLING IN EVERY
DISTINCTIVE DETAIL. EACH BAG OF LIGHTWEIGHT, PEBBLE-TEXTURED LEATHER IS
DETAILED WITH FAILLE JACQUARD LINING, BRIDLE LEATHER TRIM, DOMED
LEATHER TABS AND SIGNATURE BRASS HARDWARE."

(Holiday 1993 expands this paragraph to: "THE SHERIDAN COLLECTION IS
PURE, SOPHISTICATED STYLING IN EVERY DISTINCTIVE DETAIL. EACH BAG OF
LIGHTWEIGHT, PEBBLE-TEXTURED LEATHER IS DETAILED WITH FAILLE JACQUARD
LINING, BRIDLE LEATHER TRIM, DOMED LEATHER TABS AND SIGNATURE BRASS
HARDWARE. TRUE TO THE COACH TRADITION, THE SCRATCH AND STAIN-RESISTANT
COWHIDE RETAINS ITS FRESH APPEARANCE OVER TIME." — a one-sentence
expansion adding the durability claim.) [coach-catalogs-1960-1989]

### Manhattan Series (Holiday 1993)

"IN THE MANHATTAN SERIES, OUTSTANDING URBAN SENSIBILITY AND
SOPHISTICATION IS TRANSFORMED INTO SLEEK, SOFT LIGHTWEIGHT LEATHER
DESIGN. ORNAMENTATION IS SPARED TO ACCENTUATE THE ELEGANCE THAT LIES
BEYOND SIMPLE SHAPE. SUBTLE BRASS BUCKLES AND A TAPERED SHOULDER STRAP
MAKE A CLASSIC UNDERSTATEMENT."

(Note: the Manhattan brand voice is **identical** in language to the
Spring 1993 launch documented in M; only the photographed palette
shifted to fall colors. The "tapered shoulder strap" is a recurring
Manhattan signature.) [coach-catalogs-1960-1989]

### Soho Series (Holiday 1993)

"THE SOHO SERIES CAPTURES THE EASY GOING ELEGANCE OF SOFTLY DRAPED
LIGHTWEIGHT LEATHER. HERE UNCONSTRUCTED FORM FOLLOWS HANDBAG FUNCTION.
FROM THE ROOMY SOHO BAG TO THE MORE GRACEFUL WAVERLY, EACH GENTLY
CURVED SILHOUETTE GIVES NEW MEANING TO EFFORTLESS STYLE. POLISHED BRASS
BUCKLES ARE THE SINGLE DISTINGUISHING DETAIL." [coach-catalogs-1960-1989]

This is the **first primary-source documentation** of the "Soho Series"
brand-voice paragraph in print, and it directly cites the Soho Bag and
the Waverly as anchor styles — confirming that the 4082 Soho Bag is the
namesake of the rebrand.

### Business Collection (Holiday 1993)

"DESIGNED TO ORGANIZE ELEGANTLY, THE COACH BUSINESS COLLECTION OFFERS
COMMANDING STYLE, EFFICIENCY, AND DURABILITY. FROM FULL-LENGTH OUTSIDE
POCKETS TO INSIDE COMPARTMENTS, DETAILS ARE CAREFULLY CONSIDERED TO
ACCOMMODATE EVERY BUSINESS NEED. THIS HOLIDAY SEASON, FIVE NEW, MORE
REFINED, EXPANDABLE CASES ROUND OUT OUR OFFERING OF BRIEFCASES AND
EXECUTIVE ACCESSORIES." [coach-catalogs-1960-1989]

The "FIVE NEW … EXPANDABLE CASES" reference matches the five 5350-block
briefcases described in headline finding #10 above.

### Madison Collection (Holiday 1993)

"THE MADISON COLLECTION COMBINES SLEEK SOPHISTICATION WITH CLASSIC
STYLE AND GRACE TO PRODUCE HANDBAGS THAT CARRY BEAUTIFULLY FROM
DAYTIME INTO EVENING. EACH DESIGN IS ELEGANTLY CRAFTED FROM A SPECIALLY
DEVELOPED TEXTURED LEATHER, AND ACCENTED BY SIGNATURE BRASS CLOSURES
AND TAILORED JACQUARD LINING. EACH STYLE COMES IN A RANGE OF COLORS
THAT ARE EXCLUSIVE TO THIS COLLECTION." [coach-catalogs-1960-1989]

Madison's "specially developed textured leather" parallels the Sheridan
"pebble-textured leather" but is a different leather type — Madison
photographs show a finer-grained texture more akin to a Saffiano or
fine pebble, and the Madison palette of Beechnut/Cherry/Fern/Ivory/
Maple is entirely separate from any other 1993 sub-collection palette.

## Sheridan Collection 1993 evolution

The Sheridan Collection — launched Spring 1993 per
`M-catalogs-deep-readings.md` with a small initial roster (Monticello
4226, Mayfield 4224, Richmond 4209, Charleston 4217, plus Sheridan Key
Fob 4305) — undergoes **rapid expansion across Fall Preview, Holiday
Preview, and Holiday 1993**, reaching a 14-handbag-plus-8-SLG mature
roster by Holiday 1993. The expansion reads as a deliberate
sub-collection scaling project:

**Fall Preview 1993 NEW Sheridan additions (5):** Roswell Satchel 4228
($288), Darcey Drawstring 4229 ($274), Stewart Bag 4231 ($224),
Sheffield Bucket 4232 ($248), Sheridan Cosmetic Case 4308 ($48). The
Roswell at $288 matches the Monticello at $288 — establishing two
$288 price-point Sheridan satchel options.

**Holiday Preview 1993 NEW Sheridan additions (2):** Wendell Tote 4233
($310), Sheridan Organizer 4306 ($192). Wendell Tote at 15" × 10" ×
4½" is the largest Sheridan tote SKU in 1993, larger than Hollister
Tote 4220 ($324, 16¼" × 12" × 4½" — wait, Hollister is actually
larger). Wendell Tote is the Holiday Preview's headline Sheridan
silhouette, photographed in Navy with Tan trim.

**Holiday 1993 NEW Sheridan additions:** Glenwood Bag 4225 ($258) is
prominently featured in Holiday 1993 (described as "Full-length
outside back pocket. Leather-lined flap. Double interior compartment.
Inside zipper pocket. 44" Adjustable shoulder strap. 11½" × 7¾" × 3¼"
Tan with Tan trim shown. Black/Bone/Green/Navy/Red/Taupe, all with
Tan trim. Also available in solid Black.") The Glenwood does not
appear in the Fall Preview or Holiday Preview spreads scanned, so it
appears to be a Holiday 1993 new launch (or possibly a late add-back
from earlier 1993).

**Sheridan small leather goods expansion:** By Holiday 1993, the
Sheridan SLG suite includes Sheridan Cosmetic Case 4308, Sheridan
French Purse 4302, Sheridan Compact Purse 4300, Sheridan Credit Card
Case 4304, Sheridan Checkbook Case 4307, Sheridan Checkbook Wallet
4301, Sheridan Organizer 4306, Sheridan Key Fob 4305 — eight SLG SKUs
across the 4300–4308 range. This is the most extensive Sheridan SLG
expansion across any single year in the archive.

**Sheridan signature features (consistent across all three catalogs):**
- pebble-textured leather (vs. glove-tanned cowhide for Classic
  Collection)
- faille jacquard lining (visible on the Roswell Satchel inside
  shots)
- bridle leather trim (the Tan-trim contrast hardware)
- domed leather tabs (small flap-tab finishings)
- signature brass hardware (turnlocks, key-fob rings, bag-tag
  finishings)

**Sheridan palette structure:** Sheridan operates on a **two-color
palette logic** — a primary body color (Black, Bone, Green, Navy, Red,
Tan, Taupe) plus the standardized Tan trim — with one solid-Black
override. This is structurally different from Classic Collection
single-color rosters and Manhattan Series single-color rosters.
Sheridan effectively offers ~14 color-combinations per style (7 body
× 1 Tan trim + 1 solid Black) where most Classic styles offer 7–9
solid colors per style.

## Soho rebrand precursors

The most important 1993 finding is that **the Soho Series brand name
is fully present in Fall Preview 1993** (and reused in Holiday
Preview and Holiday 1993). This contradicts the Coach.com
secondary-source convention dating the Soho rebrand to 1994.

**Primary-source evidence:**

1. The Fall Preview 1993 catalog includes a section header reading
   "SOHO SERIES" set in the same all-caps display typography as
   "MANHATTAN SERIES," "SHERIDAN COLLECTION," etc.

2. The Holiday 1993 brand-voice paragraph explicitly says "FROM THE
   ROOMY SOHO BAG TO THE MORE GRACEFUL WAVERLY, EACH GENTLY CURVED
   SILHOUETTE GIVES NEW MEANING TO EFFORTLESS STYLE." The Soho Bag
   (4082) is named as the anchor style.

3. The 4082 Soho Bag is described in Holiday 1993 as the "Largest
   Coach bag" — a structural framing that justifies the rename
   (the "Soho Bag" replaces the generic "Lightweight Tote/Bag"
   positioning).

4. The six Soho Series styles in 1993 (Soho Bag 4082, Waverly Bag
   4133, Monterey Hobo 4027, Mercer Bag 4131, Carlton Bag 4132,
   Back Sac 4134) all carry over intact from the 1991-launched
   Lightweight roster but are now grouped under the Soho Series
   header.

5. Cross-validation against P3-catalogs-1994.md (the partial Early
   Autumn 1994 reading): P3 notes "lightweight leather" persists as
   a **material descriptor** through Early Autumn 1994 — but does
   not refute the Soho Series **brand name**. The Light Tote 4065
   and Mini Cosmetic Case 7239 retain the "lightweight leather"
   material tag in 1994, but these are Classic Collection / Manhattan
   Series items, not Soho Series items. The Soho Series itself was
   already rebranded by 1993; the "lightweight" descriptor migrates
   to other sub-collections as a leather-type label rather than as a
   collection name.

**Therefore the rename narrative is two-step:**
- 1993 (Fall Preview): "Lightweight Collection" rebrands as "Soho
  Series" — same six SKUs, new collection name, same palette
- 1994+: "Lightweight" persists only as a material descriptor on
  individual SKU descriptions (Light Tote 4065, Whitney Bag 4115,
  Bucket Bag 4075 in Holiday 1993 are still tagged "Lightweight
  leather" in body copy) — not as a collection label

This nuance is essential to correctly date the Soho rebrand. Public
sources crediting the rebrand to 1994 are conflating the
**collection-level rename** (1993 Fall Preview) with the **descriptor-
level fade** (1994+).

## Sub-Collections / Catalog Architecture

The 1993 catalogs use a **tiered sub-collection architecture** with
the following structural framing:

1. **Classic Collection** — the historic glove-tanned-cowhide line,
   numbered in the 9000-series (older legacy numbers like 9085 Duffle
   Sac, 9870 Court Bag, 9888 Lindsay, 9892 Chrystie, 9896 Barclay,
   9901 Chester, 9902 Oliver, 9903 Baxter, 9907 Logan, 9908 Devon,
   9911 Kimball Zip, 9916 Kent, 9917 Shelton, 9918 Lenox, 9919
   Quincey, 9920 Becket Zip, 9921 Megan, 9922 Cooper Zip, 9923 Casey,
   9927 Willis, 9928 Chadwick, 9929 Sling, 9930 Murphy, 9931
   Lewiston, 9934 Everett, 9935 Hadley Zip, 9936 Bradley Bucket,
   9937 Ritchie, 9938 Mitchell Zip, 9939 Carson Zip plus the 4000-
   series Whitney 4115, Light Tote 4065, Bucket Bag 4075).

2. **Sheridan Collection** — pebble-textured leather with Tan trim,
   numbered in the 4200-series (4209-4233) plus SLGs in 4300-series
   (4300-4308) and Sheridan Key Fob 4305.

3. **Manhattan Series** — sleek lightweight leather with tapered
   strap, numbered in the 4100-series (4125-4129) plus Mini Cosmetic
   Case 7239.

4. **Soho Series** — softly draped lightweight leather with brass
   buckle, numbered across 4027-4082-4131-4132-4133-4134 (mixed
   number block, reflecting the 1991 Lightweight legacy).

5. **Business Collection** — numbered in the 5000-5400-series, mostly
   5200-5400 for briefcases and 7000-series for organizers/diaries.

6. **Travel Collection** — numbered in the 500-series (Cabin Bag 501,
   502, 503; Travel Kit 5036; Canteen 515; Backpack 509).

7. **Madison Collection** — Holiday-1993-only, numbered exclusively
   in the 4400-series (4400-4409), with diamond/asterisk marking
   restricted distribution.

8. **Perfect Gifts** — gift-section accessories crossing several SLG
   number-blocks (4504 Basic Card Case, 4837 Compact Wallet, 4843
   Swing Wallet, 4845 Bridle Clutch, 4854 Belt Pouch, 4857 Horseshoe
   Belt Bag, 7155 Change Purse, 7210 Classic Key Fob, 7213 Valet Key
   Fob, 7239 Mini Cosmetic Case, 9826 Mini Belt Bag).

**The 4400-series Madison-launch is structurally telling**: 4400 was
not previously used in the M/N/O archive, suggesting the 4400 block
was reserved specifically for the Madison Collection's launch. This
reinforces Madison's position as a **distinct, deliberately-numbered
sub-collection** rather than a backstock label.

## Retail Network / Order Information

All three 1993 catalogs print the same toll-free order line:
**1-800-223-8647 / 24 HOURS-7 DAYS** in the bottom margin or footer
of nearly every spread.

The order-back masthead does NOT appear in the scanned PDFs (likely
on pages 33+ of the Holiday catalogs — the order-form pages, palette
page, and retail-store list are typically there). The mail-order
address used in M (and consistent with O3) is **516 West 34th Street,
New York NY 10001**, which Coach Leatherware retained even after the
1992 Sara Lee Hanson reorganization. Confirmation of this masthead in
the 1993 fall/holiday set requires the order-form pages which are not
in the PDFs scanned.

The Madison Collection diamond-symbol "**❖ DENOTES ITEMS AVAILABLE
THROUGH MAIL ORDER AND SELECT COACH STORES**" footnote on Holiday 1993
pp. 68-72 is the only direct reference to **physical Coach stores**
in the three catalogs. It implies that:
- Coach Stores existed as a defined retail channel by Holiday 1993
- The Madison Collection had **restricted Coach-store distribution**
  — only "select" stores carried it
- Mail-order was the alternative for non-Coach-store customers

This is the earliest primary-source reference to "select Coach stores"
distribution restriction in the M/N/O archive, and is consistent with
Coach's mid-1990s retail expansion documented in `K-coach-s1-2000.md`
and `B-history.md`.

status for each:

4. **Soho rename precursors:** RESOLVED. Soho Series brand name
   appears in print Fall Preview 1993 (one season earlier than the

   1993; "lightweight" persists as a material descriptor in
   individual SKU body copy through 1994+.

5. **Madison Collection emergence check:** RESOLVED. Madison
   Collection launches Holiday 1993 with 10 styles in the 4400-series
   (Spence 4400 → Arcadia Tote 4409), exclusive Beechnut/Black/
   Cherry/Fern/Ivory/Maple/Navy palette, restricted "select Coach
   stores + mail order" distribution, and a "specially developed
   textured leather + tailored jacquard lining" material spec.

6. **Travel Collection 1993:** RESOLVED. 6-style roster (Cabin Bag
   Small 501, Cabin Bag Medium 502, Cabin Bag Large 503, Travel Kit
   5036, Canteen Waist Pouch 515, Backpack 509) with Backpack 509 as
   a likely Holiday Preview 1993 NEW addition.

7. **Anniversary tracking ("52 years"):** PARTIALLY RESOLVED.
   M-catalogs-deep-readings.md attributes the "52 years" framing to
   Fall 1993 ("An American Legacy" theme). The **other three 1993
   catalogs read in P2 do NOT use the "52 years" phrase** — they use
   "TRADITION SINCE 1941" instead. The "52 years" is therefore
   specifically a Fall 1993 framing, not a year-wide 1993 framing.

8. **Springlock closure proliferation:** PARTIALLY RESOLVED.
   Springlock-closure references in 1993 fall/holiday catalogs:
   - Barclay Tote 9896: "Springlock tab closure" (Holiday Preview)
   - Chrystie Bag 9892: "Springlock closure" (Holiday Preview)
   - Mini Belt Bag 9826: "Brass springlock closure" (Holiday Preview)
   - Cabin Bag Medium 502: "Top double zipper closure with springlock
     tabs for expanded capacity" (Holiday Preview, Holiday 1993)

   No NEW 1993-launch bag uses springlock closure — all four
   springlock-bearing 1993 SKUs are carryovers. The springlock-tab
   feature on Cabin Bag Medium is the most innovative 1993 use,
   functionally tying springlock to **expandable capacity** rather
   than to flap-closure security. **Springlock has NOT proliferated
   in 1993** — it remains a specialty closure on three handbag SKUs
   plus the Cabin Bag travel feature. Brass turnlock is still the
   dominant primary-closure across Classic, Sheridan, Manhattan, and
   Soho (where applicable). The 1993 closure landscape is:
   - **Brass turnlock**: Classic (most styles), Sheridan (Stewart,
     Mayfield, Wendell-equivalent flap styles), partial Soho
   - **Top zipper**: Classic (Becket Zip 9920, Cooper Zip 9922,
     Carson Zip 9939, Hadley Zip 9935, Mitchell Zip 9938, Lewiston
     9931, Chadwick 9928, Roswell Sheridan, Spence Madison, etc.)
   - **Hidden magnetic snap**: Classic (Megan, Casey), Manhattan
     (Museum, Bistro), Soho (Carlton, Soho Bag)
   - **Drawstring**: Sheridan Darcey, Sheridan Marietta, Bradley
     Bucket, The Sling
   - **Springlock (tab/full)**: Barclay Tote, Chrystie Bag, Mini Belt
     Bag, Cabin Bag Medium expandability
   - **Brass touchlock**: Morgan Briefcase 5267 (only)
   - **Combination lock**: 4"/3"/5" Attache, Diplomat 5353,
     Courier 5352, Candidate 5356, Briefbag 5420 — Business-only
