## Holiday 1991 catalog

Catalog identifier: `1991-holiday.pdf`

**Cover finding**: "Introducing the Travel Collection pages 64-73" —
Travel Collection introduced Holiday 1991 (NEW timeline event).

### Coach brand voice 1991

> "Logo treatments and trendiness have no place in our philosophy
> of classical design. This collection achieves the understated
> elegance that has been at the heart of our concept since 1941.
> Even the leather itself—supple, durable, natural cowhide—is a
> classic."

This is **explicitly anti-logo** brand voice in 1991, ten years before
the Signature C launch. Documents the philosophical shift between
1991 and 2001.

### Construction detail: hand-folded seam binding

> "Our seam binding contributes to the distinctive Coach look and
> durability of a Coach bag. The seam binding is hand-folded around
> every edge and sewn through several layers of leather."

NEW authentication finding for `authentication/stitching.md`. The
distinctive hand-folded seam binding is named as a Coach diagnostic.

### Closures: turnlock, springlock, magnetic snap

The 1991 catalog distinguishes **three** closure types across bags:
- **Brass turnlock** (Station Bag 5130, Compartment 9850, Ranch 9852)
- **Springlock** / "Springlock tab closure" (Barclay Tote 9896, Scout
  Bag 9890, Chrystie 9892, Convertible Clutch 9635)
- **Hidden magnetic snap closure** (Lindsay Bag 9888)
- **Top zipper** (Broadway Satchel Spectator 6891, Duffle Sac 9085)

`authentication/hardware.md` should be amended to include
"springlock" as a documented Coach closure type. The springlock is
a distinct hardware family from the brass turnlock.

### Color palette refinements from Holiday 1991

  Navy, Putty, Red, Tabac, Mocha, **Berry** (small leather goods only)

**Mahogany IS in 1991 palette** — corrects earlier errata (Mahogany
was **introduced between 1981 and 1991**, not post-1981 generally).

**Forest Green IS in 1991 palette** — Hunter Green still absent;
Forest Green may be the canonical predecessor to or co-existing with
Hunter Green. `colors/hunter-green.md` framing as "NYC-era" needs
refinement.

**Berry** is a small-leather-goods color not previously documented.
Should be added to `colors/` directory.

### Spectator color treatment

The "Spectator" suffix (e.g., Broadway Satchel Spectator 6891 vs
Broadway Satchel 9891; Compartment Bag Spectator 6850 vs 9850)
denotes a **two-tone color treatment**: body in main color +
British Tan trim. Spectator variants get **6XXX style numbers**
while their solid-color counterparts get 9XXX. NEW finding for the
archive — this style-number-prefix convention for two-tone
Spectator variants was not previously documented.

> "F. DINKY BAG  No. 9375  $96  Full-length inside zipper pocket. 48" Sewn-on tubular shoulder strap. 9"×5"×1"  Black, Bone, British Tan, Navy, Red. Pictured in Navy."

`models/dinky.md` framed Dinky as Cashin-era E2 with `tentative` Cashin attribution. The Holiday 1991 catalog's **Dinky Bag Style 9375** uses a 9XXX style number (post-Cashin numbering convention) and is described as a simple zippered crossbody — not a Cashin kiss-lock variant.

**Resolution**: There may be TWO different "Dinky" models:
1. A Cashin-era kiss-lock Dinky (small leather goods, pre-1974) — possibly real, possibly community folklore
2. The post-Cashin Dinky Bag Style 9375 (Holiday 1991 — Classic Collection crossbody)

`models/dinky.md` should be split or clarified. The 9375 Dinky is post-Cashin and unambiguously NOT a Cashin design.

### Spectator 6XXX prefix confirmed

Multiple "Spectator" two-tone variants confirm the **6XXX style-number prefix for two-tone (body color + British Tan trim) Spectator variants**:
- Scooter Bag 9893 standard / Scooter Bag Spectator 6893
- Scout Bag 9890 standard / Scout Bag Spectator 6890
- Compartment Bag 9850 / Compartment Bag Spectator 6850 (from earlier)
- Broadway Satchel 9891 / Broadway Satchel Spectator 6891 (from earlier)

The 6XXX prefix appears to specifically denote two-tone Spectator variants of Classic Collection models. NEW finding for `authentication/serial-numbers.md`.
