## Primary findings — confirmed facts

### Founding and ownership chain

> "Coach was founded in 1941 and was acquired by Sara Lee Corporation in
> July 1985. In October 2000, Coach was listed on the New York Stock
> Exchange and sold 17.0 million shares of stock in an initial public
> offering. In April 2001, Sara Lee ended its ownership with a
> distribution of its remaining shares in Coach via an exchange offer."
> — Item 7, Overview (line 5681)

**Confirmed:**
- Coach founded **1941**
- Sara Lee acquired **July 1985** (more specific than the "1985" in
  earlier secondary sources)
- IPO **October 2000**, **17.0 million shares** sold (resolves the
  disputed 17% / 19.5% / 19.9% question — the share count is what's
  in the primary; percentage figures from other sources reflect
  different denominators)
- Sara Lee final divestiture **April 2001** via exchange offer

### Brand description

> "Founded in 1941, Coach has grown from a family-run workshop in a
> Manhattan loft to a premier accessories marketer in the United States.
> Coach developed its initial expertise in the small-scale production
> of classic, high-quality leather goods constructed from
> 'glove-tanned' leather with close attention to detail."
> — Item 1, opening overview (line 676)

**Confirmed:**
- Originally a "family-run workshop in a Manhattan loft"
- Glove-tanned leather is the canonical material identity
- Original product line: men's small leather goods (referenced
  elsewhere)

### Lew Frankfort tenure

> "Mr. Frankfort joined Coach in 1979 as Vice President of New Business
> Development." — line 4074
>
> "Mr. Frankfort was appointed President of Coach in July 1985, after
> Sara Lee [acquired Coach]." — line 4072

**Confirmed:**
- Frankfort joined Coach **1979** as VP New Business Development
- Frankfort appointed President of Coach **July 1985** — same month
  Sara Lee acquired
- Frankfort's CEO/Chairman role at Coach Inc. dates from June 1, 2000
  (date of incorporation) (line 4063)
- Concurrent Sara Lee role: Senior Vice President of Sara Lee Jan 1994
  to Oct 2000

**Resolves dispute**: earlier sources conflated Frankfort's Coach
president (July 1985) with his Sara Lee SVP role; the 10-K cleanly
separates them.

### Reed Krakoff tenure (PRIMARY for 1996 hire date)

> "Reed Krakoff was appointed President, Executive Creative Director in
> September 1999 after joining Coach as Senior Vice President and
> Executive Creative Director in December 1996."
> — line 4109

**Confirmed:**
- Krakoff joined Coach **December 1996** (more specific than
  community-cited "1996")
- Initial role: SVP and Executive Creative Director
- Promoted to President + Executive Creative Director September 1999

**Krakoff's prior background (also confirmed from 10-K):**
- Polo/Ralph Lauren, Senior Designer (Design & Merchandising):
  July 1988 – April 1992
- Tommy Hilfiger USA, Head Designer Sportswear: April 1992 – Jan 1993
- Tommy Hilfiger USA, SVP Marketing, Design & Communications:
  Jan 1993 – Dec 1996
- Education: A.A.S. Fashion Design, Parsons School of Design;
  B.A. Economics and Art History, Tufts University

**Confirmed historical fact (line 3030):**
> "In July 1999, Coach consolidated its worldwide warehousing,
> distribution and repair functions into one location in Jacksonville,
> Florida."

This is the primary citation for **Coach Repair functions moving to
Jacksonville in July 1999** — first solid date for Coach Repair
operations relative to NYC.

**Confirmed historical fact (line 3020):**
> "As part of the strategy to shift production to independent
> manufacturers in lower-cost markets, Coach ceased operations at its
> remaining facility, located in Lares, Puerto Rico, in April 2002."

**Two important consequences:**
1. By April 2002, Coach had **no remaining Coach-operated
   manufacturing facility** of any kind. All production from then on
   has been by independent contractors.
2. Coach operated a manufacturing facility in **Lares, Puerto Rico**
   — this is a *new fact* that none of our Tier 3 sources surfaced.
   The Puerto Rico facility's start date is not stated in this 10-K
   (it was inherited or established earlier). This is a **new
   research item** — when did Coach open Puerto Rico? Likely a 1990s
   offshoring step on the way to full independent-contractor model.

### Manufacturing geography (fiscal 2003)

> "All of Coach's fiscal year 2003 product requirements were supplied
> by independent manufacturers. Coach buys independently manufactured
> products from a variety of countries including China, Costa Rica,
> Mexico, India, Italy, Spain, Hungary and Turkey." — line 2999

**Confirmed countries (fiscal 2003):**
- China
- Costa Rica
- Mexico
- India *(NEW — not in earlier research)*
- Italy
- Spain *(NEW — not in earlier research)*
- Hungary
- Turkey

Earlier research had identified Costa Rica, Mexico, Italy, Hungary,
Turkey, Dominican Republic, China — this 10-K confirms 7 of those
and adds India and Spain. **Dominican Republic is NOT mentioned in
the fiscal-2003 country list** — either it had ceased by 2003 or
was a small-volume omission. Mark our DR claim as `tentative` until
corroborated.

## What this 10-K does NOT contain (limits of this primary source)

- **Pre-1985 history is summarized in a single sentence**. No
  reference to the original 1941 founders, Bonnie Cashin (1962-1974),
  or the Cahn family. The 10-K's "history" is functionally Sara
  Lee–era forward.
- **No model-by-model history**. Specific bag models (Willis,
  Cashin Carry, Court, etc.) are not named; the 10-K speaks of
  product categories ("handbags, accessories…").
- **No serial-number / creed-format detail**. These are operational
  not investor-facing.
- **No factory locations 1941-1985**. The original 310/516 West
  34th Street factory addresses (multiple iterations) are not
  narrated; only the 2003 corporate use of 516 W 34th is recorded.

For pre-1985 history (Cashin tenure, original founders, NYC factory
chronology), the **2000 S-1 prospectus** would be more useful (it
would have a more complete "Business of Coach" history section
written for first-time investors). That document remains un-pulled.
