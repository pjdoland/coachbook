## Headline Findings

1. **1983-spring.pdf and 1983-spring-2.pdf are content-identical.** Same edition, two different physical copies scanned. Every SKU, price, dimension, color list, and brand-voice paragraph matches. The "-2" file is a marginally cleaner scan with a "Shared with love by Gabi" watermark and faint pencil annotations from a former owner. They should be registered as the same source instance.
2. **No mid-1983 price changes.** Spring → Fall 1983 carries every continuing model at identical prices. No retail price increase happened between the two seasons.
3. **Anchor copy: "throughout the forty years."** Both 1983 catalogs corroborate the 1941 founding date with the prose anchor "throughout the forty years," consistent with the 1985 catalog's "forty years" / "since 1941" framing in M1. The brand-voice frame in 1983 stresses *natural leather, solid brass, lifetime workmanship, no logos.*
4. **Fall 1983 introduces a Water Buffalo Hide wallet line** (4xxx series, printed pp 29–32). This is a discrete sub-collection — a different leather hide, different finish, different pricing tier — distinct from the standard glove-tanned cowhide range.
5. **Fall 1983 launches new shoulder/clutch/tote architecture:** 5110 / 5140 Slim & Standard Clutch; 5120 / 5130 Shoulder Pouch series; 9680 / 9685 / 9690 Tote series (effectively replacing the 9565 Shopping Bag); replacement-strap section at the front (1051 / 1060 / 1065).
6. **"Special priorities" verdict:** Willis 9927, Court Bag 9870, Madison Satchel, City Bag 9790, the Lightweight Collection, and Soho 4082 are **all absent** from both 1983 catalogs. None had launched yet.
7. **Cashin-era carryovers in 1983:** Dinky 9375, Saddle Pouch 9585 / 9590, Compact Pouch 9620, Musette 9625, Duffle Sac 9085, Stewardess 9525. Cashin is **not named** anywhere in either catalog (consistent with mid-Cahn-era no-named-designer pattern).
8. **Retail network 1983: three U.S. Coach Stores plus a Paris store referenced.** Madison Ave. (NYC), Wisconsin Ave. (DC), the Olympic Hotel (Seattle). Front matter mentions Paris alongside the U.S. cities but gives no Paris street address. Promise that "Coach Stores will soon open" in San Francisco and Boston.
9. **No toll-free / 800 number.** All three direct-order phone numbers in 1983 are local-area (212 / 202 / 206). The 800 number first documented in 1985 catalogs (per M1) had not yet been adopted in 1983.
10. **Belts gap (Fall 1983):** the Fall 1983 cover advertises "bags, belts and accessories" but the belts section is in the printed-page run (~ pp 20–25) that did not surface clearly in the PDF page reads available for that file. Logged as Open Verification Item — see below.

## Brand Voice 1983

Both Spring and Fall 1983 open with the same "natural leather, solid brass, lifetime" framing. Verbatim excerpts (transcribed from the catalog front matter):

> "Throughout the forty years that we have been making leather bags and accessories at Coach, we have continued to follow the same standards…"

(That "forty years" anchor — first noted in M1 for 1985 — is already in place in 1983, so the 40-year framing predates the 1985 catalog by at least two years; it had been worked into the brand voice by Spring 1983, putting Coach's then-claimed founding reference at 1943 if read literally — but the catalogs round to "forty" rather than computing strictly. The "since 1941" / 1941 founding phrasing in later catalogs is consistent with this.)

Other brand-voice motifs present in 1983:

- **"Glove-tanned cowhide"** — central material claim; emphasized as the reason for the supple drape and durability.
- **"Solid brass"** hardware — repeatedly called out (saddlery hardware, industrial turnlocks, industrial zippers, brass "feet" on the Stewardess).
- **"Lifetime"** workmanship — Coach bags are framed as one-time purchases, not seasonal items.
- **"Authentic"** — a frequently used word; e.g., "authentic solid brass saddlery hardware" on the Saddle Pouch.
- **"Classic"** — used in section headers (Classic Shoulder, etc.) and in the Fall 1983 cover title: *"A catalogue of classic Coach® bags, belts and accessories made out of natural leather and solid brass."*
- **No designer is named.** No reference to Cashin, no reference to a creative director or designer line.
- **No logo / no monogram.** The catalogs do not use that phrasing explicitly but the visuals show no canvas, no logo print, no signature pattern — exclusively all-leather construction with the small leather "COACH LEATHERWARE" hangtag on most bags.

The Fall 1983 cover headline reads (verbatim, transcribed):

> "A catalogue of classic Coach® bags, belts and accessories made out of natural leather and solid brass."

The ® on Coach® is present on the Fall 1983 cover.

## Spring → Fall 1983 Continuity Diff

### Carryovers (price unchanged Spring → Fall)

- 9085 Duffle Sac: $98 → $98
- 9375 Dinky: $76 → $76
- 9525 Stewardess: $116 → $116
- 9546 Skinny Tote: $116 → $116
- 9585 Saddle Pouch Large: $116 → $116
- 9590 Saddle Pouch Standard: $100 → $100
- 9620 Compact Pouch: $66 → $66
- 9625 Musette: $76 → $76

**No price increases on continuing models.** This is a notable data point — there is no inflationary mid-1983 reprice, even in a period of broader U.S. inflation winding down from the late-1970s peak.

### Additions (new in Fall 1983 vs Spring 1983)

- **Replacement Shoulder Straps** (1051 / 1060 / 1065) — front-of-book accessory section
- **Slim / Standard Clutch** (5110 / 5140) — clutch line-up formalized
- **Shoulder Pouch (5120 / 5130)** — new pouch series
- **Tote series (9680 / 9685 / 9690)** — three-size open tote line
- **Water Buffalo Hide Wallets (4xxx)** — entirely new sub-collection, distinct leather

### Discontinuations / Replacements (Spring → Fall 1983)

- **9565 Shopping Bag** — appears in Spring 1983; not present in Fall 1983; effectively replaced by the 9680 / 9685 / 9690 Tote series.

### Color list shifts

The 10-color master palette (Saddle, Navy, Mocha, Red, British Tan, Putty, Sage, Burgundy, Black, Tabac) is reprinted in both seasons. Per-model color lists are model-specific and trim down from the master 10. Notable per-model patterns:

- **Saddle Pouch (9585 / 9590) and Stewardess (9525)** offer 8 colors — drop Saddle and Sage from the master list.
- **Skinny Tote (9546)** offers 6 colors — drop Saddle, Sage, British Tan, and Red.
- **Duffle Sac (9085) and Dinky (9375)** are shown across the full 10-color palette.

These per-model color drops match a pattern visible across the 1981–1985 era: the 10-color palette is the master swatch, and individual styles are offered in subsets, with Saddle and Sage being the most often dropped on shoulder bags (where the dyed leathers carry better than vegetable-tanned naturals).

## Closure Types Inventoried (1983)

From the Spring + Fall 1983 catalogs, the following closure mechanisms appear:

- **Solid brass saddlery buckle** — Saddle Pouches (9585/9590), Duffle Sac (9085) flap, Stewardess (9525) flap
- **Industrial turnlock (solid brass)** — Stewardess (9525); described as "industrial turnlock"
- **Snap turnlock** — visible on Stewardess (9525) flap close-up
- **Brass industrial zipper** — Skinny Tote (9546); inside zipper compartments on Saddle Pouches
- **Drawstring + flap (combined)** — Duffle Sac (9085)
- **Magnetic snap** — not yet referenced in 1983 (consistent with magnets being a later-1980s addition)

All hardware described as "solid brass." No nickel, no chrome.

Both 1983 catalogs include an order form / mail-order page. Customers could mail or call in orders to any of the three U.S. stores. Shipping is via UPS standard.
