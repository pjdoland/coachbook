# Research stream D — Model canon

Compiled 2026-05-01. The bulk of style-number / model-name data comes
from a single Tier 3 dealer/collector compilation (D1) which is the
most exhaustive free public list located. Every entry must be
cross-verified against a second independent source (catalog scan,
PurseForum dated thread, museum holding) before promotion to
`models/<slug>.md` proper.

## Confidence policy for this file

- `confirmed` — style number AND introduction year both have ≥2
  independent sources or one Tier 1.
- `likely` — single Tier 1, OR two Tier 2/3 that agree.
- `tentative` — single Tier 3 only; needs corroboration.
- `disputed` — sources disagree; both shown.

Every entry below is at most `likely` until catalog imagery is
matched to it.

## Bonnie Cashin era (1962–1974) — Cashin-attributed silhouettes

| Model | Style # | Confidence | Notes / attribution issues |
|-------|---------|------------|----------------------------|
| Cashin Carry tote | — (early no-style era) | confirmed Cashin | Inaugural Fall 1962 collection (B/C streams). Note: turnlock came 1964 (per H stream); 1962-63 examples used different closures. |

| Dinky | — | likely Cashin-era | D1 lists; kiss-lock variant |

| Saddlebag (early version) | — | tentative | Saddle silhouette persists post-Cashin |
| Bonnie tote | — | tentative | Named for her |
| Convertible Clutch | — | tentative | D1 |
| Slimline / Skinny Tote | — | likely Cashin | D1 attributes; "1970s, designed by Bonnie Cashin"; needs pre-1974 catalog cite |
| Musette | — | likely Cashin | D1: "1970s brief-case style, thought up by Bonnie Cashin" |
| Swinger (Double Kisslock) | — | tentative | D1 |

**Folklore flag:** D1 attributes the Skinny Tote and Musette to
Cashin "designed in 1970s." Cashin's tenure ended 1974, so this is
plausible but needs primary-catalog confirmation. Many bags
introduced 1975+ are also called "Cashin designs" — they are not.

## Collections (named lines)

Per FundingUniverse company history (D10):
- **Sheridan collection** — introduced **early 1992** (D10).
  Disputed against D6 (Coach.com Vintage Sheridan Stewart Bag),
  which says Sheridan first introduced **1993**. Resolution: likely
  1992 launch in stores per D10 (a Tier 2 history); 1993 may be a
  specific Stewart-variant introduction. Mark `disputed` until
  resolved.
- **Camden collection** — early 1992 (D10).
- **Manhattan collection** — Spring 1993 (D10) — first major use of
  bright colors.
- **Sonoma collection** — 1995 (D10).
- **Soho** — 1994 (D5: "first introduced in 1994", D10 corroborates
  with late 1994 flagship opening).
  - **Soho Lafayette Bag** — variant noted in Tier 3 search results
    as a "beloved Coach silhouette first introduced in 1994" (D5
    family).
  - **Soho Duffle Bag XL** — variant; example serial JCM-4082 noted
    in D1 (factory letter J → would map to Sept under standard
    decode; "C" factory letter; Costa Rican leather with rich
    patina noted in D1).
