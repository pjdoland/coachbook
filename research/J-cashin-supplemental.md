## New findings

### Coach tenure dates — DISPUTED

| Source | Tenure | Tier |
|--------|--------|------|
| WWD "Coach: The Early Years" | 1962–1974 | 2 |
| FundingUniverse | 1962–1974 | 2 |
| vintagecoachie | 1962–1974 | 3 |
| Wikipedia (Cashin) | 1962–1974 | 3 |
| **Maharam** | **1961–1974** (13-year period) | **2** |

Maharam is a Tier 2 design publication and writes "Cashin worked with
Coach for thirteen years (1961–1974)." This conflicts with the
prevailing 1962 start year cited by every other source.

**Plausible reconciliation**: 1961 was the year the Cahns purchased
Gail Leather Products. Cashin may have been engaged in late 1961
under contract with the first collection shipping Fall 1962. The
"13-year period" framing accommodates a contract beginning in 1961
and ending in 1974 inclusive.

**Resolution status**: `disputed`. The Cashin Coach contract document
itself sits in UCLA's Cashin papers (`ucla-cashin-papers`) — pulling
that document would settle the start date definitively. Until then,
keep WWD's 1962 as primary in entries with a Maharam alternate
flagged.

### Cashin's broader design portfolio — confirmed expansion

Per Maharam: Cashin "designed for over thirty-five houses including
Hermès, Ballantyne, and Aquascutum." This adds **three more
manufacturers** beyond the Vintage Fashion Guild list (Coach, Sills,
Meyers, Crescendoe-Superb, HBA, D. Klein).

The "35+ houses" framing is significant for the Coach archive
because it underscores that:
- Cashin was a contracted designer with a portfolio across
  many makers
- Her vocabulary (turnlock, kiss-lock, side pockets, brighter
  colors) appeared on goods from many manufacturers, not just Coach
- A bag with Cashin-style hardware is not automatically a
  Cashin-designed Coach bag — the over-attribution problem
  documented in `designers/bonnie-cashin.md` extends to this
  multi-manufacturer reality

### American Airlines flight-attendant uniforms — new fact

Per Maharam: Cashin designed "the first-ever flight attendants'
uniforms" for American Airlines.

This is significant for the **Stewardess bag claim** documented in
`models/stewardess.md`:
- The community claim is that the Coach Stewardess bag was "part of
  United Airlines approved uniform" (Tier 3 only)
- Maharam confirms that Cashin had legitimate aviation-uniform
  design credentials (American Airlines)
- This makes the broader claim "Cashin/Coach designed for an
  airline" plausible
- It does NOT confirm United Airlines specifically, and does NOT
  confirm the Stewardess (9525) was part of any airline uniform
- A WebSearch for "Bonnie Cashin American Airlines" returned no
  primary corroboration; the Maharam claim itself stands alone

### Cashin design philosophy — direct quote

Maharam quotes Cashin:
> "I like to design clothes for a woman who plays a particular role
> in life, not simply to design clothes that follow a certain trend."

Useful for `designers/bonnie-cashin.md` — adds her own voice. Cashin
herself rarely surfaces in our existing sources beyond named-design
references.

### 1964 turnlock — Maharam silent

Maharam mentions turnlocks as "signature brass elements" in her
design vocabulary but does not date their introduction. The 1964
date from `vintagefashionguild-cashin` remains the only Tier 2
sourced specific year.

A separate WebSearch surfaced "another source" claiming **1961**
turnlock introduction (per a hit on the Maharam-related search).
That source was not directly fetched and may be a community
mis-citation. The 1964 date stays the canonical claim until
contradicted by primary.

## 2026-05-02 follow-up: 1962 confirmed canonical via brand source

A second WebSearch on 2026-05-02 surfaced two further corroborations
for the 1962 Cashin start date:

- **Coach official Facebook post** dated to 1962 framing: "1962. Enter
  Bonnie Cashin, our first lead designer..." — Tier 1 brand-published
  affirmation. Registry ID: `coach-fb-1962-cashin`.
- **RISD Museum** holds a Cashin Carry artifact dated **1962** in its
  permanent collection. Tier 1 institutional dating. Registry ID:
  `risd-cashin-1962`. RISD adds to the list of museums with Cashin
  Coach material (alongside UCLA, FIT, Met Costume Institute, ASU
  FIDM, Henry Ford).

**Resolution of the 1961-vs-1962 dispute**: 1962 is now Tier 1
brand-confirmed AND Tier 1 institution-dated. Maharam's 1961 framing
is the outlier; plausibly reflects contract-negotiation period rather
than engagement start. Mark the dispute downgraded — primary use of
1962 is canonical, with the Maharam alternate noted as a footnote
rather than a `disputed` tag.
