# Research stream M1 — Deep readings of the 1985 catalogs

Compiled 2026-05-02. Companion to `research/M-catalogs-deep-readings.md`.
Primary-source readings of the four 1985 Coach catalogs — the **first
Sara Lee–era** catalogs (Sara Lee acquired Coach in July 1985).

Files read:
- `1985-spring.pdf` (25 PDF pages; printed pages 1–44+covers)
- `1985-summer.pdf` (23 PDF pages; printed pages 1–29 visible, brown/rust cover)
- `1985-fall.pdf` (23 PDF pages; **content identical to `1985-summer.pdf`** — same brown/rust cover labeled "Summer 1985", same SKUs/prices)
- `1985-winter.pdf` (25 PDF pages; printed pages 1–48, olive/grey cover)

---

## Cataloging note: "1985-fall.pdf" is a duplicate of Summer 1985

The file `1985-fall.pdf` (3.6 MB) shares the **same physical cover**
("Summer 1985", rust/brown cover stock with COACH LEATHERWARE blind
emboss), the same brand-voice copy ("prices shown in this catalogue
will be valid until June 30, 1985"), the same model lineup, and the
same prices as `1985-summer.pdf` (3.4 MB). Spot checks of pages 6–11,
12–17, 18–23, 19–23, and 10–15 produced identical content (different
file hashes likely reflect different scans of the same physical
catalogue). Treat the two files as the **same edition**.

This means we have **three distinct 1985 editions**, not four:
1. **Spring 1985** (grey cover, prices valid until June 30, 1985)
2. **Summer 1985** (rust cover, prices valid until June 30, 1985) —
   model lineup essentially identical to Spring
3. **Winter 1985** (olive/grey cover, prices valid until December 31,
   1985, **toll-free number changed** from 800-262-2422 to 800-544-3004)

A genuine "Fall 1985" catalog may exist physically but is **not in the
archive** (or has been mislabeled). The transition from Summer to
Winter shows the price increase and product evolution that took place
across mid-to-late 1985.

## Spring 1985 catalog

Catalog identifier: `1985-spring.pdf`

### Cover
- Grey/sage card stock, blind-embossed COACH LEATHERWARE bracketed logo
- Tagline: "A catalogue of classic Coach® bags, belts, billfolds,
  portfolios and other accessories made out of natural leather and
  solid brass."
- Date: **Spring 1985**
- No featured bag on cover — text-only cover

### Brand voice — Spring 1985

> "We are a small family owned company that has been making leather
> bags, belts and billfolds since **1941**."

> "Throughout the **forty years** that we have been in business, we
> have always tried to maintain the highest standards of our craft.
> In the face of pressures placed on us by the great demand for our
> products, we have chosen to remain a small, privately held company,
> deeply committed to the excellence of our work and the quality of
> our service."

This **"forty years" / "since 1941"** framing is **primary-source
anchoring of the founding date at 1941** — pre-Sara Lee voice
preserved. Spring 1985 was finalized **before** the July 1985 Sara Lee
acquisition, so it represents the last pre-Sara Lee creative direction.

> "Although widely imitated, Coach Bags and Belts have, over the
> years, firmly established themselves, both here and abroad, as the
> genuine article — true American classics that are very well made
> and never go out of style."

> "Every Coach Bag is identified at the Coach factory with its own
> registration number. If it is a genuine Coach Bag, you will find
> this registration number branded right into the leather on an
> inside pocket."

> **Anti-counterfeit framing** (page 1):
> "It's Not A Coach® Bag Without The Coach® Tag. There has, in
> recent years, been a proliferation of imitation Coach tags hanging
> from imitation Coach Bags. There is a simple way to be sure you are
> not confused or deceived by these imitations. Just remember: it is
> not a Coach Tag unless it has our name on it, and it is not a Coach
> Bag unless it has our tag on it."

NEW finding: The **counterfeiting problem was acute enough by 1985**
that Coach used the catalog's first inside-page spread to address it —
documents that imitation Coach bags were a recognized market issue
even in 1985, well before the famous 1990s counterfeit crackdowns.

### Construction details — Spring 1985

> "Except for the Water Buffalo Hide wallets and accessories on pages
> 35-43, everything shown in this catalogue is made out of the same
> leather — a full grain, uncorrected glove tanned cowhide that is
> essentially the same leather used in making **baseball gloves**.
> Working with a leading tanner of baseball glove leather, we have
> developed a special formulation of tanning extracts and emollients
> that renders this rugged leather soft and supple to the touch
> without in any way detracting from its strength or beauty."

The **"baseball glove leather"** framing is preserved into 1985.

> "Our designs are straightforward and functional. **No unnecessary
> ornamentation, no gadgets, no gimmicks.** There are no linings to
> tear, no cardboard reinforcements to break. Just generous cuts of
> full grain leather, carefully sewn together, double stitched where
> necessary, so as not to come apart."

The **"no unnecessary ornamentation"** and **"no linings to tear"**
language pre-dates the Sheridan Collection (1992) by seven years —
this becomes a notable contrast when in 1992 Coach introduces the
Sheridan Collection with **"faille jacquard lining"** as a feature.

> "The prices shown in this catalogue will be valid until **June 30,
> 1985**."

> "We make Coach Belts in both men's and women's sizes. They are made
> along with our Coach Bags — produced in the same factory, made out
> of the same leather and stitched by the same sewing machine
> operators." ... "These buckles are not brass plated. They are brass
> through and through, cast in sand by a foundry that has been
> making equestrian hardware for **over a hundred years**."

### Coach Stores listed — Spring 1985

Six retail stores are listed in the catalog:
- 23 Rue Jacob, **Paris** 75006
- 754 Madison Avenue, New York
- 1214 Wisconsin Avenue NW, Washington DC
- 417 University Street, **Seattle**
- 164 Grant Avenue, **San Francisco**
- 75 Newbury Street, **Boston**

The **Paris store** is notable — Coach had an **international retail
presence by Spring 1985**. The Madison Avenue NYC flagship is
documented. Toll-free order line: **(800) 262-2422**, and the factory
address is **516 West 34 Street, NY, NY 10001**.

### Spring 1985 — Coach color palette (10 colors, page 1)

The "ten Coach Colors now in production":
- **Burgundy**, **Saddle**, **Red**, **Mocha**, **Tabac**, **Bone**,
  **British Tan**, **Navy**, **Putty**, **Black**

Same 10-color palette as 1981 (Sage→? — Sage absent here, may have
been retired by 1985; Saddle present; no Forest Green or Mahogany or
Burgundy yet; no Hunter Green).

**Primary-source palette progression**:
- 1981: Saddle, Navy, Mocha, Red, British Tan, Putty, Sage, Burgundy,
  Black, Tabac (10 colors)
- **1985: Saddle, Navy, Mocha, Red, British Tan, Putty, Burgundy,
  Bone, Black, Tabac** (10 colors — Sage retired, **Bone** added)
- 1991: Black, British Tan, Burgundy, Bone, Forest Green, Mahogany,
  Navy, Putty, Red, Tabac, Mocha, Berry (~12 colors)

NEW finding: **Bone joined the Coach palette between 1981 and 1985**.
**Sage was retired between 1981 and 1985**.

### Spring 1985 — wallets and accessories (Water Buffalo Hide)

NEW finding: The catalog **explicitly distinguishes Water Buffalo
Hide as a distinct material from Glove Tanned Cowhide**:

> "The Wallets And Accessories Pictured On The Following Pages Are
> All Made Out of Real Water Buffalo Hide. Here Is The Story Of This
> Remarkable Leather. In the forty years or so that we have been
> making turned-edge wallets, we have never seen a leather so ideally
> suited to this purpose. It is thin. It is firm. It has the
> distinctive temper and 'squeak' of a vegetable-tanned leather, and
> the beautiful natural graining of a fine piece of wood."

> "The slow-curing vegetable tannage that gives Coach Water Buffalo
> Hide its special character was probably first developed in the
> small village tanneries of **India**, where this primitive method
> is still employed today. For months at a time, the hides are left
> to soak in tanning pits dug into the ground..."

> "We make Coach Water Buffalo Hide in **eight beautiful colors** and
> will be pleased to send you a set of swatches on request."

So **Coach used two distinct leathers** in 1985: glove-tanned cowhide
(bags) and water buffalo hide (wallets and small leather goods).
This is an authentication finding for `authentication/leather.md`:
**Coach Water Buffalo Hide is from India**, not domestic.

### Construction details — explicit mentions

- **No linings** in bags — "There are no linings to tear, no cardboard
  reinforcements to break"

  ticking that resists spills")
- **Wallets fully lined in leather**
- **Hand-folded edges** — Tab Belts ("Hand folded edges. Solid brass
  harness buckle.") and Carriage Belts ("hand folded along both edges
  and around the tip")
- **Twelve stitches to the inch** — "The Operator threads his needle.
  A good sewing machine operator draws on many years of experience in
  order to produce as many as **twelve stitches to the inch** through
  several thicknesses of leather."

  cast in sand by a foundry"
- **Hot stamping die for registration number** — "A hot stamping die

- **Bag fittings made out of glove-tanned cowhide split-down** —
  "necessary to split it down to a lighter weight... reduces it to
  the desired thickness and in the process, renders it as soft and
  supple as a piece of fine Calfskin"

### Notable Summer 1985 — interpretation

The **Spring and Summer 1985 catalogs are functionally the same
edition** — both predate the July 1985 Sara Lee acquisition. They
represent **the last creative output of the pre-Sara Lee Coach**
(Miles Cahn was still primary owner). The continuity from Spring to
Summer suggests **catalog production cadence aligned to retail
seasons**, not creative refreshes — minor cover color changes only.

## Winter 1985 catalog

Catalog identifier: `1985-winter.pdf`

### Cover
- Olive/sage-grey card stock, blind-embossed COACH LEATHERWARE bracketed logo
- Tagline: "A catalogue of classic Coach® bags, belts, billfolds,
  portfolios and other accessories made out of natural leather and
  solid brass."
- Date: **Winter 1985**
- No featured bag on cover

### Brand voice — Winter 1985

> "We are a small family owned company that has been making leather
> bags, belts and billfolds since 1941."

> "Throughout the **forty years** that we have been in business..."

**Brand voice unchanged after the Sara Lee acquisition.** "Forty
years" / "since 1941" / "small family owned company" copy is
verbatim from Spring/Summer 1985. The Sara Lee acquisition (July
1985) **did not produce immediate brand-voice change** — the company
continued to present itself publicly as a "small family owned
company" months after becoming a Sara Lee subsidiary. NEW finding for
`history/sara-lee-acquisition.md`: Sara Lee preserved the
"family owned" framing in 1985 catalogs despite being the actual
parent.

### Operational changes Winter 1985

- **Toll-free number changed**: from (800) 262-2422 (Spring/Summer)
  to **(800) 544-3004** (Winter). Possibly reflects new Sara Lee
  fulfillment infrastructure.
- **Prices valid until December 31, 1985** (vs June 30, 1985 in
  Spring/Summer)
- **Table of Contents introduced** for the first time (Spring/Summer
  catalogs had no TOC; Winter has: Bags 3-21, Bag Accessories 22-23,
  Men's & Women's Portfolios 26-31, Men's & Women's Belts 32-37,
  Wallets & Accessories 39-48). NEW finding: the **catalog TOC
  appears in Winter 1985**, an organizational refinement.
- **Catalog grew** from 25 PDF pages (Spring) to 25 PDF pages (Winter)
  with printed pages 1-48 visible (vs Spring's 1-44) — slightly
  expanded.

### Winter 1985 — Coach color palette unchanged

Same 10 colors as Spring/Summer: Burgundy, Saddle, Red, Mocha, Tabac,
Bone, British Tan, Navy, Putty, Black.

### Notable brand-voice quotes worth preserving

> "We are a small family owned company that has been making leather
> bags, belts and billfolds since 1941." (Spring/Summer/Winter 1985)

> "Throughout the **forty years** that we have been in business, we
> have always tried to maintain the highest standards of our craft."
> (Spring/Summer/Winter 1985)

> "Our designs are straightforward and functional. **No unnecessary
> ornamentation, no gadgets, no gimmicks.**" (Spring 1985)

> "Just generous cuts of full grain leather, carefully sewn together,
> double stitched where necessary, so as not to come apart."
> (Spring 1985)

> "It's Not A Coach® Bag Without The Coach® Tag... it is not a Coach
> Bag unless it has our tag on it." (1985 — anti-counterfeit copy)

> "The Coach Factory, located as it is right in the heart of
> downtown New York City, has drawn its workforce from the many
> separate ethnic communities that make up the New York metropolitan
> area." (Spring 1985)

> "...essentially the same leather used in making baseball gloves...
> Working with a leading tanner of baseball glove leather, we have
> developed a special formulation of tanning extracts and emollients
> that renders this rugged leather soft and supple to the touch
> without in any way detracting from its strength or beauty."
> (Spring 1985)

> "These buckles are not brass plated. They are brass through and
> through, cast in sand by a foundry that has been making equestrian
> hardware for over a hundred years." (Spring 1985)

> "...essentially the same leather used in making baseball gloves..."

The most striking voice characteristic of 1985 Coach: **the
"family-owned company" / "no gimmicks" / "baseball glove leather" /
"brass through and through" framing is preserved verbatim across
Spring → Summer → Winter 1985, despite the July 1985 Sara Lee
acquisition**. The voice doesn't shift to corporate language until
later catalogs (the 1991 Holiday "logo treatments and trendiness have
no place in our philosophy" language, while still reflective of the
classic Coach philosophy, is more polished and brand-architectural
than the 1985 "small family owned company" framing).
