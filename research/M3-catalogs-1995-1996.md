# M3: Coach Catalogs Deep Read — 1995-1996 (Pre-Krakoff Era)

**Scope:** 11 catalogs spanning Spring 1995 through Holiday 1996, plus the 1996-1997 Source Book annual reference. Reed Krakoff joined Coach in December 1996, so all but the very last of these catalogs (1996 Holiday) are pre-Krakoff. The Source Book reflects the full pre-Krakoff line as offered in late 1996/early 1997.

## Headline Findings

### 1. Sonoma Collection launch — DISPUTE RESOLVED

**Claim under examination:** FundingUniverse and a 1992 catalog reference suggest Sonoma launched in 1995.

**Evidence from Spring 1995 Preview catalog (verbatim):**
> "Sonoma … two NEW subtle tones … Terracotta and Slate."

The catalog adds Terracotta and Slate as **additions to an existing Nubuc palette** (Bramble, Chamois, Sand). Natural Grain palette of Black, Buff, Saddle is shown as established. Sonoma is treated as a known, on-going collection rather than introduced.

**Conclusion:** Sonoma did NOT launch in Spring 1995. It pre-dates Spring 1995. The earliest plausible launch from this evidence set is Fall 1994 or earlier; the 1992 reference cited in prior research warrants direct verification of the pre-1995 catalog itself, but the Spring 1995 catalog text definitively rules out a 1995 launch. FundingUniverse's "1995" date for Sonoma is incorrect.

### 2. Sheridan Collection trajectory — confirmed disappearance

- **1995 Spring Preview:** Sheridan featured prominently with full lineup — Wendell, Mayfield, Glenwood, Monticello, Sheffield, Newberry, Roswell.
- **1995 Holiday Preview (master catalog):** Sheridan ABSENT from the table of contents.
- **All 1996 catalogs:** Sheridan absent.
- **1996-1997 Source Book index:** No Sheridan styles listed; collection categories are Classic, Soho, Sonoma, Madison, Travel, Briefcases, Accessories.

Sheridan recedes mid-to-late 1995 and is gone by Holiday 1995.

### 3. Berkeley Series launch — Early Autumn 1996 (pre-Krakoff)

The 1996-early-autumn.pdf debuts the **Berkeley Series**:
- Suede construction in Mahogany, Red, Russet, Black.
- Voice: "casual American sensibility."
- Reintroduced in 1996 Fall catalog.

This is the last collection initiated under the pre-Krakoff design organization.

### 4. Soho expansion across 1995-1996

- Spring 1995 Preview cover banner: "Five New Soho Styles" — major billing.
- 1996 Spring cover billing: "new SOHO colors."
- 1996-1997 Source Book — Soho positioned as: "A lightweight version of our glove-tanned leather and signature brass hardware … youthful, modern appeal … Soho features the kind of clean, casual shapes that adapt to your personal style." Palette: Red, Bottle Green, Mahogany, Black, British Tan, Bone, Navy.

### 5. Madison Collection identity — sophistication and pebble grain

1996-1997 Source Book Madison voice: "The sophistication of structure sets this collection apart. Sleek, pebble-textured leather, signature brass fittings and tailored jacquard linings have an old-world refinement that is the ideal complement to current style. Always in fashion, we created polished looks for day and evening." Palette: Black, Beechnut, Navy, Caramel, Scarlet. Distribution note: "The Madison Collection is available at select Coach store locations."

### 6. Sonoma Collection identity — soft and tactile

1996-1997 Source Book Sonoma voice: "Sonoma invites touch: Textural, sensuous, softening with wear." The Small Bucket "epitomizes the graceful qualities of the Sonoma Collection." Two leathers — Nubuc (Sand, Grape, Pumpkin) and Natural Grain (Black, Camel, Chocolate, Ivy, Tomato).

### 7. Classic Collection — heritage messaging

1996-1997 Source Book Classics tagline: "By definition, Classics are for now and always." Highlighted styles include Willis Bag, Duffle Sac ("in continuous production since 1973"), Station Bag ("among the original Coach styles"), Court, Regina, Casino, Manor, Murphy, Scooter, Small Sidepack, Prairie, City, Pocket Purse, Watson, Mini Shopper, Zip Top Shopper, Multi-Zip, Lunch Box Zip, Janice's Legacy, Taft, Anderson Zip, Carnival, Lula's Legacy, Helen's Legacy, Patricia's Legacy, Bibby Sling, Drawstring Backpack, Backpack, Handle Backpack ("New"), Daypack, Duffle Sac.

## Per-Catalog Notes

### 1995 Spring Preview (28 pp)
- Cover: "Five New Soho Styles" banner.
- New colors — **Soho:** Banana, Coral, Pear, Chambray (lightweight glove-tanned).
- New colors — **Sonoma:** Terracotta, Slate (added to existing Nubuc palette of Bramble/Chamois/Sand). Natural Grain palette: Black, Buff, Saddle.
- **Sheridan** at full prominence: Wendell, Mayfield, Glenwood, Monticello, Sheffield, Newberry, Roswell.

### 1995 Spring (slice; pp 42-65 visible)
- **Travelweave luggage debut** — woven webbing/leather travel pieces.
- Madison adds **new Mandarin** color.
- Brand voice carries Madison "sophistication" theme already established.

### 1995 Autumn
- **Sonoma:** "harvest colors elderberry and dark sage" — additions for autumn.
- **Madison:** new Citron, Granite.

### 1995 Holiday Preview (46 pp; functions as master/source book)
- **Sheridan ABSENT from TOC.**
- **Footwear debut** — Coach branches into shoes.
- **Limited Editions** reintroductions — heritage program.
- Closure types catalogued across the line: turnlock (Classic flap), magnetic snap (Sonoma flap), drawstring (buckets/backpacks), buckle (Soho buckle bag), zip top.

### 1995 Holiday
- **Olympic Games Collection** — USOC + ACOG Centennial commemorative line tied to Atlanta 1996.

### 1996 Preview
- "New colors for Sonoma & Madison."
- **Sonoma Natural Grain new:** Black, Maize, Parchment, Saddle, Sea, Wheat. **Sonoma Nubuc new:** Rose, Sand.

### 1996 Spring
- "New SOHO colors" — Ocean, Chamois, Cappuccino added.
- **Lexus Coach Edition** — co-branding partnership noted (Coach interior package for Lexus).

### 1996 Early Autumn
- **BERKELEY SERIES launch** — suede in Mahogany/Red/Russet/Black. Voice: "casual American sensibility."

### 1996 Fall
- **Sonoma new:** Pumpkin, Grape (Nubuc); Black, Chocolate, Ivy, Tomato (Natural Grain).
- Berkeley reintroduced.

### 1996 Holiday
- Holiday color themes: Red, Evergreen.
- **"Modern classics"** language introduced — early signal of Krakoff-era positioning beginning to surface (Krakoff hired Dec 1996; this catalog was likely produced under outgoing creative direction).

### 1996-1997 Source Book (~104 pp)
- Subtitle: "A Comprehensive Catalogue of Classic American Leathergoods."
- Annotated reference: includes celebrity descendants used as models — Jesse Owens' granddaughter Gina Hemphill Tillman with Coach Handle Backpack; George Washington's "great great great great great grandniece" Joanne Washington Prager with Coach Buckle Bag; Robert Frost's great grandson Ben Hudnut with Coach Organizer Brief.
- **Provenance quotes captured:**
  - Willis Bag: "introduced the use of the dowel as a design motif" — Coach's best-selling handbag.
  - Duffle Sac: "has been in continuous production since 1973."
  - Station Bag: "Among the original Coach styles."
  - Beekman Brief: "A classic, with unique dowel construction."
  - Metropolitan Brief: "This longtime favorite is seen in the hands of men and women, artists and executives, young and old."
- **Style-number conventions confirmed:** 4xxx Soho/Sonoma/Madison; 5xxx briefs; 8xxx organizers/diaries; 9xxx Classic handbags; 7xxx accessory cases.
- Final visual index spans Flap Handbags, Top Handle, Zips, Satchels, Buckets, Drawstrings, Totes, Slings/Backpacks, Travel, Briefcases (turnlock/zip/structured/computer/lightweight nylon-leather), Folios, Planners/Diaries, Computer Accessories, Desk Accessories, Specialty Cases.

## Closure Types

- **Turnlock (signature brass):** Classic line, Madison Carlyle/Gracie/Biltmore.
- **Magnetic snap:** Sonoma flap bags, drawstring with magnetic snap pocket.
- **Drawstring:** Sonoma drawstring, Classic drawstring backpack, daypack drawstring under flap.
- **Buckle:** Soho Buckle Bag.
- **Top zip / center zip:** Multi-Zip, Anderson Zip, Lunch Box Zip, Helen's/Patricia's Legacy zips, Sonoma Pocket Zip.
- **Hidden snap:** Soho Belted Pouch, Sonoma envelope/wallet clutches.
- **Lock (combination):** 4" Attache.

## Collection Structure (1996-1997 Source Book master view)

1. **Classic** — glove-tanned cowhide; heritage line; Willis/Duffle Sac/Station/Court/Regina center.
2. **Soho** — lightweight glove-tanned; youthful, modern; 4xxx series.
3. **Sonoma** — Nubuc + Natural Grain tumbled; soft, tactile; 4xxx series.
4. **Madison** — pebble-textured; structured; old-world refinement; select stores; 4xxx series.
5. **Berkeley** (Early Autumn 1996 launch) — suede; casual American sensibility.
6. **Briefcases** — Classic glove-tanned (Beekman/Kensington/Essex/Metropolitan), Structured (Diplomat/Attache/Briefbag), Lightweight Nylon-Leather (Satchel/Full Flap/Flap), Computer (Organizer Brief/Double Zip Organizer/Zip Top Compartment).
7. **Travel** — Cabin Bags, Porter, Double Compartment Carry-On, Travel Kits.

**Sheridan** — present only through Spring 1995; gone from Holiday 1995 onward.

## Notable Findings Beyond the Dispute

- **Olympic Games Collection (Holiday 1995)** — official USOC + ACOG Centennial line preceded Atlanta 1996 Games.
- **Travelweave luggage** — debuted Spring 1995; woven leather/webbing travel pieces (predecessor concept to later signature treatments).
- **Footwear debut** — first appears in 1995 Holiday Preview master catalog; Coach product expansion before Krakoff.
- **Lexus Coach Edition** — vehicle co-branding partnership documented in 1996 Spring; corporate partnership precedent.
- **Celebrity descendant model casting** — 1996-1997 Source Book uses Jesse Owens' granddaughter, Robert Frost's great grandson, and a Washington descendant to anchor heritage Americana voice. Pre-Krakoff "American leathergoods" positioning at full pitch.
- **"Modern classics" rhetoric surfaces in 1996 Holiday** — earliest pre-cursor of Krakoff-era language even though Krakoff had only just been hired in Dec 1996.
- **Style-number system stabilized:** 4xxx (Soho/Sonoma/Madison fashion bags), 5xxx (briefs), 8xxx (organizers/diaries), 9xxx (Classic handbags/duffles), 7xxx (small accessories/cases).
