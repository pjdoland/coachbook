## Tier 1 — Primary sources

### Coach Inc. SEC EDGAR filings
- CIK: **1116132**
- Filings index: https://www.sec.gov/cgi-bin/browse-edgar?action=getcompany&CIK=0001116132&type=10-K&dateb=&owner=include&count=40
- 2003 10-K example seen: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1116132/000095012303010503/y89793e10vk.htm
- 2015 10-K example: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1116132/000111613215000010/coh6272015-10k.htm
- Type: regulatory filings — primary record of company history, ownership, finances post-IPO (Oct 2000).
- Tier: 1
- Why valuable: every 10-K narrates the company history in its "Business" section; the original S-1 prospectus is the most thorough single primary source on Coach pre-2000.

### USPTO trademark search (TSDR)
- https://tsdr.uspto.gov/
- Type: trademark records
- Tier: 1
- Use: search "COACH", "COACH LEATHERWARE", "CASHIN CARRY" for registration dates, drawings, ownership history.

### USPTO patent search
- https://www.uspto.gov/patents/search
- Type: patents
- Tier: 1
- Use: hardware patents (turnlock, dogleash clip variants); trademark search returns design marks.

### Coach.com — current "Coach Originals" / re-releases
- Vintage Soho Bag product page: https://www.coach.com/products/vintage-soho-bag/CU909.html
- Vintage Sheridan Stewart Bag: https://www.coach.com/products/vintage-sheridan-stewart-bag/CDE04.html
- The Coach Originals collection: https://www.coach.com/shop/new/featured/the-coach-originals
- Type: brand-published descriptions of historical models
- Tier: 1 (with caution — modern marketing copy may simplify history)

### Coach Pinterest "From the Archives"
- https://www.pinterest.com/coach/from-the-archives/
- Type: brand-curated archive imagery (88 pins)
- Tier: 1
- Use: official Coach-published imagery of historical bags, ads, and catalogs; descriptions are brand voice.

## Tier 1 — Archival institutions

### UCLA — Bonnie Cashin Collection (PRIMARY archive)
- Online finding aid (OAC): https://oac.cdlib.org/findaid/ark:/13030/kt9z09q97x/
- UCLA box list PDF: https://unitproj.library.ucla.edu/cataloging/procedures/CashinMap.pdf
- Type: archival collection, fashion + theater + film costume design, 1913–2000
- Tier: 1
- Note: Cashin's papers are at UCLA, **not LACMA** as commonly stated. Acquired as a gift of the Bonnie Cashin estate, July 2003. Finding aid prepared by Kelly Besser, 2021. Portions digitized; ~7,003 items inventoried.
- Contains: design illustrations, contracts (including her Coach contract), photographs, press materials.

### Bonnie Cashin Archive (Stephanie Lake)
- https://www.bonniecashinarchive.org
- "The Archive" page: https://www.bonniecashinarchive.org/the-archive
- Type: independent archive run by Stephanie Lake, Cashin's biographer
- Tier: 1 (operator-curated primary)
- Use: most complete Cashin-focused public-facing site; original photography of Cashin items.

### Bonnie Cashin Foundation (legacy WordPress)
- https://bonniecashindotorg.wordpress.com/2011/03/22/slideshow-coach/comment-page-1/
- Type: blog-form archive of Cashin Coach pieces
- Tier: 2
- Use: image references with provenance commentary.

### LACMA Balch Art Research Library
- https://www.lacma.org/learn/balch-research-library
- Archives portal: https://www.lacma.org/archives
- Type: research library
- Tier: 1
- Use: contextual fashion-history holdings (Cashin papers themselves are at UCLA, not here).

### The New School Archives — Bonnie Cashin / Philip Sills publicity photos
- https://findingaids.archives.newschool.edu/repositories/3/resources/75
- Type: archival photos from Cashin's Sills coat collection (pre-Coach but contextually relevant)
- Tier: 1

### NYPL — Bonnie Cashin Designs finding aid
- https://nyplorg-data-archives.s3.amazonaws.com/uploads/collection/pdf_finding_aid/Guide_to_the_Bonnie_Cashin_Designs.pdf
- Type: finding aid PDF
- Tier: 1
