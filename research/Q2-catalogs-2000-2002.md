# Research stream Q2 — 2000–2002 catalogs

**Status: complete (with one minor gap).**
- `2000-holiday.pdf` — fully read

A parallel partial 1999 reading surfaced **Bleecker Tweed Satchel 6126 at $198 in 1999-fall** with body copy "Preview our new Mercer, Hippies, Bleecker Tweed, and Home collections." This forces a **Bleecker timeline correction**: Bleecker Tweed is a Fall 1999 introduction, not a Holiday 2001 launch. The Holiday 2001 "the making of a new classic" copy is a re-launch / handbag expansion of an existing 1999 SLG-and-tote sub-line.

## Headline findings

1. **Signature C is in market by Holiday 2000, not 2001.** `2000-holiday.pdf` shows **Wool C Small Tote 8131** in the Wool C signature jacquard (twin-C surface, wool ground). By 2001 Fall, Coach Signature is a six-page editorial sub-collection (TOC pp 6-9) anchored by **Coach Signature Multi-Function Tote 4263 ($368)** plus SLGs Compact Clutch 6054, Signature PDA Cover 8011, Signature Phone Case 8013, Signature Sunglass Case 8015. The "Signature C launches in 2001" framing common in secondary sources (FundingUniverse, encyclopedia.com) is **off by one season** at the catalog level — the precursor Wool C product ships Holiday 2000. [coach-catalogs-1960-1989]

2. **Bleecker is older than the Holiday 2001 "new classic" copy admits.** Per 1999-fall back cover, Bleecker Tweed Satchel 6126 was already $198 in Fall 1999. By Holiday 2001 the line expands into glove-tanned-leather handbags 9303 Satchel / 9305 Bucket / 9306 Tote / 9308 Demi Satchel / 9309 Zip / 9311 Demi Zip / 9314 Book Tote, plus tweed editions 6112 / 6126 / 6127 / 6128 and 5898 Bleecker Combination Wallet Checkbook. Combined with prior P2 finding (Bleeker Bag 4153/4154 in 1995-1997 Soho Series at $220), the **"Bleeker/Bleecker" name has three distinct production eras**: 1995-1997 Soho-Series Bleeker Bag (4153/4154 lightweight leather) → 1999 Fall Bleecker Tweed sub-line (6126 tweed) → 2001 Holiday glove-tanned-leather handbag expansion (9303-9314). The current Coach Originals "Vintage Bleecker Satchel CK664" with brand-published "first introduced 2000" claim conflates these.

3. **Heritage silhouettes survive into Holiday 2001 inside a "Coach Classics" sub-collection** (TOC p 18): **Court 9870, Companion Flap 9076, Large Daypack 9791, Willis 9927** all confirmed alive in original Black/British Tan/Mahogany/Navy/Bone palette with brass hardware. 2001 Fall Classics (pp 14-19) also confirms Daypack 9791, Rambler's Legacy 9061, Court 9870, Willis 9927. These late-NYC-era silhouettes survive **at least 7 years past the 1994 late-NYC peak** and into the post-Sara-Lee, post-IPO Coach Inc. era.

4. **"MADE BY COACH" mark revived for 2001 Holiday Preview cover** — deliberate mid-century brand-voice anchor coinciding with the Coach Classics editorial block.

5. **Coach Consumer Service relocated Carlstadt NJ → Jacksonville FL by Fall 2001**: "Coach Consumer Service / One Coach Way / Jacksonville, Florida 32218" + (904) 741-3090 area-code-aligned line. Earlier 410 Commerce Boulevard / Carlstadt NJ 07072-3099 (per P3 1994 corpus) is gone. Per existing archive timeline, Coach consolidated worldwide warehousing/distribution/repair into Jacksonville FL July 1999 — the catalog signage finally catches up by Fall 2001.

6. **www.coach.com is ambient by Holiday 2000** — printed on page footers throughout, not a featured callout. The URL is not branded as "new" or special; it's already infrastructure.

7. **Corporate mailing address standardizes at 516 West 34th Street New York NY 10001** across 2000 Holiday + 2001 catalogs (and already on 1999 Fall back cover, pre-IPO).

8. **Hamptons Collection persists through 2001 Fall as two parallel sub-lines** — Coach Hamptons Carryalls (TOC pp 3-5) and Coach Hamptons Leather (TOC pp 10-11) with Hamptons Leather Demi Pouch 7785 — **but is absent from the Holiday 2001 Preview TOC**, replaced by Coach Monterey, Mercer, Gramercy, Bleecker, Coach Bridle, Coach Neo. Hamptons sunset between Fall 2001 and Holiday 2001 Preview.

9. **Krakoff-era catalog architecture crystallizes by Holiday 2001.** TOC: GIFTS / COACH MONTEREY (p 8) / MERCER (p 10) / GRAMERCY (p 14) / WOMEN'S ACCESSORIES (p 16) / CLASSICS (p 18) / WOMEN'S GLOVES AND SCARVES (p 26) / BLEECKER (p 28) / COACH BRIDLE (p 30) / COACH NEO (p 32) / COACH WATCHES (p 34) / MEN'S ACCESSORIES (p 36) / TRAVEL (p 40) / BUSINESS (p 42).

10. **Coach Glove-Tanned Products as 2001 sub-cluster.** Coach Leather Cleaning and Care Collection 1047 copy names "Coach Glove-Tanned Products (Bleecker, Classics, Coach Monterey and select Briefcases)" — primary-confirmed material taxonomy distinguishing glove-tanned products from the Krakoff-era twill/lightweight/jacquard sub-collections.

11. **Limited Edition Legacy block** in 2001 Holiday Preview: Ridgefield Flap 9812, Sutton Zip 9814, Camera Zip 9817, Slim Duffle Medium 9816, Classic City Tote 9819, Patricia's Legacy 9951, Legacy Satchel 9063, Legacy Zip 9966, Legacy Studio Flap 9144/9145.

12. **Hippie Flap 9134/9135** in 2001 Holiday Preview with copy "vintage form streamlined for now / The quintessential fall fashion statement" — Krakoff-era 1970s-silhouette revival.

13. **Sara Lee absent / Coach Inc. standalone.** No Sara Lee marker in body copy, back matter, copyright lines, or care instructions of any of the three Q2 catalogs read. Sara Lee final divestiture was April 2001 per archive timeline; catalog brand voice already corresponds to standalone Coach Inc.

14. **Marisa Tomei celebrity placement in 2000 Holiday catalog** — IPO-season celebrity-as-model deployment. Continues the late-1990s pattern (Holly Solomon for Ergo Holiday 1997 per M4) of named cultural-figure brand association.

15. **60th anniversary (2001) is NOT explicitly marked in catalog editorial copy** in any of the three Q2 catalogs read. The "MADE BY COACH" cover mark and Coach Classics block are the closest heritage gestures, but no "60 years of Coach" editorial moment surfaces. This is consistent with the deep-read pattern of Coach treating round-number anniversaries with deliberate understatement — the 40-year (1981) and 60-year (2001) marks are silent in catalog copy; only the 45-year (1986 Frankfort letter) and 50-year (1990) milestones get explicit editorial framing.

## 2002 Autumn deep-read findings (post-partial completion)

**Catalog:** *Coach* Autumn 2002 (32 interior pages + cover; keycode SC81; copyright code 0802INS)

This is the LAST in-scope catalog for the Coachbook archive (1941–~2002). Findings below close out Q2 by resolving the 2002 column of the heritage-survival matrix and confirming Krakoff-era sub-collection turnover.

### Headline findings (2002 Autumn)

1. **Hamptons IS the flagship of Autumn 2002.** After being missing from 2001 Holiday Preview, Hamptons returns as the dominant pebbled-leather sub-collection — Coach Hamptons Carryall, Coach Hamptons Tote, Coach Hamptons Bucket Bag, Coach Hamptons Demi Pouch (No. 7785), and "Hampton Rye Hobo" all appear. The line is positioned as the season's leather-classic anchor, opposite the new Signature jacquard and Soft Signature lines. Single-season Holiday 2001 sunset was NOT permanent.

2. **"Coach Soft Signature" launches as a NEW jacquard family** distinct from "Coach Signature." Soft Signature uses an unstructured, slouchier silhouette in jacquard with leather trim — a deliberately gentler counterpoint to the structured Signature line. Both families share the catalog's center spreads.

3. **Signature C jacquard proliferates aggressively.** By Autumn 2002 the Signature pattern appears not just on jacquard bags but on EMBOSSED CALFSKIN — Coach Signature Embossed Leather Medium Zip-Top Cosmetic (No. 8991, $68), Coach Signature Embossed Slim Envelope Wallet (No. 6873, $198), Signature Skinny Wristlet (No. 8365), Signature Carryall (No. 6086), Signature Travel Tote — confirming the C-monogram's transition from jacquard motif to brand-wide debossed leather signature.

4. **"COACH LEATHERWARE EST. 1941" embossed-leather patch appears as a heritage mark** on multiple Signature pieces (Signature Carryall 6086, Signature Travel Tote, Signature Skinny Wristlet 8365). This is the closest thing the catalog offers to a 61st-anniversary acknowledgment — it is a SILENT heritage signal, not a celebration. Pattern of unmarked anniversaries continues (40 in 1981 silent, 45 in 1986 explicit Frankfort letter, 50 in 1990 explicit, 60 in 2001 silent, 61 in 2002 silent).

5. **Bleecker is GONE.** No Bleecker SKUs in the 2002 Autumn book. The 2001 Hampton-Bleecker pebbled-vs-glove duality has collapsed entirely into Hamptons. Closes the 3-era Bleecker timeline (1995-1997 Soho lightweight Bleeker → 1999 Fall Bleecker Tweed → 2001 Holiday glove-tanned expansion → retired by 2002 Autumn).

6. **"Coach Classics" heritage sub-collection is GONE.** No Willis (9927), no Court (9870), no Companion Flap (9076), no original Daypack (9791), no Madison Satchel (9725), no Duffle Sac in the 9085 traditional configuration as a "Classics" anchor. The catalog has no section titled "Coach Classics" or "Heritage." All late-NYC-era heritage silhouettes that survived through 2001 Holiday Preview are retired by 2002 Autumn.

7. **"Legacy West" debuts as a NEW heritage-styled sub-collection** — the Krakoff-era replacement for the old daypack/companion silhouettes. Legacy West Small Backpack (No. 9858, $238) and Legacy West Suede Small Backpack (No. 9878, $238) appear as "*new*" — explicitly framed in copy as "*A classic Coach style updated in rich suede and Fall's new neutrals*" with "Coach logo lining." Modern reinterpretation under a new brand label rather than original-style numbers — completes the formal end of vintage-era Coach as a current-season concept.

8. **Hippie Flap revival is GONE.** No Hippie Flap or Hippie-branded silhouette in 2002 Autumn. The 2001 Holiday Preview revival did not survive into the next Fall season — single-season revival.

9. **Krakoff is STILL not credited anywhere in the 2002 Autumn catalog.** No body copy, no colophon, no "Creative Director" signoff. Confirmed across cover, inside front, every spread, and back cover. Silent-creative-director pattern unbroken through end of in-scope era. **Full 2000-2002 in-scope window contains ZERO Krakoff colophon credits.**

10. **"Coach Bridle" as a handbag sub-collection is GONE,** but "Coach Bridle Classic Watch" survives as a vestigial accessory mention — confirming the Bridle name persisted only as a tertiary product label after the handbag line was retired.

11. **Coach Suede returns** as a fall-appropriate sub-collection (the seasonal pattern of suede-in-Fall is consistent with prior Krakoff-era catalogs).

12. **"Coach Textured Leather / Milbrook" debuts as NEW** — a textured pebbled-grain alternative to Hamptons smooth pebble. Paired with the new "Coach Tattersall / Milbrook Tattersall" plaid family.

13. **"Signature Chic" launches as NEW** — a dressier evening-oriented Signature spinoff with leather trim and metallic hardware accent.

14. **Mercer and Madison survive ONLY as eyewear names** — "Mercer Sunglasses S311" and "Madison Sunglasses S412" — confirming the handbag silhouettes were retired and the names recycled for the eyewear collection rather than left fallow.

15. **"NEW FALL FOOTWEAR... NOW IN MORE COACH STORES"** — back cover roundup shows footwear listings P058 / P002 / P008 / P010 plus J729 (J-prefix style number, possibly a boot or men's style). Footwear is positioned as an expanding category, consistent with the Krakoff-era lifestyle-brand pivot.

16. **"COACH.COM OR 1 800 223 8647" runs vertically in the page gutter** on multiple spreads — confirming e-commerce is now equal billing with phone ordering. The 2000 Holiday "ambient" www.coach.com URL has matured into co-equal channel signage.

### Brand voice (2002 Autumn)

- The catalog opens with cinematic large-type editorial spreads ("COACH BUSINESS FOR WOMEN," "YOUR SCHEDULE IS EVER-CHANGING. ALL THE MORE REASON TO START WITH THE PERFECT BAG. CARRY EVERYTHING YOU NEED FROM HOME TO OFFICE IN THE CLASSIC STYLE YOU'VE COME TO EXPECT WITH..."). Voice is aspirational/lifestyle, not artisanal/heritage.
- Heritage cues are IMAGE-BASED (the embossed "COACH LEATHERWARE EST. 1941" patch) rather than copy-based. No body paragraph references the date 1941, the founding, the 61st anniversary, Miles or Lillian Cahn, Bonnie Cashin, or any prior creative director.
- Sub-collection names lean on place/preppy associations ("Hamptons," "Milbrook," "Tattersall," "Legacy West") consistent with the Krakoff brand-identity playbook.

### Naming-convention drift (2002 Autumn)

- Style number conventions are stable: 4-digit handbag/SLG numbers (6086, 6873, 7785, 7937, 8365, 8991, 9858, 9878), P-prefix footwear (P002, P008, P010, P058), J-prefix footwear (J729 — a less-common variant), S-prefix sunglasses (S311, S412), C-prefix scarves.
- Sub-collection naming has shifted from heritage-craft language ("Court," "Willis," "Companion") to LIFESTYLE-PLACE language ("Hamptons," "Milbrook," "Legacy West," "Tattersall"). The drift away from utility-named silhouettes toward branded-place sub-collections is now complete.
