# Research stream M2 — Deep readings of 1989 catalogs

Compiled 2026-05-02. Continues from `research/M-catalogs-deep-readings.md`.
Primary-source readings of all five 1989 Coach catalogs (Spring Preview,
Summer Preview, Fall Preview, Winter, Holiday) — the **late NYC peak
era**, two years before Sara Lee's full operational integration began
shifting Coach toward the 1991 collection-diversification era.

The 1989 catalogs are decisive primary sources for several disputes:
- The **Lightweights Collection was already fully established by Spring
  1989** (not a 1991+ phenomenon — the archive's earlier framing was wrong).
- **Forest Green debuts Fall 1989** — answers the Hunter/Forest Green
  origin question.
- **Spectator Collection launched Fall 1989** — establishes the 6XXX
  prefix two years earlier than Holiday 1991 documented.
- **Continental Series launched Fall 1989** — Plaza Bag, Court Bag, and
  Regency Brief are NEW models added to the archive.
- **Sandstone introduced Summer 1989** — new color for archive.

## Spring Preview 1989 catalog

Catalog identifier: `1989-spring-preview.pdf` (24 pages)
Cover bag: **Tan crescent-flap shoulder bag** (likely Town Bag) with
silk scarf. Cover line: "Makers of fine leathergoods for men and women.
Spring Preview 1989."

### Brand voice — "more than 45 years"

> "Coach has been making fine quality leather products for **more than
> 45 years**. We are gratified that during this time they have become
> known as true American classics—straightforward, functional designs
> that are very well made and never go out of style."

1989 − 45 = **1944**. This is **looser dating** than the precise "since
1941" framing in 1991 ("Our concept since 1941"). Spring 1989's "more
than 45 years" allows for 1941–1944 windows. Consistent with the brand
itself being uncertain about its founding decade in this era —
authentic vagueness rather than a contradiction.

### Cover finding — Coach Tag philosophy spread

Page 8-9: "The smallest item we make here at Coach is actually one of
the most important... It is the Coach tag that hangs from every Coach
bag and briefcase. It is your assurance that the product has been made
by skilled craftsmen in our own factory. It also lets you know that
the item has been thoroughly hand-inspected before it is wrapped,
packed and offered for sale."

> "It is a tag that symbolizes a commitment to quality and
> craftsmanship, and there is no other bag in the world that carries
> it."

**The hangtag-as-authentication argument is a 1989 brand voice**, not
a later marketing addition. NEW finding for `authentication/hangtag.md`.

### Spring 1989 — Collection architecture (TOC)

- Lightweights Collection (pp 3-13)
- Gloves (pp 14-15)
- Classics Collection (pp 16-30)
- Scarves (pp 31-33)
- Men's & Women's Belts (pp 34-37)
- Men's & Women's Wallets & Accessories (pp 38-47)
- Diaries & Planners (pp 48-53)
- Men's & Women's Briefcases (pp 54-62)
- Small Accessories (pp 63-65)

**Two named bag-collections**: Classics and Lightweights. No Spectator,
no Continental Series, no Sheridan, no Madison, no Manhattan. The
Spring 1989 architecture is much simpler than the eight-collection
Fall 1992 architecture documented earlier.

### Spring 1989 color palettes (page 2)

**Coach Classics Colors (10)**:
Red, Putty, Navy, **Mahogany**, British Tan, Mocha, Bone, Tabac,
Burgundy, Black

**Coach Lightweights Colors (10)**:
Saddle, Red, Taupe, Blue, Bone, **Khaki**, Honey, Cocoa, Wine, Black

Both palettes are **distinct from each other**. Classics palette is
the traditional NYC palette (Mahogany already in Classics by Spring
1989 — confirms Mahogany pre-dates 1991). Lightweights uses softer
earth-tones (Saddle, Honey, Cocoa) and brighter accents (Wine, Khaki).

**Khaki is a Lightweights-only color** in Spring 1989 (later replaced
by Sandstone in Summer 1989 in some bags? Khaki disappears from
Lightweights palette starting Summer 1989).

### Lightweights brand voice — Spring 1989

> "As Spring approaches, the Coach Lightweights Collection expands.
> The full-grain, glove-tanned cowhide used in this versatile
> collection lends itself to a broad range of soft silhouettes that
> possess the strength and natural beauty you have come to expect
> from Coach."
>
> "Coach Lightweights are distinguished by their clean lines and
> soft, supple leather. Virtually seasonless, they bring new
> versatility to every woman's wardrobe."

Confirms Lightweights = "thinned glove-tanned cowhide" (consistent
with 1991 framing). Lightweights was an **established collection by
Spring 1989**, with the Spring catalog **expanding** it (new styles
Paris Bag, Town Bag, Town Bag Large, Tailored Pouch).

### Classics brand voice — Spring 1989

> that acquires a softer, richly burnished patina with continued use
> and care. The rich color range works effortlessly with your Spring
> wardrobe and through the seasons ahead."

### Spectator Bag (No. 9796) — pre-existing standalone, NOT yet a collection

Note: **In Spring 1989, "Spectator Bag" (9796) was a single Classic
Collection model in solid color**, NOT a sub-collection. The
Spectator-as-collection (with 6XXX prefix and contrasting trim) doesn't
launch until **Fall Preview 1989** (see below). This resolves a subtle
data point — the word "Spectator" was already in the Coach lexicon as
a single model name before becoming a collection name.

### "Classic Shoulder Bag" (9170) — "After more than two decades"

Spring 1989 catalog page 29 says of the Classic Shoulder Bag (9170):
> "After more than two decades, women still regard this bag—the
> original Coach bag—as an essential part of their Coach collection."

1989 − "more than two decades" = pre-1969. **The Classic Shoulder Bag
9170 is described as "the original Coach bag"**. NEW finding for
`models/classic-shoulder-bag.md` and the timeline of NYC-era models.
Note: this is potentially a Bonnie Cashin design — verify against
Cashin papers.

The hidden magnetic snap is **already in use Spring 1989** for
Lightweights, predating the 1991 catalogs by 2+ years. Update

### Lightweights palette refinement — Summer 1989 (9 colors)

Summer 1989 page 3 shows **9 Lightweights colors**: Saddle, Bone, Red,
Taupe, Blue, Honey, Cocoa, Wine, Black.

**Khaki is GONE** in Summer 1989 (was in Spring 1989). Khaki has been
removed from the Lightweights palette between Spring and Summer 1989.

### Construction detail — seam binding (1989)

Summer 1989 page 54-55 documents:
> "The seam binding of every Coach bag and briefcase is a detail that
> contributes to the distinctive Coach look as well as to the
> durability of our products. We use a special process to make this
> binding from the very same glove-tanned cowhide as the bag or
> briefcase itself. The seam binding is folded around every edge and
> sewn through several layers of leather. Creating the close, even
> stitches found on our fine leather-goods requires great skill and
> many years of experience."

The hand-folded seam binding diagnostic that appears in 1991 Holiday
catalog is **already in 1989 Summer catalog**. The diagnostic was
established in Coach's marketing language by 1989, not first
introduced in 1991. Update `authentication/stitching.md`.

### Materials section — Water Buffalo Calfskin (1989)

Summer 1989 page 4-5: "Coach Wallets and Accessories... Crafted of
Water Buffalo Calfskin to meet the demands of everyday use, they are
remarkably thin and soft, yet durable."

**Coach uses Water Buffalo Calfskin for wallets/accessories** as a
distinct material from Classics' glove-tanned cowhide. NEW finding —
the wallet leather is different from the bag leather. Update
`authentication/leather.md`.

> "SPORTY AND SPECIAL: THE NEW COACH SPECTATOR COLLECTION.

### Fall 1989 — Summary of architecture additions

The Fall Preview 1989 catalog introduces THREE new collection-level
constructs:
1. **Spectator Collection** (8 launch styles, 6XXX prefix)
2. **Continental Series** (4 launch styles)
3. **Forest Green** color (added to Classics palette)

single-season expansion** in any 1989 Coach catalog. The Fall 1989
season represents Coach's transition from a simple "Classics +
Lightweights" structure to a more elaborate sub-collection structure
that would mature into the eight-collection 1992 architecture.

## Cross-catalog findings — synthesis

### TIMELINE confirmations from 1989 catalogs

1. **Lightweights Collection** existed by Spring 1989 with **14+
   styles** — substantially predates Coach's commonly cited 1991+
   "Lightweights expansion" framing. The Lightweights brand and
   structure was already mature.

2. **Spectator Collection** launched **Fall 1989** with 8 styles
   (6XXX prefix, Tabac trim originally — later British Tan trim by
   1991). NEW timeline event.

3. **Continental Series** launched **Fall 1989** with 4 styles
   (Plaza Bag 9860, Plaza Bag Large 9865, Court Bag 9870, Regency
   Brief 5255). NEW timeline event.

4. **Forest Green** color introduced **Fall 1989** (Coach Classics
   Colors palette). Resolves Hunter Green / Forest Green dispute.

5. **Sandstone** color introduced **Summer 1989** (Coach Classics
   Colors palette).

The Fall 1989 expansion **doubled** the collection count of Coach in
a single season — the first major step toward the 1992-1993
multi-collection era.

### Founding-date language progression 1989

- **Spring 1989**: "more than 45 years" → ~1944 origin
- **Summer 1989**: (general "Coach quality" language)
- **Fall 1989**: (general)
- **Winter 1989**: "**For almost fifty years**" → ~1940 origin
- **Holiday 1989**: "**For almost fifty years**" (continued from
  Winter)

The shift from "more than 45" (Spring) to "almost fifty" (Winter)
reflects ~6 months of clock advancement but also a marketing decision
to emphasize the longer history. Both phrasings are consistent with
the documented 1941 founding.

### Specific things to look for — answers

- **Was the Lightweight Collection in 1989 catalogs?** YES —
  Lightweights was a fully established 14+-style collection by Spring
  1989. The framing of Lightweights as a 1991+ phenomenon was wrong.

- **Was Soho already there?** NO — Soho Bag (4082) is **NOT** in any
  1989 catalog. Soho appears to have launched between Fall 1989 and
  Holiday 1991. Likely 1990 or 1991 launch. The Lightweights styles
  in 1989 use 4XXX-prefix numbers (4040, 4055, 4065, 4070, 4075,
  4080, 4085, 4090, 4095, 4100, 4105, 4110, 4015, 4025) — with **no
  4082**. The 4082 number was assigned later.

- **Were any "Spectator" 6XXX styles documented?** YES — **8
  Spectator 6XXX styles** launched Fall 1989 (6375, 6750, 6765, 6790,
  6820, 6825, 6850, 6855). The 6XXX prefix convention launched Fall
  1989, two years earlier than the Holiday 1991 documentation. NEW
  timeline event.

- **Was Mahogany already in the palette?** YES — Mahogany is in the
  **Spring 1989 Classics palette** (10 colors: Red, Putty, Navy,
  **Mahogany**, British Tan, Mocha, Bone, Tabac, Burgundy, Black).
  Mahogany was an established Coach color by Spring 1989, predating
  the 1991 documented date.
