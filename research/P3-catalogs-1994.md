## Headline findings (partial)

1. **"Lightweight leather" persists as material descriptor through
   Early Autumn 1994** [coach-catalogs-1960-1989]. Both **Light Tote
   4065** ("lightweight leather, top zipper closure, 16" × 13" × 4½",
   British Tan/Black/Mahogany/Navy") and **Mini Cosmetic Case 7239**
   ("lightweight leather, hidden magnetic snap closure, 7" × 4" ×
   2⅛", British Tan/Black/Navy") are tagged "lightweight leather" in
   body copy. **This suggests the Lightweight → Soho rename had NOT
   happened by Early Autumn 1994** — but full verification requires
   reading the rest of the 1994 catalogs to confirm there's no
   "Soho" branded section elsewhere.

2. **NEW launches in Early Autumn 1994 Business Collection**:
   - **Hamilton Portfolio 5358** $424 — solid brass combination lock,
     16" × 11½" × 2½", Bottle Green/Black/British Tan/Mahogany/Red
   - **Ambassador Brief 5357** $496 — solid brass combination lock,
     16" × 11½" × 3½", Mahogany/Black/Bottle Green/British Tan/Red
   - **Zip-Around Organizer Large 5223** $215 — three-hole punch
     portfolio, 1995 day-to-a-page year planner, 1996 fold-out
     year-at-a-view, Black/British Tan
   - The sequential 5357/5358 numbers suggest a Fall 1994 release wave.

3. **Carry-All Styles section NEW**: **Dowel Field Bag 9940** $324 —
   double flap turnlock closures, 13¾" × 10½" × 4½",
   British Tan/Black/Bordeaux/Mahogany. The 9940 is a significant
   addition to the Classic-line.

4. **Toll-free + customer service number transition mid-1994**: the
   order form has Customer Service **(800) 444-3611 handwritten** as
   an override over a printed **(203) 460-4716**. The 800 number was
   newer than the print run — toll-free Customer Service rolled out
   mid-1994 [coach-catalogs-1960-1989]. Adjacent: **fax line (203)
   553-1422** continues from earlier 1990 Fall debut.

5. **Coach Consumer Service address Carlstadt NJ**: "Coach Consumer
   Service / 410 Commerce Boulevard / Carlstadt NJ 07072-3099"
   [coach-catalogs-1960-1989]. First explicit Carlstadt-NJ address
   in deep-read corpus.

The Business Collection is a primary-confirmed sub-collection in
1994 Early Autumn — alongside the Lightweight Collection (still
named) and unspecified other lines. Per O3 (1990 Holiday), the

Lightweight / Classic) had crystallized; the 1994 catalogs
continue this architecture.
