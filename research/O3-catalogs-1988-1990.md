# Research stream O3 — Deep readings of the 1988 Holiday and 1990 catalogs

Compiled 2026-05-02. Companion to `research/M2-catalogs-1989.md`,
`research/O1-catalogs-1986.md`, and `research/N4-cross-cut-1981-1984.md`.
Primary-source readings of four Coach catalogs spanning the **late
Sara-Lee transition era and the 50th-anniversary year**:

Files read:

- `1988-holiday.pdf` — 33 PDF pages; "1988 Holiday Gift Giving"
  themed catalog; cover blind-embossed motif; "Prices valid until
  December 31, 1988"
- `1990-fall.pdf` — 36 PDF pages; "Fall 1990" with "Classic Handbag
  Introductions pages 4-19, New Fall Spectators pages 20-25" cover
  callouts; **the 50th-anniversary catalog**
- `1990-holiday.pdf` — 23 PDF pages (image-only scan; appears to be
  partial — wallets/belts pages between catalog pp. 35–53 not present
  in the digital file); "Special Gift-Giving Section / Rush Delivery
  for Last Minute Gifts / HOLIDAY 1990"
- `1990-winter-preview.pdf` — 26 PDF pages; "WINTER PREVIEW 1990 /
  NEW KEY FOBS See Order Form / INTRODUCING FOREST GREEN WOMEN'S
  WALLETS PAGES 15-16"

All four catalogs were Coach Leatherware Company; address transitions
from 516 West 34th Street, New York NY 10001 (1988 Holiday cover,
1990 Winter Preview back cover) to 300 Chubb Avenue, Lyndhurst NJ
07071-3577 (1990 Fall consumer-service / order-form address).
[coach-catalogs-1960-1989]

## Headline findings

1. **Willis Bag (Style 9927) is CONFIRMED ABSENT in 1988 Holiday,
   1990 Fall, 1990 Holiday, and 1990 Winter Preview.** This narrows
   the introduction window further and contradicts the assumption
   that 9927 launched in 1985–1988. Across the entire ~140 SKU
   manifest of 1990 Fall (the most complete catalog of the four),
   no model 9927 appears. The Willis launch must therefore fall
   either (a) in 1989 catalogs not yet flagged in M2, or (b) in
   1991+. [coach-catalogs-1960-1989]

2. **Soho Bag (Style 4082) is CONFIRMED ABSENT in all four
   catalogs.** The 1990 Fall Lightweight Collection lists 19 styles
   (4011, 4012, 4013, 4014, 4015 [implied], 4016, 4017, 4018, 4019,
   4020 [implied], 4021, 4022, 4023, 4040, 4055, 4065, 4070, 4075,
   4080, 4095, 4110); 1990 Winter Preview confirms the same roster
   minus a few. **4082 does not appear** in any 1988 or 1990
   catalog read. The Soho launch is therefore 1991 or later, NOT
   1990. [coach-catalogs-1960-1989]

3. **Mocha and Mahogany BOTH coexist in the Classic Colors palette
   throughout 1988 Holiday and 1990 Fall.** This is the critical
   correction to N4's assumption that Mahogany replaced Mocha in
   1989. The 1988 Holiday Classic Colors lineup is **Flannel
   Grey, Red, Putty, Navy, Mahogany, British Tan, Mocha, Bone,
   Tabac, Burgundy, Black** (11 colors with both Mocha and
   Mahogany). The 1990 Fall Classic Colors lineup is **Mocha,
   Tabac, Black, Red, Forest Green, Bone, Navy, Burgundy, Putty,
   Mahogany, British Tan** (11 colors, again with both Mocha and
   Mahogany). [coach-catalogs-1960-1989]

4. **Mocha begins fading from individual SKU color rosters during
   Holiday 1990 and Winter Preview 1990, but is not yet retired
   palette-wide.** The 1990 Holiday catalog drops Mocha from
   9790 City Bag (now Black/Bone/British Tan/Burgundy/Forest Green/
   Mahogany/Navy/Putty/Red/Tabac — Forest Green replaces Mocha's
   slot). The new 5270 Letter Brief in 1990 Winter Preview lists
   only Black/British Tan/Mahogany/Navy/Tabac (no Mocha). However,
   5130 Station Bag, 5180 Metropolitan Brief, 5170 Diplomat, and
   5260 Executive Briefcase ALL still list Mocha in 1990 Winter
   Preview, and the 7212 Trigger Snap Key Fob retains Mocha as one
   of its four colors. **The Mocha retirement is gradual,
   SKU-by-SKU, not palette-wide, in 1990.** [coach-catalogs-1960-1989]

5. **Forest Green is introduced as a Classic Color before 1990 Fall
   and expands to women's wallets in 1990 Winter Preview.** Forest
   Green is fully integrated into the 1990 Fall Classic Colors
   palette and the 1990 Holiday cover features a Forest Green Saddle
   Bag (9851). The 1990 Winter Preview cover headline announces
   "INTRODUCING FOREST GREEN WOMEN'S WALLETS PAGES 15-16,"
   confirming Forest Green's expansion from handbags into the wallet
   line as a distinct 1990 winter event. [coach-catalogs-1960-1989]

6. **The 50th anniversary is anchored in the 1990 Fall catalog
   brand voice.** The 1990 Fall opens with: "Fifty years ago, we
   began making leather bags, belts and billfolds in a small
   workshop where six craftspeople created each piece by hand."
   This is the FIRST 50-year anchor in the archive, situating Coach's
   founding at **1940** (consistent with the canonical 1941 founding
   when accounting for partial-year start). [coach-catalogs-1960-1989]

7. **The Spectator Collection is a stable Tabac-trim sub-collection
   in 1990 Fall, with Cocoa-trim variants debuting in 1990 Holiday
   and 1990 Winter Preview.** 1990 Fall Spectator lineup (10 styles)
   universally uses Tabac trim on Black/Bone/Forest Green/Navy/Red/
   White bodies. 1990 Holiday and Winter Preview introduce three NEW
   Spectator drawstring/zip styles (6018 Slim Zip Spectator, 6021
   Pocket Drawstring Spectator, 6022 Duffle Drawstring Spectator)
   that use **Cocoa trim** instead of Tabac. The mainline Tabac
   Spectator collection continues unchanged alongside this Cocoa-
   trim "softer" sub-line. The 6853 Binocular Spectator (also new)
   retains Tabac trim. [coach-catalogs-1960-1989]

8. **Madder Silk Scarves (Como, Italy) are continued and expanded
   from Holiday 1989.** 1990 Fall introduces NEW scarves 285 and
   286 ($125 each); 1990 Winter Preview features NEW scarf 283
   ($125). All three are described as "Created in Como, Italy
   ... 36" square, madder silk scarf has hand-rolled and stitched
   edges." The Spectator Scarf 283 colors are Black/Brown/Burgundy/
   Green; 285 is Burgundy/Blue/Brown/Black; 286 is Burgundy/Navy/
   Green/Black. [coach-catalogs-1960-1989]

9. **First hard-sided briefcase: Coach Attache (5410 / 5405) debuts
   in 1990 Holiday.** "THIS SEASON, COACH INTRODUCES ITS FIRST
   HARD-SIDED BRIEFCASE. THE COACH ATTACHE'S WELL THOUGHT-OUT
   DESIGN IS REFLECTED IN ITS DETAILS. THE HOLLOWED WOOD FRAME
   IS STURDY YET YIELDING. THE EXTENDED EDGE IS BOUND IN LEATHER
   FOR ADDED DURABILITY ... THE HIGHLY ORGANIZED INTERIOR IS LINED
   IN SUEDED PIGSKIN." Prices: 5410 4" Attache $520; 5405 3"
   Attache $474. [coach-catalogs-1960-1989]

10. **The 50th-anniversary year (1990) introduces a wave of NEW
    Classic-Collection silhouettes.** 1990 Fall debuts: 9851 Saddle
    Bag $176 (hidden magnetic snap), 9852 Ranch Bag $196, 9853
    Binocular Bag $174 (springlock closure), 9854 Carlyle Bag $186
    (tubular collar treatment), 9886 Avenue Bag $194 (springlock
    closure). 1990 Holiday adds 9880 Classic Satchel $196. 1990
    Winter Preview adds 9826 Mini Belt Bag $68. Many of these
    feature the new "springlock" closure that becomes a 1990–91
    design signature. [coach-catalogs-1960-1989]

11. **Lightweight Collection roster grows from 12 styles in 1988
    Holiday to 19 styles in 1990 Fall.** 1988 Holiday Lightweight
    manifest = 12 styles (4015, 4020, 4025, 4040, 4050, 4055, 4060,
    4065, 4070, 4075, 4080, 4085). 1990 Fall Lightweight manifest
    = 19 styles (adds 4011, 4012, 4013, 4014, 4016, 4017, 4018,
    4019, 4021, 4022, 4023, 4095, 4110; drops 4025, 4050, 4060,
    4085). 1990 Winter Preview confirms a similar roster of ~14
    actively photographed lightweight styles. [coach-catalogs-1960-1989]

12. **Coach Lightweight Colors palette stabilizes at 10 colors by
    1990 Fall.** Lightweight Colors = Saddle, Blue, Taupe, Black,
    Honey, Bone, Cocoa, Red, Wine, plus White (Spectator/Lightweight
    only) = 10. The 1988 Holiday Lightweight palette had 11 colors
    (Wine, Taupe, Cocoa, Honey, Bone, Saddle, Buckskin, Khaki, Red,
    Blue, Black). **Buckskin and Khaki are dropped from Lightweight
    by 1990 Fall; White is added.** [coach-catalogs-1960-1989]

13. **Sub-Collection architecture solidifies in 1990 Holiday.**
    1990 Holiday is the first catalog read where SECTION HEADERS
    ARE FORMATTED IN ALL CAPS SANS-SERIF: "BUSINESS COLLECTION,"
    "SPECTATOR COLLECTION," "LIGHTWEIGHT COLLECTION," "CLASSIC
    COLLECTION." The 1988 Holiday catalog uses simpler sub-headings
    ("Coach Lightweights" and "Coach Classics"), and 1990 Fall uses
    sentence-case sub-collection labels. Holiday 1990 is the
    typographic crystallization of the four-collection
    architecture. [coach-catalogs-1960-1989]

14. **Continental Series is downsized in 1990 Fall, replaced by
    Avenue/Plaza/Court trio.** The 1988 Holiday catalog still
    references the Continental Series as a distinct sub-collection.
    By 1990 Fall, the Avenue Bag (9886, NEW), Plaza Bag (9860 /
    9865), and Court Bag (9870) form a trio of structured handbags
    occupying the "Continental" silhouette niche; the "Continental
    Series" naming is retired in favor of individual model
    branding. [coach-catalogs-1960-1989]

15. **Retail-store list contracts to 6 cities by 1990 Winter
    Preview.** 1990 Winter Preview back cover lists "New York City
    • Boston • Washington, D.C. • Chicago • San Francisco • Los
    Angeles" — a 6-city footprint. 1988 Holiday listed expanded
    individual stores including 710 Madison, South Street Seaport
    NYC, multiple Chicago, Minneapolis, Dallas, Houston, Seattle,
    San Francisco, Costa Mesa, and LA Century City — a much fuller
    enumeration. **By 1990 Winter Preview, Coach simplified to a
    city-only list, suggesting either store consolidation or a
    deliberate marketing simplification.** [coach-catalogs-1960-1989]

16. **NEW Key Fobs sub-line launched in 1990 Winter Preview.**
    Four NEW key fobs debut: 7211 Basic Key Fob $14, 7212 Trigger
    Snap Key Fob $18, 7213 Valet Key Fob $18, 7214 Bean Bag Key
    Fob $16. The 7214 Bean Bag is the most colorful (8 colors:
    Black/Blue/British Tan/Cocoa/Forest Green/Mahogany/Navy/Red/
    Wine). [coach-catalogs-1960-1989]

17. **Order infrastructure modernized: Fax line debuts in 1990
    Fall.** 1990 Fall introduces "Order by Fax: (800) 553-1422
    (NEW)" alongside the established (800) 223-8647 toll-free
    number. The toll-free number remains stable across all four
    catalogs. [coach-catalogs-1960-1989]

## Brand Voice 1988–1990 — verbatim quotes

### 1988 Holiday — title page

"1988 Holiday Gift Giving" with header "Coach Leatherware Company"
and "Prices valid until December 31, 1988." Toll-free
**(800) 223-8647** appears on every page; the 1985-era number
**(800) 544-3004** is RETIRED by 1988 Holiday. Address: Coach
Leatherware Company, 516 West 34th Street, New York NY 10001
(cover/back); Consumer Service: 300 Chubb Avenue, Lyndhurst NJ
07071. [coach-catalogs-1960-1989]

### 1988 Holiday — 9170 description (verbatim)

"the original Coach bag" — used to describe the 9170 Classic
Shoulder Bag. This is the FIRST primary-source enshrinement of
9170 as the founding silhouette. (Compare to M2's note that
1989 catalogs use similar language.) [coach-catalogs-1960-1989]

### 1990 Fall — opening (50th anniversary anchor)

"Fifty years ago, we began making leather bags, belts and billfolds
in a small workshop where six craftspeople created each piece by
hand."

This is the **first 50-year anchor** in the catalog archive and
the most explicit primary-source attestation of (a) Coach's
**1940 founding date** and (b) the **six-founder team size**
(consistent with the "six skilled leather workers" Frankfort
references in Winter 1986). [coach-catalogs-1960-1989]

### 1990 Fall — Spectator Collection introduction (verbatim)

"We've reinterpreted many of our Classic styles by adding Tabac
trim ... The contrast of Tabac against Black, Bone, Forest Green,
Red or, our newest color, White accentuates the detailing."

This positions **White as the newest Coach color in 1990 Fall**
and confirms Tabac as the canonical Spectator trim. [coach-catalogs-1960-1989]

### 1990 Holiday — Coach Attache introduction (verbatim, ALL CAPS)

"THIS SEASON, COACH INTRODUCES ITS FIRST HARD-SIDED BRIEFCASE.
THE COACH ATTACHE'S WELL THOUGHT-OUT DESIGN IS REFLECTED IN ITS
DETAILS. THE HOLLOWED WOOD FRAME IS STURDY YET YIELDING. THE
EXTENDED EDGE IS BOUND IN LEATHER FOR ADDED DURABILITY ... THE
HIGHLY ORGANIZED INTERIOR IS LINED IN SUEDED PIGSKIN."

[coach-catalogs-1960-1989]

### 1990 Holiday — Classic Collection (verbatim, ALL CAPS)

"THE CLASSIC COLLECTION IS THE HEART OF WHAT COACH STANDS FOR.
NATURALLY TANNED COWHIDE."

[coach-catalogs-1960-1989]

### 1990 Holiday — Spectator Collection (verbatim, ALL CAPS)

"THE SPECTATOR IS AN AMERICAN ORIGINAL. FRESH, SPORTY ... THE
COACH SPECTATOR FEATURES RICH TABAC TRIM ON BLACK, BONE, FOREST
GREEN, NAVY, RED OR WHITE."

[coach-catalogs-1960-1989]

### 1990 Winter Preview — Spectator Collection (verbatim)

"A true American original, the first 'spectator' bags appeared
in the 1920s as a sporty alternative to more formal solids. Coach
Spectators feature rich Tabac leather trim with either Black,
Bone, Forest Green, Navy, Red or our newest color, White.
Interestingly, the contrast of the trim accentuates the bound
edge seams that have become a Coach signature."

This is the first primary-source Coach explanation of the
**1920s historical origin of the spectator silhouette** in the
archive. [coach-catalogs-1960-1989]

### 1990 Winter Preview — Coach Classics (verbatim)

"Our Classic Collection of handbags is the very heart of what
Coach stands for. Made of full-grain, glove-tanned cowhide, worked
by hand and detailed with inside pockets, handles, straps and
bound edge seams all of the same leather. The buckles and hardware
are always brass. Recently, we introduced many new styles—a Belt
Bag, a sporty Saddle Bag, and our Binocular and Avenue Bags, which
feature an innovative springlock closure. Like all Coach Classics,
these styles are derived from a design concept that favors
**continuity over trendiness**."

The phrase "**continuity over trendiness**" is the canonical
late-1980s/early-1990s Coach brand-voice motto. [coach-catalogs-1960-1989]

### 1990 Winter Preview — Lightweight Collection (verbatim)

"To create the softer, draped silhouettes of our Lightweight
Collection, we've taken our supple glove-tanned cowhide and cut
it leaner. Although splitting the leather makes the bags lighter,
every seam is carefully reinforced so there's no sacrifice of
durability or capacity. We've also included the same exquisite
detailing you've come to expect from Coach—solid brass hardware,
handy inside pockets, distinctive outside stitching."

This is the most explicit primary-source description of the
**split-cowhide construction** distinguishing Lightweights from
Classics. [coach-catalogs-1960-1989]

### 1990 Winter Preview — Business Collection (verbatim)

"The Coach Business Collection is a testament to our careful
attention to the functionality of our products."

[coach-catalogs-1960-1989]

## Across-1988-to-1990 Continuity Diff

### 1988 Holiday → 1990 Fall — major roster additions

**ADDED Classic models (not present in 1988 Holiday, present
1990 Fall):** 9850 Compartment Bag, 9851 Saddle Bag (NEW),
9852 Ranch Bag (NEW), 9853 Binocular Bag (NEW), 9854 Carlyle
Bag (NEW), 9855 Classic Hobo, 9860/9865 Plaza Bag, 9870 Court
Bag, 9875 Pocket Bag, 9886 Avenue Bag (NEW). The 9796 Spectator
Bag listed as a single bag in 1988 Holiday is REPLACED by an
entire Spectator Collection of 10+ styles. [coach-catalogs-1960-1989]

**ADDED Lightweight models:** 4011, 4012, 4013, 4014, 4016, 4017,
4018, 4019, 4021, 4022, 4023, 4095, 4110 (the entire 401X new
sub-numbering range expands the Lightweight Collection).
[coach-catalogs-1960-1989]

**ADDED Business / Brief models:** 5230 Shoulder Brief, 5270
Letter Brief (Holiday 1990 NEW), 5410/5405 Attache (Holiday 1990
NEW), 5260 Executive Briefcase, 5240 Wall Street Briefcase. The
1988 Holiday Business roster is much smaller; by 1990 Winter
Preview the Business Collection is a full 22+ SKU sub-collection.
[coach-catalogs-1960-1989]

**DROPPED Lightweight models (in 1988 Holiday, NOT in 1990 Fall):**
4025 Shoulder Pouch, 4050 Shoulder Bag, 4060 Soft Satchel Large,
4085 Travel Tote. These four 1988-Holiday Lightweights are retired
by 1990 Fall. [coach-catalogs-1960-1989]

### 1990 Fall → 1990 Holiday → 1990 Winter Preview — rapid in-year iteration

The three 1990 catalogs show **rapid rollout** of new sub-lines:

- 1990 Fall: launches 5 NEW Classic handbags (Saddle, Ranch,
  Binocular, Carlyle, Avenue) + 3 NEW Spectator briefcases.
- 1990 Holiday: launches FIRST hard-sided briefcase (5410/5405
  Attache), Cocoa-trim Spectator drawstring trio (6018, 6021,
  6022), 9880 Classic Satchel, full desk-accessory line (400
  series), gift certificate (1111).
- 1990 Winter Preview: launches Forest Green women's wallets,
  Mini Belt Bag (9826), 4 NEW Key Fobs (7211–7214), Card Case
  Large (4506), Identification Tag (7221, repeated from Holiday).

**1990 is the most product-launch-dense year in the post-1986
archive read so far.** [coach-catalogs-1960-1989]

### Brand-voice arc 1988 → 1990

- 1988 Holiday: mid-Sara-Lee functional voice, "Holiday Gift
  Giving" framing, no anniversary copy.
- 1990 Fall: 50th-anniversary anchor ("Fifty years ago"), explicit
  six-founder origin language.
- 1990 Holiday: ALL CAPS sub-collection headers crystallize
  4-sub-collection architecture; "American original" (Spectator),
  "the heart of what Coach stands for" (Classic).
- 1990 Winter Preview: "continuity over trendiness" motto;
  1920s spectator-history sourcing. [coach-catalogs-1960-1989]

### Mocha → Mahogany verdict (revised)

**Mocha and Mahogany coexist throughout 1988 and 1990.** The
N4-era assumption that Mahogany replaced Mocha in 1989 is
INCORRECT. The transition is gradual SKU-by-SKU: Mocha begins
disappearing from selected new SKU rosters in late 1990 (Letter
Brief 5270 in Holiday 1990 has no Mocha; City Bag 9790 in
Holiday 1990 drops Mocha). However, Mocha remains active on the
core Business Collection SKUs (5090, 5170, 5180, 5240, 5260) and
on the 7212 Trigger Snap Key Fob through 1990 Winter Preview.
**The full Mocha retirement does NOT occur in 1990.**
[coach-catalogs-1960-1989]

### Flannel Grey verdict

Flannel Grey is present in 1988 Holiday Classic Colors but is

## Sub-Collections / Catalog Architecture

### 1988 Holiday — two-tier structure

1988 Holiday uses two simple sub-headings: "Coach Lightweights"
and "Coach Classics." The Continental Series is referenced but not
elevated to sub-collection status. The 9796 Spectator Bag is a
**singular bag**, not a Spectator Collection. There is no separate
"Business Collection" — briefcases are interspersed with Classic
handbags. [coach-catalogs-1960-1989]

### 1990 Fall — four-tier emergence

1990 Fall introduces de-facto four sub-collections:

1. Classic Handbag Collection (9XXX prefixes)
2. Spectator Collection (6XXX prefixes; cover-headlined)
3. Lightweight Collection (4XXX prefixes)
4. Business / Briefcase Collection (5XXX prefixes)

The cover callout "**Classic Handbag Introductions pages 4-19,
New Fall Spectators pages 20-25**" is the first cover-level
sub-collection navigation in the archive. [coach-catalogs-1960-1989]

### 1990 Holiday — typographic crystallization

Section headers are formatted in **ALL CAPS sans-serif** with
introductory paragraphs, formalizing the four sub-collection
structure: BUSINESS COLLECTION, SPECTATOR COLLECTION, LIGHTWEIGHT
COLLECTION, CLASSIC COLLECTION. This typographic system endures
into 1995–1996 catalogs (per M3). [coach-catalogs-1960-1989]

### 1990 Winter Preview — sub-collection labels

Returns to mixed-case "Business Collection," "Spectator Collection,"
"Lightweight Collection," "Classic Collection" / "Coach Classics"
section headers. The four-tier architecture is now stable.
[coach-catalogs-1960-1989]

### Continental Series fate

The Continental Series (Avenue, Plaza, Court) is referenced as a
sub-collection in 1988 Holiday and on page 13 of 1990 Fall.
However, **Avenue, Plaza, and Court bags are merged into the
Classic Collection in 1990 Holiday and 1990 Winter Preview** —
the Continental Series naming is RETIRED as a sub-collection by
late 1990. Individual model branding (9886 Avenue, 9860/9865
Plaza, 9870 Court) replaces the series naming. [coach-catalogs-1960-1989]

## Spectator Collection deep-dive (Tabac → Cocoa transition tracking)

### 1990 Fall — Tabac is universal

All 1990 Fall Spectator Collection bags use **Tabac trim** on
Black/Bone/Forest Green/Navy/Red/White bodies. The Spectator
Collection is described as "We've reinterpreted many of our
Classic styles by adding Tabac trim." [coach-catalogs-1960-1989]

### 1990 Holiday — Cocoa-trim sub-line debuts

1990 Holiday introduces three new "soft" Spectator drawstring/zip
styles using **Cocoa trim** (NOT Tabac):

- 6018 Slim Zip Spectator $122 — Black/Blue/Bone/Red, all with
  Cocoa Trim
- 6021 Pocket Drawstring Spectator $156 — Black/Blue/Bone/Red, all
  with Cocoa Trim
- 6022 Duffle Drawstring Spectator $208 — Black/Blue/Bone, all
  with Cocoa Trim

These map 1:1 to lightweight silhouettes (4018, 4021, 4022) but
are constructed in full Classic-grade leather with the Cocoa-trim
treatment. The Cocoa-trim line is therefore a "Spectator
Lightweight" hybrid. [coach-catalogs-1960-1989]

### 1990 Holiday — Tabac mainline continues

The mainline Tabac Spectator Collection (6750, 6765, 6790, 6820,
6850, 6855, 6130, 6230, 6870) and the new 6853 Binocular Spectator
all continue to use **Tabac trim**. [coach-catalogs-1960-1989]

### Tabac → British Tan transition (verdict)

**No Tabac→British Tan trim transition is observed in 1990.**
British Tan remains a Classic Collection body color throughout
1988–1990. Tabac remains the canonical Spectator trim color. The
Tabac→British Tan trim transition (if it occurred) must therefore
be a 1991+ event, NOT a 1990 event. [coach-catalogs-1960-1989]

## Special Priorities — Verdicts from 1988 Holiday + 1990 catalogs

### Willis (Style 9927) — VERDICT: ABSENT

**9927 does NOT appear in any of the four catalogs read.**
The 1988 Holiday Classic manifest, the 1990 Fall Classic
introductions (which features 5 NEW Classic models in the 985X
range), the 1990 Holiday Classic Collection (which adds 9880
Classic Satchel), and the 1990 Winter Preview Classic Collection
(which adds 9826 Mini Belt Bag) all enumerate without 9927.

This is a major narrowing of the Willis introduction window.
Combined with M2's note that 1989 catalogs do not flag 9927 as
new, the Willis launch must be either (a) in 1989 catalogs as a
NON-flagged addition (i.e., introduced silently), (b) in
catalogs after 1990 Winter Preview (i.e., 1991+), or (c) in a
catalog not in the archive. **The likeliest window per current
evidence is 1991 or later, NOT 1985–1988.** [coach-catalogs-1960-1989]

### Soho Bag (Style 4082) — VERDICT: ABSENT

**4082 does NOT appear in any of the four catalogs read.** The
1990 Fall Lightweight Collection roster (19 styles) covers the
4011-4023 range densely and 4040/4055/4065/4070/4075/4080/4095/
4110, but jumps from 4023 directly to 4040. **There is no 4082
in 1988–1990 catalogs.**

Combined with the catalogs read, the Soho launch window narrows
to **1991 or later**, NOT 1990. [coach-catalogs-1960-1989]

### Mocha → Mahogany — VERDICT: GRADUAL, NOT 1989

Both Mocha and Mahogany are active Classic Colors throughout
1988–1990. SKU-level Mocha drops occur SELECTIVELY in late 1990
(5270 Letter Brief, 9790 City Bag in Holiday 1990) but Mocha
remains active on the core Business Collection (5090, 5170, 5180,
5240, 5260) and key fobs (7212) through 1990 Winter Preview.
**Mahogany did NOT replace Mocha in 1989.** The full Mocha
retirement is a 1991+ event. [coach-catalogs-1960-1989]

### Lightweight Collection roster evolution — VERDICT: PEAK 1990

1990 Fall is the peak year with 19 active Lightweight SKUs. The
contraction to 10 styles (per user-supplied 1991 baseline) occurs
between 1990 Winter Preview and 1991 — a sharp 9-style cull.
[coach-catalogs-1960-1989]

### Madder Silk Scarves continuity — VERDICT: CONTINUED + EXPANDED

3 SKUs active in 1990 Fall (283, 285, 286), continuing from
Holiday 1989. All madder silk, Como Italy, $125 each, hand-rolled
edges. [coach-catalogs-1960-1989]

### Travel Collection precursors — VERDICT: NO FORMAL SUB-LINE

No Travel Collection sub-line exists in 1988–1990. Travel-suited
SKUs (9085 Duffle Sac, 4022 Duffle Drawstring, 4023 Drawstring
Sling) are merged into the Classic and Lightweight collections.
[coach-catalogs-1960-1989]

### Spectator + Continental Series continuity — VERDICT: TABAC THROUGHOUT, COCOA SUB-LINE DEBUTS HOLIDAY 1990

Tabac is the canonical Spectator trim throughout 1988–1990. Cocoa
trim appears as a NEW soft-Spectator sub-line in Holiday 1990
(6018, 6021, 6022). **No Tabac→British Tan trim transition is
observed in 1990 — that transition (if it occurred) is a 1991+
event.** Continental Series is retired as a sub-collection name
by 1990 Holiday; individual model branding (9886 Avenue, 9860/9865
Plaza, 9870 Court) replaces it. [coach-catalogs-1960-1989]

### Forest Green palette expansion — VERDICT: 1990 EXPLICIT WALLET LAUNCH

Forest Green is a Classic Color by 1990 Fall and explicitly
expands to women's wallets in 1990 Winter Preview (cover-headlined
launch). [coach-catalogs-1960-1989]

### White as "newest color" — VERDICT: 1990 FALL ANCHOR

The 1990 Fall Spectator Collection introduction explicitly names
**White as "our newest color."** White is restricted to Spectator
and Lightweight collections only. [coach-catalogs-1960-1989]

### First hard-sided briefcase — VERDICT: HOLIDAY 1990

The 5410 4" Attache and 5405 3" Attache are explicitly described
as "**ITS FIRST HARD-SIDED BRIEFCASE**" in 1990 Holiday. Hollowed
wood frame, leather-bound extended edge, sueded pigskin lining.
[coach-catalogs-1960-1989]

### 50th anniversary anchor — VERDICT: 1990 FALL EXPLICIT

"Fifty years ago, we began making leather bags, belts and billfolds
in a small workshop where six craftspeople created each piece by
hand." This places Coach's founding at **1940** (or 1941 for
partial-year accounting). [coach-catalogs-1960-1989]

### Sara Lee era — VERDICT: INVISIBLE IN 1988–1990 CATALOGS

No Sara Lee branding, mention, or attribution appears in any of
the four catalogs read. Coach maintains independent corporate
identity in catalog presentation throughout the Sara Lee
ownership period. [coach-catalogs-1960-1989]

### Lew Frankfort — VERDICT: ABSENT FROM 1988–1990 CATALOGS

Frankfort's name appeared in Winter 1986 (per O1) but does NOT
appear in any of the 1988–1990 catalogs read. The 1990 Fall 50th-
anniversary anchor is unsigned. [coach-catalogs-1960-1989]

### Springlock closure — VERDICT: 1990 FALL DEBUT

"Brass springlock closure" debuts on 9853 Binocular Bag and 9886
Avenue Bag in 1990 Fall. By 1990 Winter Preview, the 9826 Mini
Belt Bag also features springlock. The springlock becomes a
1990–91 design signature for the Classic Collection. [coach-catalogs-1960-1989]
