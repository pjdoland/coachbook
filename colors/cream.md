# Cream

## Lead

Cream is a light off-white neutral in the Coach palette, sitting
alongside Bone, Putty, and Khaki in the lights-and-neutrals family
[yourgreatfinds-collectors-guide]. Cream is referenced in collector
listings as a body color and appears in some two-tone Coach
combinations paired with Black or contrasting trim; the per-model
catalog of Cream offerings is not yet compiled.

## Description

Cream reads as a warm, slightly yellow-tinged off-white on
glove-tanned cowhide — distinct from the cooler, more neutral Bone
and the warmer, beige-leaning Putty. On Coach's drum-dyed-through
NYC-era leather (see
[authentication/leather.md](../authentication/leather.md)), Cream's
cut edge should match the surface color, though light-colored leather
makes the edge-color check less visually obvious than on darker
colors.

Light-colored Coach bags develop visible patina and edge wear faster
than dark colors; a Cream bag with no surface darkening on handles or
corners is unlikely to be a vintage example with significant use.

## Authentication

- Cream should pass the standard drum-dyed-through edge-color check,
  though the visual contrast on light leather is reduced (see
  authentication/leather.md). Inspect the edge against a dark
  reference for the cleanest read
- Cream is easily confused with Bone, Putty, and Khaki in photos;
  matching to a specific Coach color name should rely on a catalog
  page or dealer-corroborated identification rather than photo color
  alone
- Yellowing or staining on a Cream bag is age- or environment-related
  rather than an authenticity signal; original Cream is a clean warm
  off-white

## Era anchoring

Cream persists across the NYC era through the transition era. Color
alone never dates a bag — combine with creed wording, serial format,
hardware, and country-of-origin (see [authentication/](../authentication/))
for era placement.
