# Khaki

## Lead

Khaki is a Coach color name for a light olive / muted tan neutral,
sitting alongside Bone and Putty in the NYC-era light-neutral
palette but distinguished by its olive or greenish-tan undertone
[yourgreatfinds-collectors-guide]. Coverage in working research is
thin — the strongest anchor is the Pocket Purse "black, bone,
khaki" colorway list.

## Description

Khaki reads as a light olive-tan — warmer and greener than Bone,
cooler and less yellow than Camel, and noticeably less saturated
than Saddle. On Coach's glove-tanned cowhide the color is
drum-dyed through (the cut edge shows the same color as the
surface — see [authentication/leather.md](../authentication/leather.md)),
so a Khaki bag's edges and worn corners stay the same olive-tan
rather than revealing a contrasting under-color.

Khaki's olive cast is the diagnostic against the rest of the
neutral palette. A "tan" Coach bag with a clear green or
gray-green undertone is more likely Khaki than Camel or Bone.

## Models documented in Khaki

The following is documented in Khaki in research/D-models.md
[yourgreatfinds-collectors-guide]:

- **Pocket Purse** — research/D-models.md notes "black, bone,
  khaki documented"

This is the only model with a Khaki colorway anchored in current
working files. Khaki was likely offered on additional NYC-era
small leather goods and possibly larger silhouettes, but the
per-model catalog is open and should be expanded as catalog scans
are added.

## Authentication

- Khaki should pass the standard drum-dyed-through edge-color
  check: the cut edge reads the same olive-tan as the surface
  (see
  [authentication/leather.md](../authentication/leather.md))
- Khaki's olive undertone separates it from Bone (cooler, closer
  to off-white) and Camel (warmer, more yellow-brown).
  Side-by-side comparison or a dated catalog color card is the
  most reliable identification
- Faded or sun-yellowed Bone and Camel can drift toward an
  olive cast and read as Khaki at a distance; check the
  protected interior leather and the edge color for the original
  hue

## Era anchoring

Khaki appears in NYC-era catalog references via the Pocket Purse
colorway. Whether it persists into the transition era is not
established in current working files. It is not useful as a
precise era anchor on its own; combine with creed wording,
serial format, hardware, and country-of-origin (see
[authentication/](../authentication/)) for era placement.

## Open verification items

1. **Catalog-anchored introduction date** — when did "Khaki"
   first appear as a Coach color name? An NYC-era catalog page
   would settle this. Currently `tentative`.
2. **Khaki across more models** — only Pocket Purse is
   anchored in working files. Match additional NYC-era models
   to Khaki via catalog scans or dated examples.
3. **Khaki vs aged Bone / Camel** — visual confusion is
   plausible on faded examples. A dated color card would
   establish whether Khaki is a distinct Coach color at
   introduction or a community label retrofitted to other
   neutrals.
