# Aqua

## Lead

Aqua is a rare 1990s Coach colorway documented on the Drawstring
Backpack #9994 [yourgreatfinds-collectors-guide]. The colors/index.md
palette overview specifically calls out Aqua as one of the few colors
that "appears on specific 1990s models" — a partial era anchor, where
most Coach color names persist across decades.

## Description

Aqua reads as a light blue-green on glove-tanned cowhide. On Coach's
drum-dyed-through NYC-era leather (see
[authentication/leather.md](../authentication/leather.md)), Aqua's
cut edge should match the surface color, distinguishing original
Aqua from a surface-dyed reproduction or a refinished bag.

Aqua sits outside the standard brown / dark / neutral Coach palette
and is documented as quick-selling when it appeared
[yourgreatfinds-collectors-guide]. Aqua-colored Coach bags are
uncommon enough that listing photos should be cross-checked against
a known authentic example before purchase.

## Models documented in Aqua

The following models are documented in Aqua in the working research
files [yourgreatfinds-collectors-guide]:

- Drawstring Backpack (style 9994) — explicitly noted as available
  in "rare Aqua coloring; quick-selling style" per the dealer-compiled
  record (research/D-models.md)

The full per-model catalog of Aqua offerings is open and likely
limited to a small number of 1990s silhouettes.

## Authentication

- Aqua should pass the standard drum-dyed-through edge-color check:
  the cut edge reads the same blue-green as the surface (see
  authentication/leather.md). A bag whose edge color contrasts with
  the surface is a fake or reproduction signal — particularly
  relevant for unusual colors where reproduction surface dyes are
  more likely
- Aqua is rare enough that a listing claiming Aqua deserves extra
  scrutiny on creed, serial, and hardware before being treated as
  authentic
- Painted glossy edges on an Aqua bag claiming pre-1995 manufacture
  is a strong fake or reproduction tell (see
  research/E-authentication.md §7)

## Era anchoring

Aqua is one of the few Coach colors with a partial era anchor: it
appears on 1990s models per the working research and is not
documented on Cashin-era or earlier-NYC bags. This is consistent
with Coach's broader 1990s expansion into brighter colors (see
research/D-models.md on the Manhattan collection's "first major use
of bright colors," Spring 1993). Combine with creed wording, serial
format, and country-of-origin for confirmation.
