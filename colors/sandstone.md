# Sandstone

## Lead

**Sandstone** is a Coach Classics palette color introduced in
**Summer 1989** [coach-catalogs-1960-1989]. A warm tan/sand neutral,
distinct from the brand-default British Tan and from the lighter
Putty/Bone neutrals.

## Debut: Summer Preview 1989 catalog (primary)

Sandstone first appears in the Summer 1989 Classics palette as a
NEW color, alongside the established palette of Black, Bone, British
Tan, Burgundy, Mahogany, Navy, Putty, Red, and Tabac. The Collegiate
(Style 9815, $118) is a Summer 1989-introduced model documented in
"Sandstone shown on page two" [coach-catalogs-1960-1989]. By Fall
1989 Sandstone is integrated into the broader Classics palette
across multiple silhouettes.

## Era anchoring

Sandstone is a **post-Summer-1989** color. A Coach bag dated to 1988
or earlier in Sandstone is anomalous and should be re-examined. The
Spring 1989 Classics palette did NOT include Sandstone — it appears
specifically in Summer 1989 as a palette addition [coach-catalogs-
1960-1989].

## Authentication

- Sandstone should pass the standard drum-dyed-through edge-color
  check (cut edge = surface color).
- Confirm color in daylight before describing a bag as Sandstone —
  warm-toned neutrals can read close to British Tan or Putty under
  indoor lighting.
