# Tabac

## Lead

Tabac is a tobacco-brown variant in the Coach palette, sitting adjacent
to British Tan and Saddle in the warm-brown family
[yourgreatfinds-collectors-guide]. The name is listed in the working
research palette but has not yet been catalog-anchored to a specific
era introduction; Tabac is currently `tentative`.

## Description

Tabac reads as a darker, smokier brown than British Tan — the color
name evokes cured tobacco leaf. On Coach's drum-dyed-through
glove-tanned cowhide (see
[authentication/leather.md](../authentication/leather.md)), Tabac's
cut edges should match the surface color, and worn examples should
deepen rather than rub off to a contrasting under-tone.

Distinguish Tabac from British Tan (warmer, more reddish), Saddle
(lighter, more orange-tan), and Mahogany (deeper red-brown). Without
a Coach color card scan, the boundary between Tabac and adjacent
browns is not yet primary-sourced.

## Models documented in Tabac

The working research files list Tabac as a documented Coach color name
[yourgreatfinds-collectors-guide], but per-model attributions in Tabac
have not yet been compiled. Expand this list as catalog scans and
dated listings surface specific Tabac silhouettes.

## Authentication

- Tabac should pass the standard drum-dyed-through edge-color check:
  the cut edge reads the same tobacco-brown as the surface (see
  authentication/leather.md). A bag whose edge color contrasts with
  the surface is a fake or reproduction signal
- "Tabac" is a Coach color name; a listing description that says
  "tobacco brown" should be matched to a specific Coach color before
  being treated as Tabac
- Tabac and British Tan are easily confused in photos; in-hand
  inspection or a color-card cross-reference is preferable to
  photo-only color identification

## Era anchoring

Tabac is not currently anchored to a specific era. The color name
appears in Tier 3 dealer compilations alongside other brown variants
that span the NYC era through the transition era. Color alone never
dates a bag — combine with creed wording, serial format, hardware,
and country-of-origin (see [authentication/](../authentication/)) for
era placement.
