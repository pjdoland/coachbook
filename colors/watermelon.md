# Watermelon

## Lead

Watermelon is a rare pink-red Coach colorway documented on the
Kisslock Watermelon Tote [yourgreatfinds-collectors-guide]. The
research record notes "Watermelon colorway variant with high-quality
Talon zipper" (research/D-models.md). The Talon zipper places the
example pre-mid-1970s per the authentication research stream — Talon
was the standard early-Coach zipper supplier, and its presence is a
soft early-era signal.

## Description

Watermelon reads as a saturated pink-red on glove-tanned cowhide —
named for the fruit's flesh color. On Coach's drum-dyed-through
early leather (see
[authentication/leather.md](../authentication/leather.md)),
Watermelon's cut edge should match the surface color, distinguishing
original Watermelon from a surface-dyed reproduction.

Like Persimmon, Watermelon belongs to the unusual saturated-color
palette that distinguishes early Coach (Cashin-era and immediate
post-Cashin) from the more conservative brown / dark / neutral
palette that dominated later decades.

## Models documented in Watermelon

The following models are documented in Watermelon in the working
research files [yourgreatfinds-collectors-guide]:

- Kisslock Watermelon Tote — colorway variant with Talon zipper per
  the dealer-compiled record (research/D-models.md)

The per-model catalog of Watermelon offerings beyond this tote is
open and likely limited.

## Authentication

- Watermelon should pass the standard drum-dyed-through edge-color
  check: the cut edge reads the same pink-red as the surface (see
  authentication/leather.md). A contrasting under-color is a fake
  or reproduction signal — particularly relevant for unusual colors
  where reproduction surface dyes are more likely
- A Talon zipper on a Watermelon bag is consistent with pre-mid-1970s
  manufacture (see research/E-authentication.md). A non-Talon zipper
  on a claimed early Watermelon bag warrants extra scrutiny —
  acknowledging that Coach Repair / Factory Restoration sometimes
  swapped hardware, so a non-original zipper alone does not condemn
  authenticity (see STYLE.md §4 #5)
- Watermelon is rare enough that a listing claiming Watermelon
  deserves extra scrutiny on creed, serial, and hardware before
  being treated as authentic

## Era anchoring

Watermelon is `era: cashin-or-post-cashin` (E2). The Talon-zipper
detail places the documented example pre-mid-1970s, consistent with
either the Cashin era (1962–1974) or the immediate post-Cashin
transition. Without a catalog page or dated bag, the entry cannot
be more precise than "early." A Watermelon Coach bag from later
decades has not been documented in the working research; flag any
claimed later-era Watermelon as needing extra verification.

## Open verification items

   kiss-lock as a closure persisted post-Cashin.
