# Bordeaux

## Lead

Bordeaux is a deep wine-red Coach color that may overlap with — or be
identical to — Burgundy [yourgreatfinds-collectors-guide,
vintagecoachie]. The colors/index.md palette overview lists Bordeaux
as "sometimes catalog-listed as Bordeaux" alongside Burgundy, flagging
the redundancy as an open verification item. Both names persist in
collector references; whether Coach used both as distinct stock colors
or the names are interchangeable is not yet primary-sourced.

## Description

Bordeaux reads as a deep, slightly purple-leaning red on glove-tanned
cowhide — named for the French wine region. On Coach's drum-dyed-through
NYC-era leather (see
[authentication/leather.md](../authentication/leather.md)), Bordeaux's
cut edge matches the surface color, and worn examples should darken
rather than reveal a brown or natural under-tone.

Distinguish Bordeaux (or Burgundy) from Mahogany (red-brown rather
than wine-red) by undertone: Bordeaux leans cool and purple-red;
Mahogany leans warm and brown-red.

## Models documented in Bordeaux

Per-model attributions specifically labeled "Bordeaux" (vs Burgundy)
are not yet compiled in the working research files. See
[burgundy.md](burgundy.md) for the deeper-documented sister entry;
many bags described as "Burgundy" in dealer listings may correspond
to "Bordeaux" in original Coach catalog copy or vice versa.

## Authentication

- Bordeaux should pass the standard drum-dyed-through edge-color
  check: the cut edge reads the same wine-red as the surface (see
  authentication/leather.md). A contrasting under-color is a fake or
  reproduction signal
- A bag listed as "Bordeaux" or "Burgundy" should be treated as the
  same authentication problem until the two names are separated by
  a dated catalog page
- Reproductions sometimes match the surface color approximately while
  missing the depth and slight purple cast of original Bordeaux

## Era anchoring

Bordeaux / Burgundy persists across the NYC era through the transition
era. Color alone never dates a bag — combine with creed wording,
serial format, hardware, and country-of-origin (see
[authentication/](../authentication/)) for era placement.
