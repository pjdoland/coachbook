# Forest Green

## Lead

**Forest Green** is the canonical Coach name for the deep-green color
that has appeared in Coach palettes since **Fall 1989**
[coach-catalogs-1960-1989]. The "Hunter Green" label common in
resale listings and Tier 3 collector references is a community
misnomer — Coach catalogs have consistently used "Forest Green."

## Debut: Fall 1989 catalog (primary)

Forest Green debuts as a Coach color in the Fall 1989 catalog as part
of the broader palette refresh that accompanied the **Spectator
Collection** launch (Fall 1989, 8 styles, originally with Tabac trim)
and the **Continental Series** launch (Fall 1989, including Court Bag
9870 in Forest Green among other colors). Forest Green was offered
across both new sub-collections and the perennial Classics
[coach-catalogs-1960-1989].

Forest Green is documented in the Holiday 1991 Court Bag color list
(Black, British Tan, Burgundy, Forest Green, Mahogany, Navy, Putty,
Tabac), the Holiday 1991 Duffle Sac color list (Black, British Tan,
Forest Green, Mahogany, Navy, Red), and across Lightweight and
Classic palettes through the early 1990s.

## Why "Hunter Green" propagates

Tier 3 collector compilations (yourgreatfinds, vintagecoachie,
coachbagrehab) reference the color as "Hunter Green" — a generic
American color name that predates Coach's specific dye usage. Resale
listings inherited the label, and PurseForum threads sometimes
correct it but rarely retract earlier posts. The result: "Hunter
Green" is the dominant search term; "Forest Green" is the correct
catalog term.

When evaluating a vintage Coach listing that uses "Hunter Green,"
treat the listing as referring to the same color as Forest Green —
no actionable difference, just nomenclature.

## Authentication

- Forest Green should pass the standard drum-dyed-through edge-color
  check (cut edge = surface color). A bag whose cut edge shows a pale
  or contrasting under-color is not Coach NYC-era leather regardless
  of surface color.
- Confirm color in daylight before describing a bag as Forest Green —
  indoor light flattens it toward black.
- On two-tone bags (e.g., Forest Green / British Tan trim), both
  leathers should pass the edge-color check independently.

## Era anchoring

Forest Green is a **post-Fall-1989** color. A Coach bag dated to
1988 or earlier in Forest Green is anomalous and should be
re-examined: either the color is mislabeled (different green
formulation), the bag is later than claimed, or the color is a
post-1989 retro re-issue.
