# Red

## Lead

Red is a rare Coach colorway documented as a Willis variant
[yourgreatfinds-collectors-guide]. The research record specifically
flags a "rare red variant documented" for Willis 9927
(research/D-models.md). Among Coach colors, true Red is uncommon
enough that the dealer-compiled record calls it out as rare even
within the well-documented Willis lineup.

## Description

Red reads as a saturated, slightly cool true red on glove-tanned
cowhide — distinct from the warmer, deeper Burgundy / Bordeaux
(wine-red) and Mahogany (red-brown). On Coach's drum-dyed-through
NYC-era leather (see
[authentication/leather.md](../authentication/leather.md)), Red's
cut edge should match the surface color, distinguishing original
Red from a surface-dyed reproduction or a refinished bag.

Distinguish Red from Burgundy / Bordeaux (wine-leaning, deeper),
Mahogany (brown-leaning), and the early Watermelon (pink-leaning).
A "red" listing description should be matched to a specific Coach
color before being assumed to be true Red.

## Models documented in Red

The following models are documented in Red in the working research
files [yourgreatfinds-collectors-guide]:

- Willis (style 9927) — rare red variant documented per the
  dealer-compiled record (research/D-models.md). Willis is among
  the highest-priority verification items in research/D-models.md
  due to its data density

The full per-model catalog of Red offerings is open.

## Authentication

- Red should pass the standard drum-dyed-through edge-color check:
  the cut edge reads the same true red as the surface (see
  authentication/leather.md). A contrasting under-color is a fake
  or reproduction signal — particularly relevant for unusual colors
  where reproduction surface dyes are more likely
- A claimed Red Willis warrants extra scrutiny on creed, serial,
  and hardware before being treated as authentic, given Willis's
  high counterfeit rate as a popular vintage style
- Painted glossy edges on a Red bag claiming pre-1995 manufacture
  is a strong fake or reproduction tell (see
  research/E-authentication.md §7)
- "Red" listings in marketplace photos can shift toward Burgundy,
  Bordeaux, or Mahogany under different lighting; in-hand inspection
  or a daylight color reference is preferable to photo-only color
  identification

## Era anchoring

Red on Willis (9927) places the colorway in the 1980s NYC era
through early 1990s, consistent with Willis's documented production
window (research/D-models.md). Color alone never dates a bag —
combine with creed wording, serial format, hardware, and
country-of-origin (see [authentication/](../authentication/)) for
era placement.
